## Why

Our draft #5640 (fork `feat/relinker-frame-frameless-leaves`, head **a376e37b88**) frames leaf functions that keep values below RSP (`--frame-red-zone`).

**tibzejoker's results**, Grand Theft Auto V (PPSA04264), Windows, RTX 4090:
- 240 leaves are framed. 4 are skipped with "rsp operand that cannot move and has no room for a stub".
- One of the 4 is zlib's `inflate_fast` (0x28aa440-0x28aad38). Its only unframed operand is:
  ```
  28aac85: 49 01 f8        add %rdi,%r8
  28aac88: 48 39 3c 24     cmp %rdi,(%rsp)
  28aac8c: 0f 85 ...       jne ...
  ```
  The 4-byte `cmp` is followed by a branch, so the forward extension can't reach `kJumpBytes` (5).
- `inflate_fast` takes resumed writes on the HDD Streamer thread. Unframed, that crashed 2 of 8 runs.

**Their local experiment** takes the contiguous `Sequential` instructions *before* the operand when the forward extension stops short:
- 243 framed, 1 "no room" left, and `inflate_fast` framed;
- 4 × 7-min open-world runs, 0 crashes.

`red_zone_framer`'s negative case "the leaf whose short operand sits right before the exit" then frames, which is intended.
