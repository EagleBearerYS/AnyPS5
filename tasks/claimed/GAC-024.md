**3. My reading, for you to confirm or correct.**
- **The loop:** the address-space build (`space == nullptr`, GuestBufferMemory.cpp ~2632-2720 at 4cd4260a9a) walks every readable GPU range of the lease and calls `importAllocation(context, state, range->address, lease, 0, false)` once per range.
- **No join:** `importRun` (~722-746) joins neighbours only when they tile the aligned 8 MiB window **exactly** (`lease[first]->address == low` and the last range ends at `high`). Here a 4 MiB range fills each window's first half, possibly with different attributes, so `continuesRun` may fail at the 4 MiB→64 KiB edge. Each 64 KiB range then becomes its own import.
- **The budget:** the whole lease (~10.8 GiB imported ever) is more than the budget. So every rebuild of the address space re-walks ~27k ranges and re-tries the refused ones: budget refusals aren't remembered in `failures`.
- **What changed from e34:** e34c479210 has the same loop (~2511-2519). So the change is in the range layout (#5596-era direct memory as private pages?) or in how often `space` is rebuilt, not in the loop itself. Please pin down which.

**4. Fix options to weigh, on top of the four in the task:**
- **Join within a window:** join compatible contiguous ranges inside the window even when they don't tile it, aligned to `hostImportAlignment`. 64 ranges then make 1 import.
- **Remember refusals per build epoch:** a range refused for budget isn't re-tried on every rebuild until the budget frees.
- **Import only what the build needs:** the address-space build imports only ranges the build's shaders can reach, if a bound exists (the SRT plan, the V# ranges). If none exists, say so.
- **Leave small ranges out of imports (the Bug Finder's direction):** serve ranges below a size (64 KiB?) by copy or mirror instead of an import. A host import's fixed cost and budget share are poor value for tiny ranges, and the big ranges would then fit. Measure the per-build cost either way; this is on the per-draw path, and the Bug Finder offers to review a design or branch.
- **Size the budget from the device:** `VK_EXT_memory_budget` (`state->memoryBudget` exists in VulkanDevice.cpp) instead of a fixed MiB, so the shared-memory ceiling isn't hit.
- Keep the completeness gate: a red/green test on Linux with many small adjacent registered ranges, the cost, and the overlaps with open PRs.
