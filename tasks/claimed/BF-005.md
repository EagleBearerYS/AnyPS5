## Context

`ShaderMemory::read` (`core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/ShaderMemory.cpp`, about line 123 on upstream `main`) runs `++CaptureTotals().reads` for every word a shader capture reads. That's a sequentially consistent read-modify-write on one process-wide atomic.
- Its siblings on the same path:
  - `CaptureTotals().reads += words` in `readWords` (about line 228);
  - `++CaptureTotals().pages` (about line 101);
  - `++CaptureTotals().pagesWordwise` (about line 107).
- As far as I can see, these counters are printed only by the `[capture]` profile line, under `CaptureProfiled()` / `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW`.
- Resident Evil Village's title reads about 100k words per capture, and two or three queue workers capture at once. So a shared atomic RMW per word may be a real cost even with profiling off.
