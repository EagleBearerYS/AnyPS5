# BF-336: GTA V's 21,000 collect refusals per 10 s on `comparedWhenShared` pages: claim check, the exact proof options, and the cost (read-only design, no code)

## Why

On our open #12921 (head 23ef222060, "keep an untracked fast proof across collect epochs on Windows while its write-watch hint has not moved"), chitters2004 posted Grand Theft Auto V (PPSA04263) Story counters on Windows: https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/12921#issuecomment-6538271696.
- **The counts:** ~21,000 of ~21,500 `collect` refusals per 10 s are on plain sampled snapshots (no storage source, uncompressed), recorded with the epoch stamp.
- **Why #12921's hint doesn't help:** their blocks have write-watch hints, but `UntrackedHint` returns 0, because `walkHints` fails: `GuestArenaCollectWrites` refuses those ranges.
- **The refusals:** in one 10 s sample, 19,091 of 19,091 were by `collectable` on `comparedWhenShared` pages. In 1,469 of them, the range also holds pages not marked that way.
- **Their reading:** direct memory pages that stopped being armed when they were shared, so there's no write mark to walk.
- **Context:** their split shows the Story gain is #6192's, and #12921's hinted stamp adds nothing there.

**The question (the PM's lead):** is there an exact way to prove those shared pages unchanged without a full walk or compare? And what would it save? This task reads and designs; it builds nothing. The PC checks Village's shape separately.

2. **The exact options.** For each, say whether it's exact, what it costs, and its failure modes. A wrong "unchanged" is a correctness bug, so be strict.
   - (a) **Windows write watch on shared views:** `MEM_WRITE_WATCH` works only on private `VirtualAlloc` memory. Confirm from the code and docs whether any form of it can cover a section view.
   - (b) **Protection-based tracking for shared pages:** read-only plus a fault handler that marks a per-page dirty bit or generation, re-protected after each collect. It must catch writes through **every** alias of the page, not only the faulting view.

   - (d) **Anything else the code suggests:** for example, keeping one alias armed and writing through it, or treating pages whose every alias is read-only as unchanged.
