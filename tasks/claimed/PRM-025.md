adriadam10 requested changes on #6012 (the F10 performance overlay, branch `feat/videoout-performance-overlay`, head e09870006d) after running `agc_performance_overlay` on RADV (AMD Radeon 660M, Linux). Two findings:

1. With `VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation`, 28 errors `VUID-vkCmdBeginRenderPass-initialLayout-00898` and `-00899` at `PerformanceOverlay.cpp:745`: the attachment view had only COLOR_ATTACHMENT usage while the pass starts in TRANSFER_DST_OPTIMAL and ends in TRANSFER_SRC_OPTIMAL.
