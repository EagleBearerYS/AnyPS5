## Why

Lane P ("recording without per-draw proof", `pr-drafts/arch/lane-p-memo.md` on the PC) asks a question.

At Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556)'s menu, a recorded draw spends ~888 of ~1,990 µs in "lookup": `ShaderResources::Revalidate` proving a cached template current, at ~1,150 µs per call.
- Its fast proof (`fastRevalidate`) is a write-watch collect over the template's captured regions (fast-fail reason `collect`), plus per-image checks: stamps, keys, pending writes, eviction, replacement, cleared views, DCC keys, depth surfaces.
- Its full walk (the `fullWalk` lambda in `Revalidate`) repeats every texture, storage-image and image-table lookup.

**The question:** how much of lookup goes if a template no longer proves its images (as a stage whose T#s are read at run time from Raghav314's slot table wouldn't need to), keeping only the `collect` / buffer part?
