## Why

C-1 (fork `perf/agc-address-read-passes`, head **a939efe1a0**, two commits on upstream main 661a1118cc) lets an address-based draw that only reads through the BDA table continue the open render pass. BF-047 (`results/cloud-3/BF-047.md`) showed it working on the harness: passes begun per 100 address-based draws fell from 100 to 1.7. Before C-1 can open, its branch needs that test and the counters it reads. C-1 waits on Raghav314's answer on upstream PR 6947, which makes the same change.

- a module the scanner can't read to its end counts as storing.
