# BF-121: why queue 0x20's WAIT_REG_MEM on a label never ends in six UE4 titles: model, red test, fix proposal
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: normal

## Why

Six UE4 titles log `[gpu] queue 0x20 WAIT_REG_MEM at <label> timed out after 1000ms (function 3 ref 0x1 mask 0xffffffff value <v>)`. `<v>`, the awaited word's live value, is the address of another label slot in the same area, or 0. The titles and reports:
- **Kena: Bridge of Spirits (PPSA01746):** hangs. #6558 (xXPBSXx), with `earlier write by queue 0x0 opcode 0x50 / 0x49` at label+0x4.
- **Coral Island (PPSA17226)** and **Stray (PPSA02100):** #6558.
- **Sifu (PPSA03001):** ends with its own `GameThread timed out waiting for RenderThread after 120.00 secs` (#6558, issuecomment-6265221718, main 4ffb0cd12).
- **Lies of P:** #5983.
- **Infliction: Extended Cut (PPSA02688):** the level load never finishes (discussion #6996, JJenkx).
- **Hi-Fi RUSH (PPSA17191):** queue 0x20 waits ~85 ms of every frame. With `APS5_GPU_WAIT_TIMEOUT_MS=50` it shows a stale pointer-like value with `table miss` (#9117, orezraey, main 2f161d4f33).

#7865 (merged) only documents the wait.

**Code on main:**
- `WaitMemory.cpp` ~339 calls `LabelHistory::storedSince` (`LabelHistory.cpp` ~21). A store counts only when it's newer than the stamp at which the wait packet was *received* by queue 0x20's worker.
- The timeout line is `WaitMemory.cpp` ~386.

**The reading to test (unchecked):** the title recycles a label slot after the 1 is stored.
1. Queue 0 stores 1 to the label (the 0x50/0x49 writes).
2. The CPU, or another packet, then writes the slot's free-list link: the address of another slot.
3. Queue 0x20's worker receives its wait only after that, because it lags behind the guest.

At that point neither the live value nor the history (stores after receipt) satisfies the wait. On hardware the wait would have been reached before the recycle.
