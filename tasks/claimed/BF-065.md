# BF-065: stop VideoOut's present loop before the AGC recorder and device are torn down (a crash at close)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: normal

## Why

Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) crashes at close on the PC: 2 of 2 closes with `APS5_NO_GPL=1`, against 0 of 9 earlier closes, mostly with GPL.
- **The fault:** `FATAL: unhandled exception 0xc0000005 on thread 'presenter'`, at `nvoglv64.dll+0xe6b3f6`, reading a freed address. It happens 15–47 s after a normal or guard close starts.
- **The stack** is the same both times. The addr2line on the PC's `-g1` libs gives:
  - `libSceAgcDriver.prx+0x1c9f8b` = `Recorder::endOpenRenderPass(bool)`, the return after `EndRenderPass(context, open->commands)` (`Recorder.cpp` ~1412–1415 on the PC's tree 7b093d5fbc). That's `vkCmdEndRenderPass` into the NVIDIA driver.
  - `libSceVideoOut.prx+0x3c5b7` = `VideoOutDriver::presentLoop`'s `TimedWait::WaitUntil`.
- **The reading:** the presenter thread, flushing the open batch for a present, ends a render pass in a command buffer that shutdown is freeing. That's a teardown race.
- **What the two crashes share:** both runs used `APS5_NO_GPL=1` on tree 7b093d5fbc. The CPU indirect draw cache (BF-052) was on for the first and off for the second, so it's not involved.
- **Why `NO_GPL` might widen it:** with monolithic pipelines, teardown takes longer. That's a guess; confirm or refute it from the code.
- **Cost:** a crash at exit. The periodic pipeline-cache saver (`PipelineCache::run`, every 10 s) means at most ~10 s of new pipelines are lost.

**No game data:** use the code and synthetic harnesses only.
