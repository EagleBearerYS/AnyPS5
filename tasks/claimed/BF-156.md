# BF-156: raise the recompiler's sampler (32) and sampled-pair (64) limits, widening the 32-bit sampler masks (Rift Apart after #9749)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart (PPSA01474), with our #9749 (b2d4afc64d), stops about 310-425 s in with `ShaderRecompiler::Recompile: sampler resource limit exceeded`. Tamalero's counts, with the caps lifted in a tracing copy (https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9749#issuecomment-6350154374):

| | 0x116bb67b00 | 0x116bb5e100 | 0x116bb5ad00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| distinct sampler sources | 63 | 62 | 63 |
| images (limit 64) | 51 | 50 | 51 |
| sampled image/sampler pairs (limit 64) | 112 | 101 | 112 |

**Where the sampler sources come from:**
- 60 are indirect image-table columns of one unkeyed per-lane table. They differ in chase depth, and in the S#'s offset in the pointed-to record (36 offsets).
- 3 are direct SRT S#s.

**What can't help:**
- Sharing evaluable heap S#s (BF-153) has nothing to share here.
- Merging the same-offset columns across depths would still leave more than 32 samplers.

So these shaders need the limits themselves raised. With only the sampler cap lifted, they stop next on `sampled image/sampler pair limit exceeded`.

## What depends on the limits (`upstream/main` d6e7ab42df)

- **`ShaderInfo`** (`core/shader/recompiler/IntermediateRepresentation/include/IntermediateRepresentation/IrMetadata/ShaderInfo.hpp:15-17`): `MaxImages = 64`, `MaxSamplers = 32`, `MaxSampledPairs = 64`.
- **The refusals:** `ResourceTracker.cpp` refuses in `AddImage` (`:1835`), `AddSampler` (`:1899`) and `AddSampledPair` (`:1913`); `PlannedWithinLimits` is at `:1458`.
- **The 32-bit sampler masks.** These are why `MaxSamplers` is 32:
  - `DescriptorBindingBuilder.cpp:513-534`: `samplerMasks` are `std::uint32_t` with `1u << element`, and `:519` refuses with "prepared sampler mask capacity exceeded";
  - `binding.descriptor.imageSamplers` (`Recompiler.hpp:487`, `std::vector<std::uint32_t>`) and `tableSamplerMasks` (`DescriptorBindingBuilder.hpp:19`);
  - the driver: `Graphics/src/Sampler.cpp` (`RequireFilterMinmax`, `ForcesDegammaOn`, `RequireBorderSwizzle`, `RequireNonSeamlessCube`, which take `std::uint32_t samplerMask` and loop `element < 32u`);
  - `Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp`: `:4681`'s `sampler < 32u` loop, and every other user of `imageSamplers` and `tableSamplerMasks`. Find them all with `git grep -n 'imageSamplers\|tableSamplerMasks\|samplerMask'`.
- **Pairs:** `MaxSampledPairs` is used only by `AddSampledPair`, `PlannedWithinLimits` and a test (`tests/ShaderMemory.cpp:2737`). Check whether anything else sizes an array by it.
- **The driver already refuses past the device's limits** (`ShaderResources.cpp:4437` against `maxPerStageDescriptorSamplers`, `:4462` against `maxDescriptorSetSamplers`), so a device with few samplers, such as MoltenVK's 16, still refuses cleanly at the driver.

## The change

- **Replace the 32-bit sampler masks** with a representation that isn't capped at 32. Either works:
  - `std::uint64_t` masks with `MaxSamplers = 64`. Rift Apart's worst is 63, so there's no headroom.
  - Better: a representation that scales, such as a small sorted list of sampler elements per image element, or a dynamic bitset, with `MaxSamplers` raised to 128.
- **Choose and justify.** Weigh the per-draw cost of the driver's loops over the masks: they run per bound image.
- **Raise `MaxSampledPairs`** so 112 pairs fit with headroom (for example 256), and `MaxImages` only if something forces it (it isn't needed here: 51).
- **Keep every refusal text** for shaders past the new limits.
- **`FormatVersion`** (`ShaderDiskCache.hpp:16`, 44 on main): bump it, because serialized binding descriptors change. Our open PRs already hold 45-48 (#7223 45, #7395 46, #9633 47, #9749 48), so use **49** and say so; the PR Manager re-numbers at merge time if needed.
- **Every consumer of the masks moves together**, in the recompiler and the driver. No silent truncation to 32 anywhere: grep for `32u` and `1u <<` near sampler code and say what you checked.
- **Docs:** if `ARCHITECTURE.md` or a debt file states the 32/64 limits, update it.
