## Why

BF-055 (`results/cloud-4/BF-055.md`, §5) found that BF-046's "GPU (rasterizer CPU) per draw" column wasn't the GPU's work per draw.
- **What it measured:** the harness never waits for the GPU, so lavapipe's two rasterizer threads were saturated on a growing backlog for the whole window. The column was about 2 × the wall time per draw: arm A 686.5 against 2 × 347.2, arm B 341.9 against 2 × 174.5. "Arm B costs half of arm A on the GPU" only mirrored arm B's faster CPU.
- **With the GPU waited for each frame** (`VILLAGE_GPU_SYNC=1`), arm A's shape costs about 23,300 µs of rasterizer CPU per draw, a median 10,073 ms of GPU per frame.
- **What depends on it:** the redesign's K4 has a GPU half, "the GPU cost per draw rises more than 30% over CPU-resolved binding". It was called a pass from that column, so it needs measuring properly. BF-046 §5's per-sample table-lookup cost was measured the same way.

**No game data:** the harness and its synthetic shaders only.
