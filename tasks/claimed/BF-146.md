## Why

JJenkx found the gap on #9533, and it is on `main` too: for a pipelined queue-0 DMA_DATA fill over 64 KiB, `enqueueDmaPacket` queues `storeBufferFill(..., false, false)` at `PacketMemory.cpp:307` (upstream `main` 4777caca34). The last argument is `noteMetadata`, so nothing notes the fill.

Two earlier tasks couldn't show a red:
- **BF-142** (results/cloud-2/BF-142.md): `NativeMultisampleDepth.cpp`'s targets live on the test's own Vulkan device, while the queued fill and the recorder check run on the driver's private device.
- **BF-143** (results/cloud-2/BF-143.md): a single-sample surface recovers the clear without the note, because `storeBackClearedHtile` re-reads the HTILE on bind. Only a native multisample owner (`RoutesToNativeDepthOwner`) skips that and relies on the note.

So the only red is a native multisample depth target created, filled and sampled entirely through `sceAgcDriverSubmitDcb`, so that the driver's device owns it. That is BF-142's option 1.
