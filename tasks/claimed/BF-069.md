# BF-069: a Windows heap report and heap trim behind switches, for Resident Evil Village's memory budget (lane B, milestone 2)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) holds ~26 GB private at its menu on the PC. Our budget is ≤ ~16 GB, then ≤ ~12 GB, and private bytes on Windows are commit charge.
- **The process heap** (`GetProcessHeap()`, which UCRT's `malloc`/`new` use) is 6.3 GB in ~400 segments at the menu.
- **~1.8 GB of the heap's pages hold only zeros.** BF-064 (`results/cloud-3/BF-064.md` §2–3) reasons they are most likely committed but never written, or committed free space, rather than zeroed live objects.
- **Telling which** needs a walk of the heap from inside the process: `HeapWalk` works only on the calling process's heaps.
- **If much of it is free space,** a periodic `HeapSetInformation(GetProcessHeap(), HeapOptimizeResources, …)` decommits it.

This task builds both tools behind switches, so the PC can measure them in one run. If it's ready by ~13:00 PT, it can ride this afternoon's PC memory session.

## Steps

1. **Base:** current upstream main. Branch: fork `exp/heap-trim`. Windows-only code (`#ifdef _WIN32`); on Linux the switches do nothing.
2. **`APS5_HEAP_REPORT=<seconds>`.** Every N seconds, print one line:
   - **Locking:** use `HeapLock`/`HeapWalk`/`HeapUnlock` on `GetProcessHeap()`. Keep the lock short. If a full walk would hold it too long on a 6 GB heap, say so and walk in slices.
   - **What the line holds:**
     - busy bytes and blocks;
     - free bytes and blocks;
     - uncommitted ranges' bytes (`PROCESS_HEAP_UNCOMMITTED_RANGE`);
     - region count;
     - zero pages among the committed free blocks' whole 4 KiB pages. Sample them if a full scan is too slow, and say how.
     - zero pages among busy blocks' whole pages, sampled the same way.
     - the process's `PrivateUsage`.
   - **Format,** for example: `[heap] busy 4.12 GiB in 9,812,334 blocks (zero pages ~0.31 GiB), free 1.71 GiB in 14,022 blocks (zero pages ~1.40 GiB), uncommitted 0.80 GiB, 402 regions; private 25.9 GiB; walk 210 ms`.
3. **`APS5_HEAP_TRIM=<seconds>`.**
   - **What it does:** every N seconds, call `HeapSetInformation(GetProcessHeap(), HeapOptimizeResources, &info, sizeof(info))` with `HEAP_OPTIMIZE_RESOURCES_INFORMATION{HEAP_OPTIMIZE_RESOURCES_CURRENT_VERSION, 0}`.
   - **What it prints:** one line per call, with `PrivateUsage` before and after and the call's duration.
   - **Optionally,** also call `_heapmin()` and say whether it changes anything beyond the first call.
4. **Where it runs:** one small thread, started where the driver or libc already starts helper threads, joined at shutdown in the same place as the others. Not on a hot path.
5. **Tests,** Windows-only, run on GitHub's Windows runner by the Bug Finder:
   - **Trim:** allocate ~512 MiB in many 64 KiB–1 MiB blocks, free most of them leaving one live block per segment-sized stride, call the trim function directly, and require `PrivateUsage` to fall by at least half of the freed amount.
   - **Report:** after the same pattern, require the report's free bytes to be at least the freed amount within 10%, and its zero-page count to be sane.
   - Give the ctest names. On Linux the test is a skip (77).
6. **Checks:**
   - `ctest -R '^(agc|libc)'` on Linux;
   - CI's Linux job mirrored on the head;
   - the ctest names for the Windows run.
