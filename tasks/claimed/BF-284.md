# BF-284: our next Village speed PR candidate: resolve a draw's resources outside the GPU mutex on the draw committer (BF-282 candidate 3)

## Why

BF-282 (`results/cloud-2/BF-282.md`) traced Resident Evil Village's menu frame. It's one serial chain on queue worker 0x0: about 865-917 ms of its own work and about 670 ms blocked per frame.
- **The largest block:** waiting for the GPU mutex, which the draw committer holds for every commit (`Execution/src/Driver/Draw/Draw.cpp:1283`, `commitDraw`).
- **What the committer does under it,** per frame:
  - `Resources.Complete` (`ShaderResources::buildComplete`, `ShaderResources.cpp:2406`), 219-255 ms;
  - `Graphics.Draw.draw_and_resource_release`, 118-169 ms;
  - `Recorder.Sync`, 69-79 ms.
- **The upper bound** of moving that resolve work outside the lock is about 420-470 ms/frame. No open PR does it.

BF-036 built a prototype of the idea: fork `exp/mt-record-ceiling` **4c2e622f28**, behind `APS5_PRERESOLVE=1`, "variant (b)". Lookups that mutate state refuse and retry under the lock. It's not a PR.

The two other levers are other people's open PRs, and are measured separately on the PC:
- #1896, `APS5_PIPELINE_INDIRECT`;
- #9858/#11847, the completion-label wait.

Your design must compose with both.

## Do

1. **Read** BF-036's prototype (`git log`/`git diff` of 4c2e622f28 against its base) and its result file if it exists (`results/cloud-*/BF-036.md`). Then read the current main code: `commitDraw` and the committer loop in `DrawPipeline.cpp`, `ShaderResources::buildComplete`, `Draw.cpp`'s `lockForDraw`, and what under the GPU mutex really needs it (the recorder, the resident caches, the import set, the label table).
2. **Design the smallest correct form:**
   - **Outside the GPU mutex:** the parts of a draw's resolve that only read immutable or snapshot state: descriptor words, the cache lookups that don't mutate, the T# decode, the image-table proof.
   - **Under the GPU mutex:** whatever mutates or needs ordering with the recorder: uploads, imports, recording, `UploadFinish`, write-back setup.
   - **The re-check under the lock:** cheap and complete (generation, epoch, or a stamp). If it fails, the work is redone under the lock as today. No result computed outside the lock may be used without that re-check.
   - **The switch:** behind `APS5_PRERESOLVE=1` (keep BF-036's name if it fits), off by default, so the first PR can open as a measured opt-in like `APS5_TABLE_INDEX`.
3. **Build it** on upstream main (current), as fork `perf/agc-preresolve-outside-gpu-mutex`, in reviewable commits: any preparatory refactor first, then the feature with its tests.
4. **Tests:**
   - **Correctness:**
     - a re-check that fails (memory changed between the pre-resolve and the lock) redoes the work, and the draw sees the new data;
     - CPU, driver and GPU writes racing the pre-resolve;
     - a cache eviction in between.
     Use the existing Recorder/ShaderResources fixtures. No placeholder-throw assertions.
   - **Off by default** equals main; the whole `^agc` suite passes with it off and on.
   - **The Village harness** (`agc_driver_village_draw_cost`, as BF-265 ran it): per-draw lookup and wall time, off against on, at 4,096 entries, 0 % and 2 % writes, median of 3. Run it also with `APS5_PIPELINED_DRAWS` at its default and at a larger depth.
5. **Overlaps:** merge-tree against #1896, #9858, #11847, #12745 (cda1b61077), #12921 (fdc70046f8) and #12478 (58171b22e8), and against the open PRs on `Draw.cpp`, `DrawPipeline.cpp` and `ShaderResources.cpp`. Say how the design composes with #1896's queued indirect draws.
