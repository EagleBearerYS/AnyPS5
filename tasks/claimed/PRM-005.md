FSR 1 presentation upscaling (PM-004/PM-006, the fork branch `feat/agc-fsr1-presentation-upscaling`, head fea68147a) needs images for its PR description. Game frames are off-limits, so the images come from the FSR test's synthetic pattern on lavapipe. Nothing is committed or pushed except your result file.

Remotes in your environment: `origin`/`fork` is EagleBearerYS/AnyPS5 and `upstream` is boykopovar/AnyPS5.

1. Check out the branch: `git fetch origin feat/agc-fsr1-presentation-upscaling`, then `git checkout --detach FETCH_HEAD`. Confirm the head is fea68147a, and run `git submodule update --init --recursive`.
2. Make a **local, uncommitted** change to `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/PresentationPass.cpp` (its `--fsr` cases), or a small separate local program linked the same way. Upscale the test's synthetic 960x540 pattern (the edges, the thin-line grid and the gradient) to 1920x1080 three ways, through the production code, and read each result back:
   - **bilinear:** today's scaler path, as `ANYPS5_UPSCALE` unset presents it;
   - **EASU + RCAS** at the default sharpness 0.2 (`ANYPS5_UPSCALE=fsr`);
   - **EASU + RCAS** at sharpness 2.0, the weakest setting.
3. Make the images:
   - **A:** a side-by-side crop of the same 320x180 region (in output pixels) from bilinear and from EASU at 0.2. Pick the region with the thin-line grid and an edge. Show it at 2x with nearest-neighbour enlargement, so pixels are visible: two 640x360 panels next to each other with a 4-pixel black gap, bilinear on the left.
   - **B:** the same as A, but EASU at 0.2 on the left against EASU at 2.0 on the right, to show what the sharpness setting does.
   - **C:** the whole 1920x1080 EASU result, downscaled 2x with a box filter to 960x540, for context.
4. Encode each as PNG with Python's standard library (zlib, struct; no Pillow), then base64-encode it.

Report in `results/cloud-<n>/PRM-005.md` on `cloud-results`, text only:
- the exact head and how you rendered: the local change as a diff, the crop region, the scaling;
- for each image: its size in pixels, its PNG byte size, its sha256, and the base64 between lines `-----BEGIN PNG A-----` and `-----END PNG A-----` (B and C the same);
- one line per image saying what it shows;
- a short RESULT: block.
Keep each PNG under ~400 KB. If C is larger, crop it instead and say so.
