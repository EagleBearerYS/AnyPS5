# BF-164: #5640 brought up to main, then framing extended to frame-allocating leaves like GTA V's inflate_fast (stage 1, in place)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

Grand Theft Auto V (PPSA04264) crashes in zlib's `inflate_fast` when Windows resumes a write fault while the function keeps values below `rsp` (#361, #9136). Windows' exception dispatch writes its frames below `rsp`, over the guest's 128-byte red zone.

**#5640 is ours** (closed 2026-10-14; fork branch `feat/relinker-frame-frameless-leaves`, head cef664f770). It frames red-zone leaves without a frame pointer: it lowers `rsp` by 0x80 in a prologue stub, raises it in exit stubs, and moves every rsp operand by 0x80.
- It sits on #5052's commit fab7d203fb by Josip Parlov. Keep that commit unchanged.
- The user has decided to reopen and extend it, jointly with tibzejoker.

**tibzejoker's GTA V relink with it** ([#9136 comment](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/9136#issuecomment-6356343541)): 917 red-zone leaves, 100 framed. The skips:

| skips | reason |
|---|---|
| 540 | "rsp operand that cannot move with rsp" |
| 151 | "other use of rsp" |
| 99 | "exit shorter than a jump" |
| 20 | "branch leaves the function" |
| 5 | "indirect jump" |
| 2 | "overlaps another stub" |

**`inflate_fast`** (eboot 0x28aa440) isn't framed. It does `push rbp; push r15; push r14; push r13; push r12; push rbx; sub $0x40,%rsp`, then spills below its own allocation (`mov %rdi,-0x20(%rsp)`). Two things stop it:
- the `sub` counts as "other use of rsp" (`use.RegisterRsp` in `core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/RedZoneFramer.cpp`);
- `movedDisplacement` refuses every positive disp8 operand, because +0x80 overflows it.
