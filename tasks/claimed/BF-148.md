## Why

With #9457, Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (PPSA03016) gets past `ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x8560`. It then stops at 131 s in compute 0x11e2b38300 (hugodearaujo, https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9457#issuecomment-6340285887):

```
GetImageResource dword 0 is not a valid runtime value; chain: ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x00008fa0(GetBufferResource(LoadAddressU32@pc=0x00008f54(GetAddressResource(Phi(Phi(ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x00001fc4(...
```

hugodearaujo's IR (https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/8939#issuecomment-6340144242), condensed:
- A record table V# comes from the SRT. Record dwords 2/3 are read at a waterfall key (`ReadFirstLane << 4`): `%7757`, `%7758`.
- The pointer's low word is a Phi of `%7757` and the constant 0xea (two Phi levels). Its high word is `%7758` directly.
- At pc 0x8f54: `GetAddressResource(low, %7758)`, then 4 x `LoadAddressU32` at offset 0x0. That's a V#.
- That V# feeds **two** `GetBufferResource` handles:
  - `%10906`: 8 `ReadConstBuffer` at 0x0, the T# read at pc 0x8fa0, into `GetImageResource` and then `ImageSampleRaw`;
  - `%10987`: 8 `ReadConstBuffer` at +0x4c0, the read at pc 0x9000, into a second `GetImageResource` and `ImageSampleRaw`.
- The second T#'s dwords 0 and 1 (`%10988`, `%10989`) also flow into Phis that later join the record pointer pair. That's register reuse. 15 `GetAddressResource` loads follow after pc 0x8428, and hugodearaujo can't tell whether any of them runs with that pair.

**Where #9457 refuses** (ResourceTracker.cpp at #9457's head 2a5db7ae45, the nested-table branch of `TryMakeTableColumn`, around lines 720-744):
- **:735-739:** every V# word read (`parent.reads`) may feed only `GetBufferResource` handles whose uses all belong to *this* T#'s `plan.reads`. The second handle `%10987` belongs to the other T#, so the T# at 0x8fa0 refuses. That's the debt's "shared V# consumers".
- **:731:** every T# read's uses must be the T#'s own handle (`sameHandle`). The second T#'s dwords 0/1 also feed the Phis, so after the first fix, the T# at 0x9000 refuses there. That's the debt's "T# or S# words read by anything but their descriptor handle".

This is not #9749's split-words shape (no chased pointer read), so it's a separate follow-up.
