## What the branch does

Three commits on upstream main 5d9dcc42d2 that stop and join the AGC driver's thread pools at shutdown:
- 0365a367f9: shutdown closes only the shader preparation pools that exist;
- afb1d0aad8: the preparation workers are joined when their pool closes;
- 0ba4f056cd: the parallel compare helpers and the pipeline library's job thread are stopped and joined from `Driver::stop`.

New tests: `agc_driver_shutdown_threads`, `_joined` and `_compare_helpers`. `agc_driver_pipeline_optimized_readback_*` now also checks the library worker.
