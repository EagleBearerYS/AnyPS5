# BF-109: stop paying a `VirtualQuery` per write-watch collect on Windows: it costs ~380 µs on a large committed region
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

BF-106 (cloud-4, `results/cloud-4/BF-106.md`, fork `exp/rewatch-phases` 2d90e39b0) split #8926's ~440 µs per restored span on GitHub's Windows runner ([run 39588773071](https://github.com/EagleBearerYS/AnyPS5/actions/runs/39588773071), artifact `verify-windows`, `rewatch-cost.txt`). Nearly all of it is one call.

| bench commit layout | `Rewatch` per span | `VirtualQuery` per span | `GetWriteWatch` per span | retiring refreshes, total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the 449 MiB block in one placeholder replacement | 383.9 µs | **379.2 µs** (1 call; its region runs 235 MiB on average from the queried address) | 2.2 µs | 2,221 ms |
| the same block in 16 KiB placeholder replacements | 28.2 µs | 9.5 µs (25 calls) | 14.4 µs (25 calls) | 171 ms |

- In the one-block case the per-span cost climbs through the run: the first 500 spans have a median of 136 µs, the last 500 a median of 373 µs. That's consistent with Windows walking the rest of the region to compute `RegionSize`.
- **Where the call is:** `WindowsMappings::Collect` (`core/libs/prx/libc/include/WindowsMappings.hpp`). In its walk, the branch for memory that is no view calls `query(cursor)`, that is `VirtualQuery`, then checks `MEM_COMMIT`/`MEM_PRIVATE`, bounds `stop` by `BaseAddress + RegionSize`, and calls `GetWriteWatch(cursor, stop - cursor)`.
- **This isn't only #8926's cost.** Every `GuestArenaCollectWrites` over private guest memory that isn't a view pays it.
  - Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556)'s `[guestmem]` line shows ~960K collects taking 12.7 s over a run. Many are memo hits, so the `VirtualQuery` share in the title isn't known yet.
  - For #8926 it is ~2.2 of its 2.8 s over a Village-sized load.

## What to do

Branch `perf/libc-collect-without-region-query` on upstream `main`.

1. **The fix.** Keep `VirtualQuery` out of the collect walk's common case, by one of these:
   - **(a) Try first, fall back.** When `collectable()` has proved `[address, end)` (its `collectableRanges` memo), first try `GetWriteWatch` over `[cursor, end)` directly. If it fails because the range spans more than one allocation, fall back to today's query-bounded call.
     - Check what `GetWriteWatch` does with a range that crosses allocations or placeholders. It should fail without resetting anything, but confirm it on Windows through the PC's GitHub run, and keep the fallback either way.
   - **(b) Cache the extents.** Keep the extent of each private allocation the walk has queried: allocation base, region start and end. Drop the cache on any commit, decommit, protect or placeholder split in `WindowsMappings` or `GuestArena`, through a generation counter the commit paths bump.
   - Choose by correctness first. Say why, and what invalidates what.
   - Keep the `MEM_COMMIT` and `MEM_PRIVATE` checks' meaning: a range that isn't committed private must still return `false`.
2. **Correctness tests:**
   - the libc write-watch tests (`GuestArena`, `WindowsMappings`, `SharedWrites`, `DccKeysFirstWrite` and others that collect over private memory);
   - the AGC heap-mirror and watch tests (`agc_heap_mirror_*unwatch*`, `agc_driver_graphics`);
   - a new case that collects over a range crossing two private allocations, and over a range whose allocation was just decommitted and recommitted. It must return the same pages and the same result as before.
   - Red for the new case: the fix hunk reverted.
3. **The cost evidence comes from the bench.** Stack the fix onto fork `exp/rewatch-phases` 2d90e39b0 as a local branch `exp/rewatch-phases-fixed` and push it. The PC runs it on GitHub's Windows runner for both commit layouts, against 2d90e39b0's numbers above.
   - Give your expectation: the one-block case should fall from ~380 µs to a few µs per span.
4. **Run on Linux:** the libc and AGC tests above, and `^agc` once. The change is Windows-only code, so say what Linux exercises.
5. **MinGW:** `-fsyntax-only -Wall -Wextra` on the changed files.
6. **`git merge-tree`** against every open PR that touches `WindowsMappings.hpp` or `GuestArena.cpp`.
