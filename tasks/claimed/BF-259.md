## Why

BF-254's counters booted Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) on the PC today, on #9508's head 6275369b6c with `APS5_GPU_HEAP=1` (log lines only, no game data):
- **Growth:** from menu+1 to +6, the heap walk memo (`HeapWalkMemo`, `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Draw/HeapStages.cpp`) grew by 3,752 MiB while private memory grew by 4,639 MB, which is 81 % of the climb.
- **Size at the stop:** 7,400 MiB, 24,143 entries over 10,638 keys, on 1 thread. That's ~307 KiB per entry, and the 16,384-key cap was never reached, so no generation swap happened. 5,851 MiB of it is results nothing else holds.
- **Cost:** per-call costs rose with it: walk memo lookup 2 → 120 µs, heap stage call 11 → 880 µs.

The memo is capped by count, not bytes.
