# PM-096: Render-target scaling prototype, step 1 (opt-in, not for a PR yet)
from: AnyPS5 Project Manager
for: any
priority: high

## Why

The user wants to try true internal resolution scaling: render a 4K title's big screen-sized targets at 1080p (or another scale) inside the emulator. This is a **time-boxed prototype** to learn what works and what breaks before anyone commits to a PR. Read PM-094's result first (results/cloud-5/PM-094.md on `cloud-results`, sections 4 and 5(d)). It lists what other emulators had to handle (RPCS3, Xenia, yuzu, Ryujinx) and our hook points.

## Scope of step 1

- **Branch:** fork `exp/render-scale`, based on current upstream `main` (the remote is `upstream`; push to the fork).
- **Switch:** opt-in `APS5_RENDER_SCALE` (accept `0.5`, `0.75` or `N/D`). With it unset, everything is **bit-identical** to `main`; prove that with a test.
- **Which surfaces scale:** color render targets and depth surfaces whose extent equals the extent of the title's registered main video-out buffer, or larger, and nothing smaller. They're created at the scaled extent. Pick one generic rule, write it down, and don't special-case any title, value or path.
- **Draws into them:** scale viewport and scissor at bind (`State.cpp`, near where `viewport`/`scissor` are built from registers and `renderExtent`).
- **Sampling them** through normalized coordinates works unchanged.
- **Demotion instead of rescaling, for v0:** any use that needs native-size bytes or integer coordinates demotes that surface back to native size, recreated at full size and logged once with the reason. That covers:
  - CPU readback or a guest-memory write-back;
  - storage-image access;
  - `texelFetch` or image load/store with integer coordinates;
  - size queries;
  - copies or resolves to an unscaled surface;
  - compute writes.
  Find where each is visible in the AGC driver and recompiler, and count how often each reason fires.
- **Presentation:** a scaled display buffer is presented as today, scaled to the window. FSR comes later.

## Deliver

1. **A design note:** the rule, the hooks (files and functions), the demotion reasons and their detection points, and what step 2 would add (rescaling integer coordinates in the recompiler instead of demoting, compute grids, and so on).
2. **The code** for the smallest end-to-end slice you can get working on lavapipe.
3. **Synthetic tests in `tests/execution`:**
   - draw a 3840x2160 target at scale 0.5 and compare it with a native 1920x1080 render of the same stream;
   - check one demotion case per reason you implement;
   - check the unset switch is bit-identical.
4. **A `VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation` run:** `main` against the branch, with no new `VUID-`.
5. **Size so far,** and the files touched.
