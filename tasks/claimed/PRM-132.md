## Background

This follows PRM-130 (cloud-3's result on `cloud-results`, `results/cloud-3/PRM-130.md`, commit 5959745ad). Read it first.

On Intel UHD (Gfx12, Windows), `agc_driver_pointer_vsharp_child_key_arm_table` fails at pc 0xfc in three forms:
- a poison entry whose word is the selected descriptor's address low word + 0xc0 (palette base + 0x100);
- `selected the descriptor at 0xffffb8a2`;
- a wrong texel.

PRM-130's backward trace shows only `table_map` loads and constants reach the poison word, and the value appears nowhere in the map or guest memory. mrfelek is about to run `prm/9969-intel-fix` (99dabd0848) on Intel. If the poison form survives that, the next single variant we offer him is the build below.
