## Why

BF-052 lets CPU-path indirect draws keep their compiled stages in the draw cache, behind `APS5_CACHE_CPU_INDIRECT=1` (off by default).
- On a Village-shaped harness it reuses stages 100% after warm-up, against 0% on main, and makes draw packets 28–34% cheaper.
- Its Resident Evil Village test runs on the PC now (~04:00–06:10). Its kill line: reuse under ~30%, or private memory up more than 1 GB. Its bar: +10% menu fps.
- If it passes, it becomes a PR soon after.

This review runs in parallel so the PR isn't held for it. If the board shows this task withdrawn, or `results/cloud-*/BF-052*` says the Village gate failed, stop and report what you have.
