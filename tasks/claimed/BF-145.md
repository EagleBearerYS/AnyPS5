## Why

With #9633 (fork `fix/shader-pointer-chase-closure`, 6a72841083), Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart (PPSA01474) gets past its 0x10b84 stop. It then stops at compute `LoadAddressU32@pc=0x13bc0` with `GetImageResource dword 0 is not a valid runtime value` (Tamalero, https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9633#issuecomment-6338006613).

Tamalero traced it with the IR (issuecomment-6338256607):

| Step | V# load | V# at | Pointer words read |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | | | record read at 0x1aa8 |
| H1 | 0xfd88 | P+0x60 | 0xfd94: 0x0 / 0x4 |

| H3 | 0x12de8 | P+0x110 | 0x12e9c: low at 0x54, high at 0x6c |

- **The H3 read:** one `s_buffer_load_dwordx16` from offset 0x40. The next pointer is rebuilt from dword 5 (low) and dword 11 (high).
- **The refusal:** `ChaseHighRead` (ResourceTracker.cpp:937-942 at 6a72841083) requires `highImmediate == readImmediate + 4`, so `walk()` returns false there.
- **What already passes:** the V# offset 0x110 is already per site. The unpaired Phis and the lane-mask arms walk fine. With only the adjacency check relaxed, Tamalero's scratch build finds three sites, no loop, depth 3.

## Commit 1: the high word at its own offset

1. **The field.** `PointerChase` (`IrMetadata/ControlFlowInfo.hpp`) gains `readHighImmediate`, which the defaulted `operator==` covers.
2. **The tracker.** In `ResourceTracker.cpp`, `ChaseHighRead` takes the high read when it has:
   - the same handle as the low read (or an `EquivalentValue` one);
   - the same `readOffset`;
   - its own immediate, which is 4-aligned and differs from `readImmediate`.

   `ChaseHighRead` returns that immediate, and the walk stores it on the chase.
3. **The materializer.** `ResourceMaterializer.cpp` `Chase()` (:965-966) reads the high word at `chase.readHighImmediate` instead of `readImmediate + 4u`. Its bounds and `AddressAccessible` checks stay as they are for both words.
4. **The disk cache.** `ShaderDiskCache.cpp` encodes and decodes the new field in the `pointerChases` list, and `FormatVersion` goes 47 → 48 (`ShaderDiskCache.hpp`). The format ladder settles at merge, as for #9633. Update any static_asserts that count `PointerChase` fields.
5. **Debt.** In `docs/dev/debt/shader.md`, change #9633's line from "A chase arm is two words a scalar buffer load reads at a fixed offset" to say the two words are read at their own fixed offsets of one load through one V#.

## Commit 2: one site per chase, not per V#

In `PointerRecordPairs`, the `site` lambda finds an existing site by handle alone. So a second chase arm read through the same V# at other words keeps the first arm's `PointerChase`, and the snapshot never follows the second. Key the sites by handle **and** the full `PointerChase`.

- **The test:** a node whose V# holds two links at different words (say 0x0/0x4 and 0x8/0xc), each taken in different groups.
- **On #9633's head:** the second link's T# faults as outside the snapshot, or reads the wrong image.
- **With the change:** both resolve.
