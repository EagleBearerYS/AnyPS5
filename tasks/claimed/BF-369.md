## Why

BF-368 (fork `fix/agc-native-depth-reuse-publication` @ **6d1dd3174e**, three commits on upstream acf7ac208a) marks eight sites `DepthReadCause::Reuse`. A reuse publication takes the guest's undecided bytes and then **retires the native owner**.

**Three of those sites aren't memory reuse in general:**
- `ShaderResources.cpp:860` "a texture read";
- `requireGuestBuffers` (the shader buffer access);
- `GuestBufferMemory.cpp`'s dispatch-written ranges.

**Why that matters:** a compute pass or a texture can read a depth target's planes legitimately, as depth.
- **On main,** such a read publishes correctly when nothing is undecided, and the owner stays resident.
- **On the branch,** the owner would be retired after every such read. A title that later samples or draws that target natively would lose its native owner, which risks a new regression.
- **None of the four titles needs those three sites.** The tester's traces name `prepareDrawInputs` (vertex/index reads), `refreshResidentTarget` and `cachedStorageTexture` (the storage image access) and `DepthSurfaceViewWithStencilWriteIntent` (a new depth surface).
