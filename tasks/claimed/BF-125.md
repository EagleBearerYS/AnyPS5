## Why

Ghost of Tsushima (PPSA02225) samples a 2x D32S8 1920x2160 target that #6300 and BF-122 don't cover yet (milescoviello on #6300: comments 6266476777 and 6327252710):
- **The stencil and HTILE:** the stencil is a separate plane, and the HTILE is depth-only (no stencil state in HTILE). The HTILE is **not ITERATE_256 and not pipe-aligned**.
- **Two whole-range fills before the sample, both pattern 0:**
  - the HTILE, 0x50000 bytes, exactly `HtileMetadataBytes` for that extent. So the footprint formula holds for this non-pipe-aligned HTILE;
  - the stencil plane, 0x870000 bytes, exactly the 2-sample stencil footprint.
- **The stops:** on main it stops at 15 s warm on `native multisample stencil or depth format import is not implemented` (`DepthSurface.cpp:102`). With #6300 it stops at `native multisample stencil import requires HTILE stencil metadata`. With the format, stencil and HTILE refusals relaxed locally, the next stop is `native multisample depth metadata fill transition is not implemented`.

## Changes (each with its test, red/green)

1. **`fix(agc): import native multisample depth with a non-pipe-aligned depth-only HTILE when every word proves the same clear`.**
   - Accept a depth-only HTILE that isn't pipe-aligned (and not ITERATE_256; #6557 already handles that flag on its own) when **every HTILE word proves the same clear**. With identical words, their order doesn't matter, so the pipe layout can't change what they prove.
   - Keep refusing a non-pipe-aligned HTILE whose words differ.
   - Use `HtileMetadataBytes(extent)` as the range, which milescoviello's fill confirms.
2. **`fix(agc): take a whole-plane fill of a separate stencil plane as a stencil clear`.**
   - A DMA or compute fill covering exactly the separate stencil plane of a sampled native target becomes a stencil clear to the fill's byte value. Prove it the way #6300 proves a whole-HTILE fill: complete tracked stores since the fill, no CPU or queued GPU write since, the plane unchanged.
   - Record the clear on the stencil aspect.
   - A partial fill keeps the refusal.
3. **The `native multisample depth metadata fill transition` stop.**
   - With 1 and 2, Ghost's two fills (HTILE pattern 0, stencil plane pattern 0) before the sample should both be taken as clears.
   - Find what still refuses in `NoteDepthMetadataFill` for this shape. Likely the non-pipe-aligned HTILE fill, or the order of the two fills against the target's creation (the import may not be active yet when they land). Fix it within the same rules, or report exactly why not.
   - Gashrod1's note on #6300 (comment 6211048701) explains that this message can hide an earlier creation refusal. Check it's the real boundary, and keep #6423's first-refusal behaviour if main has it.
