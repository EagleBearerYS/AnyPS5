## Why

Lane F ports fable-void's shared-code fixes (BF-123, fork `port/fable-shared-fixes` 12aa911767). Resident Evil Village's (PPSA01556) profiled run on that tree shows fix 11 firing: `[storage] refreshes proved 536562, 655 of them over pending driver key stores (cumulative)` over 5 minutes at the menu. The PM decided (in the user's name) that fix 11 opens as its own PR, with his authorship and the counter line as its title evidence.

**The fix:** his 77e9ac4632, ported as **4cc49a9de** on our fork. A color target with fast-cleared DCC keys loses its key proof while the first draw's key store is still pending in the batch. So every later draw into the unchanged target runs the full storage refresh (host import lookup, write preparation over its pages, alias checks), once per draw instead of once per change. The fix keeps a proof tagged with the recorder's newest write note over the key range, while that write is still unsignaled and the range unstamped.

**The counter:** our **12aa91176**, `[storage] refreshes proved N, N of them over pending driver key stores (cumulative)`, behind `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW`.

**The test gap:** BF-123 carried the fix without his test. His `agc_driver_resident_target_refresh` (`ResidentTargetRefresh.cpp`, Windows-only) needs `DrawSubmitFixture.hpp` and his NewPath fixture, which main doesn't have. The PM wants the test written first.
