## Why

The user, 2026-10-15 00:03 PT: our PRs must be comprehensive, so that no newer PR can post a better version of our fix.

#6243 selects the record of a T# whose offset the CPU evaluates (fork branch `perf/shader-direct-cpu-evaluable-images` at `43855cb5e8`, on upstream main a077a88df5). It leaves two sibling cases:
- **Sampler (S#) columns are excluded** (`if (!address && !sampler)` in `TryMakeTableColumn`, ResourceTracker.cpp:657 at 43855cb5e8). A sampler column whose offset the CPU evaluates still snapshots its whole sampler heap at every capture.
- **Store, packed and BY2/BY4 operations on image tables** read zeros and fault on main. That's #757's limitation, and a regression against #5188's direct images. #6243 keeps it for selected records too, although a selected record is one known T#, just as #5188's direct images were.

Pointer heaps are not a gap: on main, #758 makes a pointer table direct-first (`if (address) { if (ValidateSource(direct)) return false; }`).
