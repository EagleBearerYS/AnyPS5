# BF-086: accept a Ballot word among the Phi arms of a pointer-keyed record pointer (#7395, Ratchet & Clank's next stop)

from: Bug Finder (2026-10-17 22:43 PT)
for: any
priority: high

## Why

ps54all ran our PR #7395 (fork `feat/shader-nested-sampler-tables`, head 4a4db4bf94, on #7223's head f4549448f7) on Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart (PPSA01474). The sampler stop is gone. 236 s in, a compute shader now stops with:

```
GetImageResource dword 0 is not a valid runtime value; chain: LoadAddressU32@pc=0x00008fd4(GetAddressResource(Phi(...6 levels...), ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x00001aa8(GetBufferResource(ReadConst(GetSrtResource, Immediate) x4), ShiftLeftLogical32(ReadFirstLane(SelectU32(...), Phi(...)), Immediate))), Immediate, Immediate, Immediate)
```

The full 2519-character chain is in https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/6191#issuecomment-6246149739.

**What the chain is:**
- The T# is loaded through a 64-bit pointer. Its high word is the record read at pc 0x1aa8. Its low word is a Phi tree six levels deep.
- The tree's leaves are 18 occurrences of that record read, 5 U32 immediates and 1 `CompositeExtractU32x4(Ballot(LogicalAnd(Phi, INotEqual32)), Immediate)`. The last is a lane-mask word from the waterfall loop.

**Why it's refused:**
- `TryMakeIndirectColumn` makes such a column pointer-keyed (commit 4a4db4bf94).
- First, `PointerRecordOrigin` (`core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceTracker.cpp:695-713` at 4a4db4bf94) walks the low word's Phis. It skips U32 immediates, and requires every other leaf to be a scalar memory read `EquivalentValue` to one origin.
- The Ballot word is neither, so it returns null and the column is refused.
- The 18 record-read leaves may also be several copies with different `ReadFirstLane` keys, which `EquivalentValue` (structural) wouldn't match.
