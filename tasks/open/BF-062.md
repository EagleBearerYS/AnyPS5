**BF-059 left one question for the PC** (`results/cloud-3/BF-059.md` §3). Under lazy preparation, the first use of an ABI-resolved shader prepares the queued ABI, then looks for its own artifact.
- On the harness the draws didn't match the resolved ABI. That cost 146 ms and 548 KB per drawn shader for entries nothing used.
- If Village's draws match their resolved ABIs, the queued preparation is the draw's own, and costs nothing extra.

A counter on the PC run decides it.
