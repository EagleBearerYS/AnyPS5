## Context

- **#6366** "fix(agc): leave a stage the title's pipeline ABI cannot prepare to its first use", head 695ec6cc4f (`upstream refs/pull/6366/head`). It defers a stage whose *preparation* fails at the title's pipeline ABI call to the stage's first use (`DeferPipelineStage`).
- **What it missed:** Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) on our fast tree aborted in the save load at that ABI call, with `AGC graphics: VGT_SHADER_STAGES_EN=0x2030: unsupported geometry input or output assembly`, then SIGABRT. Our older explorer tree never hit it, because it deferred every ABI call.
- **The follow-up (GAC-043, done):** fork `fix/agc-abi-decode-defer` at **661d3cba8d**, two commits on #6366's head: 6bd8647c27 (a decode failure defers every stage of the call) and 661d3cba8d (its test in `PreparedShaders.cpp`). With it, the title reaches control in its first scene.
