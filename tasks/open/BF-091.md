## Why

Our open #7395 ("read samplers through nested and pointer-keyed descriptor tables", head aed137caee, `upstream refs/pull/7395/head`) gets Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart (PPSA01474) further with each commit. Two testers now stop at the same code in two sibling compute shaders: every pc in their chains is 0x44 apart, and the record-table reads at pc=0x1aa8 are shared. The stops:
- **Tamalero:** compute 0x116bb61900, pc=0x99ec, in #6191 issuecomment-6247236255.
- **ps54all:** compute 0x116bb67000, pc=0x9a30, in #7395 issuecomment-6250828427.

The error is `GetImageResource dword 0 is not a valid runtime value; chain: LoadAddressU32@pc=0x9a30(GetAddressResource(<low>, <high>, Immediate, Immediate, Immediate))`.

In `core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceTracker.cpp` at aed137caee, two things refuse it:
- **(a) The low word:** besides record reads, immediates and the bare `CompositeExtractU32x4(Ballot(...), Immediate)` arms that b91f0508d skips (`LaneMaskWord`, ~715), `<low>`'s Phi tree has nested lane-mask ANDs. An example: `BitwiseAnd32(Phi(Phi(Phi(Phi, Phi), BitwiseAnd32(Phi, CompositeExtractU32x4)), Phi(…)), CompositeExtractU32x4(Ballot(Immediate), Immediate))`. `PointerRecordOrigin` (~729-752) returns null on them.
- **(b1) The high word:** `<high>` is itself a Phi of reads of the same record table at pc=0x1aa8, under different `ShiftLeftLogical32(ReadFirstLane(SelectU32(...)), Immediate)` keys. `TryMakeIndirectColumn` (~766-779) takes the high word only as one direct read at the low word's offset + 4 (`ScalarReadMemory` on the Phi fails).
- **(b2) Not part of this task:** `<high>` also has one arm `ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x908c(GetBufferResource(LoadAddressU32@pc=0x900c(GetAddressResource, …) ×4), Immediate)`, a dword at a fixed offset in a second buffer whose V# is loaded through a pointer. What it is waits on a tester's runtime values. This task keeps it refused.

No game data is involved. Shape synthetic shaders as BF-086 did for `--pointer-phi-ballot` and `--pointer-phi-copies` (wave64 waterfall shaders in `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/execution/ImageTable.cpp`, encodings checked with llvm-mc `-mcpu=gfx1030`).
