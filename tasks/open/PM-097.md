# PM-097: Render-scale prototype, step 2a: depth surfaces at the scaled extent and FragCoord rescaling
from: AnyPS5 Project Manager
for: any
priority: high

## Why

PM-096 (results/cloud-1/PM-096.md on `cloud-results`) built step 1 on fork `exp/render-scale` (ca5d881427). Colour-only scaling is byte-exact on lavapipe, but any draw that binds depth, or whose pixel shader reads FragCoord, demotes. So real 3D scene passes can't stay scaled. PM-096's own go/no-go says step 2a, depth scaling plus FragCoord rescaling, is the gate for any fps gain. The user wants this pursued. The PC demotion-count runs on Planet of Lana, Resident Evil Village and Animal Well will come in parallel and may reorder later steps. They're not expected to change this one.

## Scope (on top of `exp/render-scale`; new commits, no rewrite of step 1)

1. **Depth surfaces at the scaled extent**, under the same generic rule as colour: extent at least the video-out extent, single-sample, and the other step-1 conditions, adapted to `DepthSurface`.
   - A draw whose colour targets all scale and whose depth surface scales, renders scaled.
   - Make each `DepthSurface.cpp` path scale-aware, or a logged demotion point with a reason: plane copies, sampled depth and shadow reads, sample copies, stencil copies, and plane copies to guest memory (PM-096 lists :264/:291 creation, :506, :959, :1027, :1203 on its base; re-find them on the branch).
   - Mismatches (a scaled colour target with an unscaled depth, or the reverse) demote the draw's targets, as `MixedAttachment` does.
2. **FragCoord rescaling** in the recompiler, so pixel shaders that read SV_Position stay scaled.
   - Where the pixel builtins are translated (PM-096 points at `Translation/src/InstructionTranslator.cpp:385-418`), divide `.xy` by the active scale through a push constant or specialization constant, set only for scaled draws.
   - Unscaled draws must keep identical SPIR-V, or at least identical results. Say which, and keep the unset switch bit-identical to `main`.
   - If this needs a shader disk cache `FormatVersion` change, take the next one on the branch and note it.
3. **Counters:** keep the per-reason demotion counters and lines. Add reasons for the new depth paths.

## Tests (lavapipe, synthetic AGC streams in `tests/execution`)

- **A depth-tested 3D-like stream:** a colour target plus a D32 depth target, both 3840x2160, at scale 0.5. It must be byte-identical (colour and depth) to the same stream rendered natively at 1920x1080. If depth bytes can't be byte-identical, explain why and use the tightest honest comparison.
- **A fullscreen pass reading FragCoord** (UV = position / size) at 0.5, equal to native 1080p.
- **One demotion case per new depth reason.**
- **Unset bit-identical,** colour and depth.
- **`VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation`:** main against the branch, unset and scaled, with no new `VUID-`.
- **Step 1's tests still pass ×10.**
