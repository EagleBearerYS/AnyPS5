# BF-235: what fills the queue-0 committer's ~300 µs per commit (Octopath Traveler 0 is committer-bound), with a profile split (high priority)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why
Speed push in the user's name. Octopath Traveler 0 (PPSA28495) runs at ~6 fps at a character-creation UI page on main 16ff208dc3 (RTX 3070 Laptop, Windows, `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW=1`).

**Its 10 s profile lines** (medians over 60 intervals):
- **`[draw] pipelined (10 s, depth 8)`:** ~30,600 commits at **~301–308 µs each** on the committer, which is **~93% of the committer thread**. Each enqueue waits ~140 µs for room; 3.3 commits per draw packet. Packet drains: ~3,800, taking **4.0–4.4 s per 10 s**, by opcode 0x15 ×1,960, 0x3c ×910, 0x46 ×370, 0x10 and 0x3f ×245 each.
- **`[dispatch] driver phases queue 0 direct`:** 1,555 dispatches, **prologue 2,484 µs per dispatch** (4.1 s per 10 s), device call 103 µs.
- **`[draw] driver phases`:** a total of only ~100 µs per draw packet (key/lookup/validate 17.5, lock wait 31.7). So it's not lookup-bound like Resident Evil Village.
- **`[rescache] revalidate`:** 241 ms per 10 s (8,461 fast, 1,153 full, 1,031 of them audit walks).

**Other signals:**
- `[buffers] staging`: last 10 s, 1,132 regions, **726,084 KiB copied in and 726,084 KiB copied back**.
- `[resources] guest memory upload took 52–63 ms` (1 texture, 12 buffers), about 15 per 10 s.
- `[vk] deviceProc lookups inside the device call`: **56.3 per dispatch** (cumulative 14.0 M).
- `[recorder] in-place reads`: 284.9 M noted, **0 queries** (cumulative).
- The "read ranges" figure on the pipelined line is **cumulative** (`heldReadRanges.load()`, never reset). The real rate is ~73k per 10 s, ~2.4 per commit.

**Frame time:** 30,600 commits × ~300 µs ≈ 9.2 s of every 10 s, and at ~160 draws per frame that matches the ~170 ms frame. The committer is the ceiling at this scene. The queue worker's dispatch prologue (it waits for the committer to drain before each 0x15) is the same cost seen from the other side.
