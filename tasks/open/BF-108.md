# BF-108: bound the draw cache by bytes, as the dispatch cache is: Resident Evil Village's menu holds ~2.1 GB in draw-cache variants
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

At Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556)'s warm static menu, private memory grows ~0.5–0.64 GB/min. This is lane B's top lead.
- **The run:** qbf098a, the PC's tree = main 1ff5c8ac5c + #8926's fix + BF-092's `[buffers]` counter, with `APS5_NO_GPL=1 APS5_HOST_IMPORT_MIB=6400 APS5_PROFILE_DRAW=1`, lazy preparation.
- **The draw cache's own `[draw-cache]` line,** over the same window: entries grow to the 4,096 cap, variants to ~16,000, and the variant bytes from ~21 MiB to **~2,149 MiB** (`... 4096 entries (16393 variants, ~2114.9 MiB) ...`).
- **Its hit rate there** is ~4% (69 hits in 1,742 lookups per 10 s), and a validation costs ~836 µs.
- **The census** (read-only, outside the guest spans) grew mostly in 1 MiB blocks holding (begin, end) pairs of 32-byte guest ranges spaced 0x30 apart. Those are an image table's T# records, and they fit draw variants' `runs` captured over Village's ~21,500-entry bindless table. The 2 MiB heap blocks fit their words.

**The cause, on main 0a5080477f as on the PC's tree:**
- The **dispatch** cache evicts least-recently-used entries while `dispatchCache.size() > dispatchCacheEntries() || (dispatchCache.size() > 1 && dispatchCacheVariantBytes > DispatchCacheBytes())` (`Execution/src/Driver/Dispatch/DispatchInsert.cpp:128`). `DispatchCacheBytes()` defaults to 256 MiB (`APS5_DISPATCH_CACHE_BYTES`, `Dispatch/DispatchCache.cpp:48`). It came with Celegans12's d9e47d1e98, "implement image and sampler tables indexed at runtime offsets", whose test is `tests/execution/ImageTableCacheBounds.cpp`.
- The **draw** cache keeps `drawCacheVariantBytes` (`accountDrawVariant`, `Draw/DrawCache.cpp:66-76`), but its insert evicts only while `drawCache.size() > drawCacheEntries()` (`Draw/DrawCache.cpp:169`; `APS5_DRAW_CACHE_ENTRIES`, default 4,096). Nothing bounds its bytes.
