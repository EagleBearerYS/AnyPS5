## Context
- Resident Evil Village aborts 1 run in 3 on current main, ~3 min into the title warm-up:
  `FATAL: an address-based build copies 768 MiB of 1 registered ranges, past 64 MiB ... (... outside the write-watched arena)`
- PM-022's chain:
  1. the address-based pass imports without eviction (`importAllocation(..., evict = false)`, GuestBufferMemory.cpp ~2610), so a heap 8 MiB over the budget is refused;
  2. the heap can't be mirrored, because a window import earlier took some of its blocks off the write watch and nothing puts them back on Windows;
  3. it's copied, and the 64 MiB cap aborts.
- **A related throw** in the runs that go on: `AGC graphics: a heap mirror's range is no longer write-watched` (~1556 and ~1693, PM-022's experiment C).
- **Experiment A:** `evict = true` at ~2610 turns the red test green and keeps `agc_driver_graphics` green.

## Tasks (on current upstream/main)
1. **Find out why the address-based pass imports with `evict = false`:** git log/blame, comments in commit bodies, debt entries. State what eviction there could break, such as evicting an import that the same build or an in-flight batch still uses, or eviction thrash between builds.
2. **The fix candidate.** Prefer the smallest change that is safe given (1):
   - evict idle imports only as the last resort, when the alternative is a copy past the cap, rather than on every refusal;
   - a reused heap mirror whose range has left the watch is dropped and re-planned (import, then mirror with the unwatched blocks compared, then copy) instead of throwing;
   - the copy reason at ~2643 states the real reason (budget refusal, or partly off the watch) instead of always "outside the write-watched arena".
   If the "mirror a heap that is partly off the watch by comparing those blocks" option is clearly better than eviction, say why and build that instead.
3. **Tests:**
   - the red test turns green;
   - a new test for experiment C (mirror first, block off the watch, second build) that passes;
   - `agc_driver_graphics` and the `^agc` ctest stay green under CI's Linux flags, as the runner user.
   - Give the per-build cost of the change in the normal case (no refusal) with a number; it should be ~0.
4. **Overlaps:** recheck #6060 (head 2406c85f6 or newer), #5851, #4448, #1201 and #1896 against your diff.
