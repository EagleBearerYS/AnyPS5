# BF-059: let ResolveShaderAbi honour lazy preparation, so shaders that never draw stay unprepared (lane B)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

BF-054 (`results/cloud-3/BF-054.md`) measured what holds the emulator's memory after shader registration.
- **Lazy preparation works at registration.** With `APS5_LAZY_SHADER_PREPARE=1`, registration prepares nothing: 5,000 programs × 50k words take 108 MiB anonymous.
- **`ResolveShaderAbi` undoes it.** `Driver::ResolveShaderAbi` (upstream main `ShaderRegistry.cpp` ~1277) runs `PlanRegistered` and `PrepareRegistered` for every shader whose ABI it resolves, whatever the switch says. So lazy plus ABI resolution matches eager: 2,530 MiB.
- **What preparation holds:** about 0.5 MiB per prepared program. That's decoded plans 189 KiB, `ShaderInfo` image modes 71 KiB, registration SPIR-V 170 KiB, libraries 39 KiB.
- **Village matches the pattern (statistics only).** Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) runs with lazy preparation, yet reads all 12,252 of its cache's artifacts at boot, which fits it resolving every shader's ABI. At its menu the emulator's process heap holds 6.2 GB. At ~0.5 MiB for each of ~6,000 programs, preparation would account for ~3 GB of that.
- **Memory room is what limits Village runs on the PC now:** its menu takes 25.5–26.7 GB of a 47.4 GB commit limit.

**Related open PRs:**
- **Our #6366** (fork branch `fix/agc-defer-unpreparable-abi-stages-v3`, head 8cd0b37a5f) already leaves a stage the title's ABI can't prepare to its first use. Build on it.
- **MarcZX100's #6178** releases a registered shader's resource plan after preparation, until a capture uses it. This task's change should compose with it, not duplicate it.

**No game data:** synthetic shaders only.

## Steps

1. **Read the draw path:** how `ResolveShaderAbi`'s prepared entries (`prepared.entries`, `registeredAbis`) are used at a draw, and what a draw does today for a lazily registered shader. Say whether the ABI key, and the context and primitive registers it was resolved with, can be kept and the preparation deferred to the first draw that needs that ABI. Say what, if anything, has to stay at resolve time, such as a validation that needs the plan.
2. **Prototype it,** stacked on #6366's head 8cd0b37a5f, as fork branch `perf/agc-lazy-resolve-shader-abi`.
   - Under `APS5_LAZY_SHADER_PREPARE=1`, `ResolveShaderAbi` records the ABI key and its state, and prepares nothing. The first use plans and prepares it with that state.
   - Without the switch, behaviour is unchanged.
3. **Measure** on BF-054's harness (fork `exp/shader-memory-residency`, `tests/execution/ShaderResidency.cpp`, `RESIDENCY_RESOLVE_ABI=1`) at N = 5,000 × 50k words. Measurement code goes on `exp/bf059-measure`.
   - Anonymous memory after registration and after drawing 10%: lazy, lazy + ABI on the base, and lazy + ABI with the change.
   - The first use's preparation time per program.
4. **The red/green test,** a new ctest in the same commit as the change. Under lazy preparation, N registered shaders get their ABI resolved and only some of them draw.
   - **The checks:** the ones that never draw hold no prepared entries (use the registry's counters, or add one), and a drawn one draws the same bytes as with eager preparation.
   - **The red:** the change commit with its `ShaderRegistry.cpp` hunk reverted must fail. Give the message.
   - **The green:** the commit as committed must pass. Give the passing line.
5. **Checks:** `ctest -R '^(agc|shader)'`, plus CI's Linux job mirrored on the head.
