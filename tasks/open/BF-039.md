# BF-039: keep every texture lookup record of a build, so builds with over ~1,000 textures revalidate on the fast path (PR candidate)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

BF-033 found the cause (results/cloud-3/BF-033.md §2 and §5). `logLookup` (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp`, about line 389 on main) keeps at most 1,024 lookup records and drops the oldest half when it is full. So a build with more than ~1,000 texture lookups loses its own earliest records. Its resource object then has no record for those textures, and every later `Revalidate` fails the fast proof with `no record` and walks every texture.
- **BF-033's harness, 2,000 textures:** 4.1 ms per dispatch on main, against 0.68 ms with the bound raised to 8,192. That's 6x, on the plain (non-address) path, for draws and dispatches alike.
- **Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556), profiled menu:** draws bind about 2,000 textures. Over the second half of the run, `[rescache] revalidate` made 15,528 full walks. 97.7% of them were `no record`, and each took 2.32 ms. A fast proof averaged 0.078 ms.
  - So the bound costs about 1.0 s of every 10 s of queue 0's time.
  - It runs inside `GuestMemory::GpuMutex`: `Driver::commitDraw` holds the mutex across `VulkanDevice::Draw`.
  - That makes it ~10% of the menu's frame time, and it serializes the other queues too.
