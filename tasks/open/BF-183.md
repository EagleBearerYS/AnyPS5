**What exists:**
- **`main`** serves CMASK-compressed FMASK only as the 32-bit 8-sample, 8-fragment view, from the tracked multisample target. See `MultisampleTarget.cpp` `fmaskSnapshotResource` (requires `Format32UInt` and a CMASK address), `fmaskResource(target)` and the target match at ~1261.
- **#8429** (milescoviello, head 4feda344e6, open) adds the narrower *uncompressed* layouts through an expansion: `FmaskLayoutOf` in `RdnaDescriptorFormat.cpp` (S2F1, S4F1, S8F1, S2F2, S4F2, S4F4, S8F2, S8F4, S8F8) and `FmaskExpansion` in the materializer. It refuses dword 6 bit 21 (CMASK compression) unless the format is `Fmask32_S8_F8`:
  - `ResourceMaterializer.cpp` ~232;
  - `GuestTextureResource.cpp` ~146.
- **The titles' layout is unknown.** Their logs don't name it. If it's EQAA (fewer fragments than samples), frankea's #9700 ("draw EQAA color targets at their coverage sample count") and fma-rc's #9450 ("draw a one-fragment multisampled color target as one sample") are on the same path. Read both, and say how your change composes with them.
