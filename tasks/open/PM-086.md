## Questions
1. Group his commits in this scope into **pieces**: sets of commits that make sense alone.
2. For each piece:
   - what it does, in two lines;
   - whether it depends on code he deleted (capture, ShaderMemory, the draw and dispatch caches, recipes) or could run on main's CPU path, behind a switch, as a fallback-safe change;
   - its files, and whether it cherry-picks onto current main (dry runs or `git merge-tree`);
   - his numbers from commit bodies;
   - a cheap measurement before the PC.
3. Name the **fixes to things main also has**: bugs he found and fixed in shared code. Those can be ported as they are, and are often the quickest wins.
4. Rank the pieces by what they'd give Village (and generally) on main, with your confidence.
5. Overlaps with open upstream PRs (fetch `refs/pull/N/head` where you suspect one: #6946, #6947 and Raghav314's heap work, #1896, #9012, #9243, #9248). Name what you checked.
