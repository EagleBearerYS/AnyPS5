## Why

PRM-056 (`results/cloud-1/PRM-056.md`) rebased #7642 onto #9508 5547aa11ef as fork `rebase/7642-on-9508-5547aa1` = **9e843c1a9e**. In its CI mirror, the only failure on our head alone was #9508's own test `agc_driver_pipelined_fill_gpu_heap`, failing with "AGC graphics: a satisfied WAIT_REG_MEM skipped an overlapping queued write" (`tests/execution/PipelinedFill.cpp:219`).
- **Under load** it failed 8 times in 350 runs on the head, against 1 in 350 on #9508 alone.
- **Interleaved side by side,** it failed 3/250 against 1/250.

Our code should be inert in that test, since it sets `APS5_GPU_HEAP=1` but not `APS5_GPU_PATH`. Before #7642 is force-pushed, the Project Manager wants that gap either closed by a second batch or explained.
