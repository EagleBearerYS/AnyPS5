## Context

- **Read `results/cloud-3/GAC-030.md` first.** Its branch, fork `fix/agc-sample-location-grid-fallback` **668ecaeca**, is your base. Keep:
  - its pipeline-creation decision (`hostSampleLocationGrid`, Pipeline.cpp:184-189);

  - its 24 mock-device pipeline builds.
- **GAC-030's addendum 1 was never answered.** It was appended about 4 minutes after the claim. Its §6 rejects the row-parity split and the index remap on cost, but nothing was measured, and it doesn't weigh shader-side reconstruction or interpolation only. This task is that addendum.
- **The pattern.** Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556), 2 samples:
  - `PA_SC_AA_SAMPLE_LOCS_PIXEL_X0Y0_0` = `X1Y0_0` = 0x00000c04, and `X0Y1_0` = `X1Y1_0` = 0x0000040c.
  - So every pixel has samples at x = +4/16 and -4/16 (y = 0). Only their indices swap on odd rows. That's a **same-set** pattern: a per-row permutation of one set of positions.
  - Resident Evil 4 (PPSA07411) has the same scheme with the rows swapped (fdfawef324 on #5907).
- **The devices:**
  - NVIDIA RTX 3070 Laptop (driver 617.42): `sampleLocationSampleCounts` 1|2|4|8|16, `maxSampleLocationGridSize` 1x1. This is the device that needs the emulation.
  - The PC's AMD iGPU (Ryzen 9 5900HS): grid 2x2 for 2|4|8, with no 1-sample custom locations. It reproduces the pattern natively, which makes it the bit-exact reference.
  - Lavapipe has no `VK_EXT_sample_locations` and no 2x.
- **Why the fallback isn't exact:** with pixel (0,0)'s pattern everywhere, coverage and depth at each position are exact, but odd rows store them under swapped sample indices. Anything that sees the index then differs from the console: sample-indexed loads and fetches, FMASK, resolves that pick a sample, SampleID-dependent shading, and sample masks. A checkerboard resolve swaps two full-resolution pixels on odd rows.

## Task
