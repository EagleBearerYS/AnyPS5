## Why

Our open #7395 ("read samplers through nested and pointer-keyed descriptor tables") now merges `main` 9caecfd2d5. Its head is aed137caee (`upstream refs/pull/7395/head`). Two features meet in `core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceTracker.cpp` there:
- **#6243's selected columns (from `main`):** in `TryMakeTableColumn`, the `if (!address && !sampler)` block (~684-694 on aed137caee) gives a read whose key the CPU evaluates a column with `selected = true`, holding only the selected record.
- **#7395's nested tables:** a leaf column whose heap T#s come through a V# read from a parent table gets a `parentPointer` on its plan (~650-671; `TryMakeIndirectColumn` sets it at ~825).

When both apply, the materialization loop (~149-158) gives the handle's arguments 1 and 2 the parent pointer's words and the other arguments `Constant(column.addend)`, the selected offset. It also keeps the selection source alive.

A reviewer, blawesom, pointed out that no test covers that intersection: https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/7395#issuecomment-6248752249. This task is coverage. There's no known bug.
