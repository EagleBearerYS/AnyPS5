## Goal

BF-245's census (`APS5_MEMORY_CENSUS`) ran on Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) on the PC: Windows, RTX 3070 Laptop, 16 GB.
- **Numbers:** with the draw cache at its 256 MiB default (main since #9129 merged), private at menu+15 is 17,844 MiB = guest arena 6,495 + process heaps 6,085 + host-visible device memory 883 + **unattributed 4,453**.
- **What we don't know:** three things are unnamed. This task adds the census lines that name them. It is diagnostic only: no PR, and no game data is involved.
  1. **Unattributed ~4.4 GB, flat over 14 min.** Candidate: the Vulkan driver's descriptor pools. 284-307 live, for ~41.5k sets and ~423k descriptors; on NVIDIA they land in process private. Your harness run had 173 pools = 2.2 GB of 3.7 GB RSS on lavapipe.
  2. **Heap growth with the draw cache capped:** +922 MiB over 14 min on a static menu. Candidates are your BF-245 caps table's never-erased owners: `drawRelocations`/`drawRelocationTrials`/`drawAliases` (DrawRelocate.cpp), the recompiler's `sources` map (Recompiler.cpp:468/:502), and the resource/template cache (no byte counter).
  3. **Process heaps' flat ~4.9 GB** not covered by the draw and dispatch caches.

## What to add (all behind `APS5_MEMORY_CENSUS`; with it unset, nothing new runs)

### (a) Descriptor pools sized

Add a `[mem-census] descriptor pools` line. It must give the private memory the driver commits for our pools, split into chain pools (the 1024-set pools in `DescriptorCache`, ShaderResources.cpp ~:4201) and dedicated pools (one per set too big for them).
- **Required:** per kind, the live count, the requested `maxSets` and descriptor counts, and the measured private bytes.
- **Acceptable ways to measure:**
  - **Create-time delta:** take `PrivateUsage` (`GetProcessMemoryInfo`, `PROCESS_MEMORY_COUNTERS_EX`) right before and after each `vkCreateDescriptorPool`, under the existing census wrapper, and also around the first set allocation from that pool. Drivers may commit lazily. Other threads allocate at the same time, so report the sum and the median per kind.
  - **Probe at census time (preferred if you can make it clean):** under the descriptor cache's lock, create one pool with each distinct live create-info, allocate its sets as the live pool did, measure the private delta, and destroy it. Report bytes per create-info × live count. Bound the probe, for example to the 16 largest distinct create-infos, and print how many it skipped.
- **Linux:** the same numbers from RSS (`/proc/self/statm`), or the memfd mapping sizes, as BF-245 found on lavapipe.

### (b) Byte counters for the never-erased owners

Add them to the `driver` line, computed under each owner's own mutex at census time:
- `drawRelocations`, `drawRelocationTrials` and `drawAliases`: entries and bytes (container capacity × element size, plus owned vectors' capacities);
- the recompiler's `sources` map (SPIR-V and plan bytes held, entries);
- the resource/template cache: entries and bytes, including table elements.

Estimates from capacities are fine. Say in the result what each counts.

### (c) Unattributed private memory grouped

On Windows, from the existing `VirtualQuery` walk, group the MEM_PRIVATE committed regions that are neither guest arena nor process-heap segments:
- **Heap segments:** take them from `HeapWalk` region entries at census time.
- **Print:** a size histogram of allocation bases (by committed bytes: <1 MiB, 1-4, 4-16, 16-64, ≥64 MiB, with count and total per class) and the 16 largest allocation bases (base, committed MiB, region count, protection).
- **Cost:** keep the block's cost printed. It was 1.9-4.3 s per block on the PC; stay within about 2× that.
- **Linux:** group anonymous `/proc/self/smaps` mappings by size the same way.
