## The fix

The test must stop depending on how large the fixture's SPIR-V is. Two options; pick the one that keeps the test's intent, and say why:
- **(a)** give `EvictionWatermarkTests` its own empty journal, as 582e856556 does for the race;
- **(b)** size its three records from the rewrite target instead of fixed 16 Ki-word payloads.

What the test checks stays the same: three consecutive records after a rewrite must not trigger another rewrite. Keep the 1 MiB cap and the watermark the production code uses. Don't change production code.
