## A. #6636 with #6646 (heap mirrors that left the write watch)
- **Heads:** #6636 ec17620356c3f3207cd8548cfd91fb5e9d7eeffa, #6646 48c17f97dd4c62a1332b29256b393dd8b753d862. They conflict in `GuestBufferMemory.cpp`.
- **The fix:** see results/cloud-2/GAC-040.md section 6 on the `cloud-results` branch. #6636's `ResumeSpace` refreshes a cached space's heap runs after `spaceMiss` returns `SpaceMiss::None`, so it can still hit "a heap mirror's range is no longer write-watched". Make `spaceMiss` return a new `SpaceMiss::Unwatched` when any of `current->heapMirrors` fails #6646's `heapMirrorWatched`, and give `AcquireRegistered`'s switch `case SpaceMiss::Unwatched: break;` in place of #6646's `else hit = std::all_of(...)`. Count it with the other rebuild reasons if that switch counts them.
- **Branch:** merge #6646's head into #6636's head (a merge commit that resolves the conflict this way), then one commit with the test. Push it as fork branch **`integration/6636-x-6646`**.
- **Test:** a cached address-based draw whose heap mirror's range leaves the write watch: the next draw rebuilds or replans instead of throwing. Put it in `tests/execution/AddressDrawReuse.cpp` (#6636) or #6646's test file, whichever fits. Report its red with the `Unwatched` case removed, and its green.

## B. #6636 with #6628 (native MSAA spans left out of BDA tables)
- **Heads:** #6636 as above, #6628 = fetch `pull/6628/head`, which should be 4c415b5135 or later. Record the head you used. They merge cleanly.
- **The fix:** #6628 builds a BDA table under `guardSerial = MultisampleTargetRangeGeneration()` and filters native MSAA spans out of it. A #6636 reuse keeps the build's `BdaResources` (ShaderResources.cpp, `bda` is made once), and none of its checks read that generation.
  - Have `BdaResources` keep the guard generation its table was built or found under, and expose `bool TableCurrent() const` (that generation equals `MultisampleTargetRangeGeneration()`).
  - In `ShaderResources::Revalidate`'s address-based path, next to `SpaceImportsCurrent()`, refuse the hit when `bda != nullptr && !bda->TableCurrent()`.
- **Branch:** merge #6628's head into #6636's head, then one commit with the fix and its test. Push it as fork branch **`integration/6636-x-6628`**.
- **Test** (in #6628's `tests/execution/SampleMultisampleTarget.cpp` or #6636's `AddressDrawReuse.cpp`):
  - build a cached address-based draw;
  - add a native MSAA guard over one of its BDA ranges, and require that the next draw rebuilds (no reuse) and that its table leaves the span out;
  - clear the owner, and require another rebuild with no BDA fault.
  - Report its red without the `TableCurrent()` check, and its green.
