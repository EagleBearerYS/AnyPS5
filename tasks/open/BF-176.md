## Why

Lane F ports fable-void's shared-code fixes (BF-123, fork `port/fable-shared-fixes` 12aa911767). Resident Evil Village's (PPSA01556) profiled run on that tree shows fix 5 firing: `[present] ... waits outside the GPU mutex for the display keys' writer: 5, 9.8 ms` per 10 s at the menu. The PM decided (in the user's name) that fix 5 opens as its own PR, with his authorship and the counter line as its title evidence.

**The fix:** his 9ed1466f35, ported cleanly as **3636975be**, one line in `VulkanDevice.cpp` (~3152):

    if (buffer.dccAddress != 0 && buffer.tilingMode == 0) state->recorder->SyncThrough(buffer.dccAddress, DisplayBufferKeyBytes(buffer), true);

- The presenter checks a display buffer's DCC keys under the device lock.
- When the batch with the kernel that writes those keys hasn't finished, the check waits for the GPU while holding the lock, and the queue worker recording the next frame waits behind it.
- The fix syncs through the key range first, with the unlocked wait the flush hook uses.
- His message reports, in a title's menu on Windows: GPU waits under the lock went from 721 (1.38 s) to 1 (0 ms) per 10 s.

**The counter:** our **8429b39d2**, the `[present]` line `waits outside the GPU mutex for the display keys' writer: N, X ms`, behind `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW`.

**There is no test**, on his side or ours.
