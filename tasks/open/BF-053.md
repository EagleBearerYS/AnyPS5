# BF-053: why mesh-path programs are ~1M SPIR-V words, and a prototype that shrinks them
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) has 12,252 compiled modules in its shader cache, measured on the PC. The mesh-path ones are huge on upstream main today:
- **Mesh** (execution model MeshEXT): 1,703 modules. Medians are 651k words without image tables and 929k with them; 1,375 are over 500k, and the largest is 1.18M.
- **Vertex:** 2,877 modules, median 44k–57k words, but 253 are over 500k.
- **Fragment:** 7,600 modules, median 51k–79k.

So the mesh-path modules are ~15–20× their vertex programs.
- **Inside the largest one:** 15 functions, 23,772 blocks. Almost all its words are integer and bit arithmetic: Select 10.5%, LogicalAnd 8.6%, IAdd 7.4%, IEqual 6.7%, ISub 4.9%, BitwiseOr 4.5%, ShiftLeftLogical 4.3%, BitFieldUExtract 4.1%. Modules that also read image tables carry seven 77-way switches each.
- **Why it matters:** Village's cold title menu is pipeline-bound (~42–102 s of `Vulkan.GraphicsPipeline` per frame on a cold run). Pipeline objects for ~1M-word modules likely also take a share of its ~25 GB of private memory.

No open upstream PR or issue covers this size. Where it comes from: `Graphics/src/State.cpp` ~849–899 sends a draw down the mesh path when it has a geometry shader (GS_EN) or a vertex program that needs a workgroup. The recompiler then builds a mesh program from the stages (`MeshConfiguration`, `ShaderInputInfoBuilder`, the SPIR-V backend).

**No game data:** use synthetic shaders only. The numbers above are statistics from the PC.

## Steps

1. **Find the multiplier.** Read how the recompiler builds a mesh-path program from a vertex program, with and without a geometry program. Find what's emitted once per vertex slot, per primitive or per output, and what the 77-way switch is.
2. **Measure on synthetic programs:** module words as the multiplier's inputs grow. For example: vertex programs of 1k, 5k and 20k instructions; geometry programs with 3, 6 and 16 output vertices; a vertex program that needs a workgroup. Report words per configuration and the ratio of mesh words to vertex words, plus pipeline creation time on lavapipe for each.
3. **Prototype the cut,** behind a switch, default off. For example, emit a duplicated body once as a function called in a loop, rather than inlined per slot. It must give the same results on the existing mesh-path tests (`ctest -R '^(agc|shader)'`, mesh, geometry and tessellation cases included), plus one new test comparing output with the switch on and off.
4. **Report** words and creation time, before and after, for the step-2 configurations. Name the disk-cache `FormatVersion` bump the change needs.
