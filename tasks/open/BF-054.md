# BF-054: what holds the emulator's own memory after shader registration, and whether it can let go of SPIR-V
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

Lane B of the AGC redesign (`pr-drafts/arch/redesign.md`) cuts the memory the emulator holds. Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) pages at 26 GB of private memory on a 16 GB PC. On 2026-10-17 its title menu was measured (statistics only):
- private usage 26.2 GB, working set 1.6 GB;
- **the emulator's own private memory, outside the guest arena: 19.7 GB.** The guest arena holds 6.2 GB, and 8.6 GB more is mapped outside the guest spans;
- at boot, before the title, the emulator held 11.0 GB of its own. So it grew by 8.7 GB on the way to the menu;
- over that same stretch the run read **all 12,252 SPIR-V artifacts** of its shader disk cache, **8.32 GiB** in total (`[shader-disk-cache]` totals), with `APS5_LAZY_SHADER_PREPARE=1`;
- the cache's mesh-path modules are ~1M words each (BF-053); 1,703 of them come to ~6 GB of SPIR-V.

So the question: does the emulator keep every loaded artifact's SPIR-V words (and the recompile results around them) in memory for the whole run? If it does, can it let go of them once their pipelines exist, and reload them from the disk cache when a new variant needs them?

**No game data:** synthetic shaders only. The numbers above are statistics from the PC.

## Steps

1. **Read and report** (upstream main). With and without `APS5_LAZY_SHADER_PREPARE`, cover:
   - which objects hold a registered or used shader's artifact: `SharedSpirv` holders, `RecompileResult`, the draw and dispatch caches' variants, the registry's prepared entries and plans, the pipeline libraries and journal;
   - when each is created and when it's freed;
   - when registration reads an artifact from the disk cache (`ShaderRegistry::Register` → `PlanRegistered` / `PrepareRegistered`) under lazy preparation.
2. **Measure on a synthetic, registration-heavy test.** Register N synthetic shaders (N = 1,000 and 5,000) with modules of ~50k and ~500k words, draw with 10% of them, then report:
   - process private bytes, split into SPIR-V words held (count them), recompile results, caches and the Vulkan driver;
   - with and without lazy preparation;
   - with a warm disk cache, to match the title.
3. **Prototype the cut,** behind a switch, default off: drop a program's SPIR-V words (and anything else only needed to build pipelines) once its pipelines are built, keeping the disk-cache key, and reload on demand for a new variant or pipeline state. Report:
   - private bytes before and after, at step 2's settings;
   - the reload count and time for a draw sequence that needs new variants;
   - correctness on `ctest -R '^(agc|shader)'`, plus one new test where a program dropped and reloaded draws the same pixels.
4. **Name** the counters a profiled title run would need to size this on the PC (for example, SPIR-V bytes held, artifacts resident, reloads per 10 s), and add them to the prototype under `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW`.
