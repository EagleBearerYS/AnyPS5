# BF-016: count the guest arena's committed bytes in the profile output
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

A diagnostic for our Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) test tree. No PR now; it may become one if the number proves useful.

## Why

In Resident Evil Village's first scene on this PC, private memory grows ~140 MB/min (22.4 to 25.2 GB in ~20 min). PM-049 (`results/cloud-1/PM-049.md`) found that refused imports keep nothing alive on main. The scene's profile has no driver figure that grows by that much: heap mirrors are flat at 621 MiB, the buffer pool is flat, and the texture cache sits at its budget.

The remaining candidate is the game itself committing more of its own memory as the scene touches it. That isn't a leak. To tell the two apart, the profile needs the guest arena's committed bytes next to the driver's own figures.

## Task

1. **The counter.** Keep a running count of the guest arena's committed bytes, from the arena's own bookkeeping. Don't walk the address space every 10 s.
   - Windows: `GuestArena` / `WindowsMappings`, both private commits and shared views. Commit, reset, release and remap must all keep it exact.
   - Linux and macOS: what the arena commits, resets and releases.

   Split it by kind where the arena knows the kind: private, shared or direct, flexible.
2. **The output.** Print it every 10 s under `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW=1`: either one line `[arena] committed X MiB (private Y, shared Z), reserved W MiB`, or fields on the existing `[guestmem]` line. Follow the existing reporting style, and print nothing without profiling.
3. **A test.** Commit, reset and release known amounts, and check the counter after each step on both platforms. Register it in `CMakeLists.txt`, next to the arena's existing tests.
4. **Branch:** `diag/agc-guest-arena-committed` on the fork, on upstream main 5d9dcc42d2.
5. **Checks:**
   - CI's configure flags and the build;
   - `tools/check_test_registration.py build`;
   - `ctest -R '^(guest_|agc_write_tracking)'` and the new test ×10;
   - `tools/check_conventions.py --base upstream/main`;
   - MinGW `-fsyntax-only` on the Windows paths.
