## Why

This is tonight's top priority. The PM decided it in the user's name, because the user wants real speed gains.

On Resident Evil Village's menu (profiled boot, PROFILE_DRAW, main ebb4075168 + #12478), the busy queues 0x0 and 0x20 take turns on the GPU mutex. Inside each dispatch's resource phase:
- **`resources: hook waits`** is 32 s over 74,000 dispatches, so 0.43 ms per dispatch (`[dispatch]` phase totals).
- **The `[hooksync]` lines, per 10 s:**
  - recorded-store syncs: 360–397, waiting 1.75–1.88 s, with 101–102 batches in flight at them;
  - by queue/packet/site:

    | queue | packet | site | count | wait |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | 0x0 | DISPATCH_DIRECT | unknown | 130–145 | 421–468 ms |
    | 0x0 | DRAW_INDIRECT | unknown | 104–116 | 405–449 ms |
    | 0x28 | DISPATCH_DIRECT | buffer-upload | 26–29 | 333–364 ms |
    | 0x20 | DISPATCH_DIRECT | unknown | 78–87 | 327–355 ms |
    | 0x0 | WAIT_REG_MEM_64 | unknown | 20–22 | 243–264 ms |

  - pending-write syncs: 115, waiting 1,233 ms. The largest are 0x0 DRAW_INDEX_INDIRECT indirect-args (28 / 760 ms), 0x0 DISPATCH_DIRECT buffer-upload (65 / 238 ms) and 0x0 WAIT_REG_MEM_64 (22 / 235 ms).
- **`[syncwait]`, unlocked hook waits by read site:** unknown 59–74, waiting 358–595 ms; indirect-args 25–29, waiting 188–273 ms; average target submit-to-signal 7.6–10.7 ms.
