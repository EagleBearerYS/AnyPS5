## Context
- **Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) at its title:** the draw stage's capture walk plus resolveImageTables take ~55% + 15% of the queue-0 worker (profiled). The clean load trace has Shader.DrawStage at 171 of 332 ms per frame. Narrowing applies 0 of 34,294 snapshots, and each table has 32,768 keys.
- **The cause** (PM-026, results/cloud-2/PM-026.md on `cloud-results`):
  - since #757, a T# read from a constant buffer at a user-data offset is planned as a one-column table instead of an ordinary image;
  - every draw-cache miss then snapshots the whole column (~100k words).
  - Celegans12 stated this change on #757 at 10:35 UTC: "a T# read from a constant buffer at a user-data offset becomes a one-column table instead of an ordinary image". That case is #5188's, which is the user's merged PR.
- **The helper patch already written:** test/agc-image-tables-direct-first bad2ffdc5 (cloud-2). With `APS5_IMAGE_TABLES_DIRECT_FIRST=1`, CPU-evaluable buffer-heap T#s go direct, the way pointer tables already do (ResourceTracker.cpp:615-622), and a second commit names why narrowing is skipped.
