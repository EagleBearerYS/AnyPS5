## Why

jotitadev reviewed #10043's head **d0bd686d8d** (https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/10043#pullrequestreview-5720354784). With Khronos validation layer **1.4.341**, `agc_driver_presentation_fsr` and `agc_performance_overlay` log `VUID-vkCmdBeginRenderPass-initialLayout-00897` on every run, on an RTX 3070 and on lavapipe. Layer 1.3.275 (Ubuntu 24.04's `vulkan-validationlayers`, and the one GitHub's runners and the NVIDIA bot use) reports nothing.

The cause: the FSR stage pass and the EASU pass end in `SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL`. But the framebuffer views in `PresentationPass::destinationFramebuffer` and `PresentationUpscaler::transientFramebuffer` narrow their usage through `VkImageViewUsageCreateInfo` to `COLOR_ATTACHMENT` plus transfer bits, so they lack `SAMPLED`.

MarcZX10 has requested changes. They ask us to rerun the focused presentation, FSR and performance-overlay tests "with the current validation layer and report the exact head and VUID results". GitHub's runners can't show this red, so the cloud has to.
