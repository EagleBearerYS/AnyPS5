## Context

- **Read first:** `results/cloud-3/PRM-128.md` §3a, §3e and §4.
  - In #9508's 73c5187825 (Raghav314, `perf(agc): embed constant samplers in GPU heap pipelines`), `RecompileResult::Identity()` (`core/shader/recompiler/Recompiler.hpp:868-885` at 73c5187825) doesn't mix `DescriptorBinding::samplerEmbedded` or `samplerEmbeddedWords` (`:586-587`), or the sampler flags.
  - The words reach neither the SPIR-V (`SpirvDescriptors.cpp:276-284` emits only `DescriptorSet 6` and `Binding arrayIndex`) nor the specialization.
  - `ComputePipelineKey::Of` (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:443`) keys on `Identity()`. So two compute stages that differ only in a constant sampler's words share one pipeline and one embedded sampler. The pipeline journal names stages by the same identity (`Graphics/src/PipelineJournal.cpp:824`).

  - If #9508's head moves and already carries a fix for this, stop and report.
