# BF-102: compose our two BDA fault-buffer PRs: BF-099's spare fault records taken from BF-098's shared arena
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: normal

## Why

Two of our PRs, both opening as drafts today, change the same fault buffers:
- **BF-098**, fork `perf/agc-shared-bda-fault-buffers` **2545cdfbd4**, two commits on upstream main a07e985c8f.
  - Fault buffers become slots of a per-device arena of 512 KiB host-visible chunks, and an idle owner's clean slot is reclaimed under the GPU lock.
  - Programs whose SPIR-V provably reaches the fault buffer only at its 8 report words (`SpirvFaultReportOnly`) get a 32-byte slot instead of `BdaAbi::FaultBufferBytes`.
  - `APS5_NO_SHARED_FAULT_SLOTS=1` restores one full buffer per owner.
- **BF-099**, fork `perf/agc-reuse-address-dispatch-builds-v3-8104` **8b4e909421**, three commits on #6636's head fc9c5416fc.
  - Address-based dispatch builds are reused.
  - Every concurrent use after the first takes a spare from the build's pool: its own fault buffer, plus a descriptor set that repeats the build's writes with that buffer in the fault binding.

They conflict in `Graphics/include/BdaResources.hpp` and `Graphics/src/BdaResources.cpp`. Whichever merges second has to take BF-099's spare records from BF-098's arena; otherwise every spare keeps a full fault buffer for the life of its build. This task makes that resolution now and tests it, so it's ready whichever lands first.

2. **On top, one commit:** the spare records' fault buffers come from the arena.

   - A spare's slot follows BF-098's rule: it never moves while its use is in flight.
   - Decide whether a spare parked in the build's pool keeps its slot or returns it to the arena and takes a new one on reuse. Say why. Returning it is the default unless it costs measurably on BF-099's harness.
   - `APS5_NO_SHARED_FAULT_SLOTS=1` must still give full buffers to spares too.
