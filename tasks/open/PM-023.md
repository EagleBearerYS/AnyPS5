## Context
- Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556): after the scene cut from the title to the first 3D scene, the title's foreground objects stay visible, dimmed, over the dining room. They don't fade.
- PM-021 ranked **A** first. When a title fast-clears a surface's DCC keys through a path that doesn't reach `StorageTexture::NoteKeysFill`, sampled reads keep serving the pending title-era image:
  - the un-noted paths: a CP DMA fill of at most 64 KiB, a kernel the fill HLE doesn't take, or a CPU store;
  - the code: `ShaderResources.cpp` ~567-621 at 153d5d469, which gives the pending image up only for a noted fill or comp-to-single keys, and refreshes on colour bytes but not keys;
  - the target/storage `Refresh` (`Texture.cpp` ~1913) treats the same new clear keys as killing every result, so the two paths disagree on the same state.
- **B:** address-based shaders skip `FlushPending` (`GuestBufferMemory.cpp` ~3563; `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1` removes the exemption).
