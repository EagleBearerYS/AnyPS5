## Why

Raghav314 closed #6947 and opened **#9508** (`perf/agc-vk-descriptor-heap`, head 13764c98cc, upstream `refs/pull/9508/head`). It's stacked on his #6946 (draw pipeline); its last 24 commits are the heap. He **dropped the GPU descriptor walk**: the device slot table, `DeviceSlotTableAbi.hpp` and the ring-header route aren't in it. Resolving skipped draws from live guest memory at GPU time caused flicker and cost frame rate.

What he **kept:** image tables are resolved on the CPU into heap slots, through a per-element slot map in the heap ring that the shader reads via two pushed dwords. Tables with image atomics are on the heap too.

Our **#7642** (EagleBearerYS, upstream `refs/pull/7642/head`) does run-time image-table lookups on the GPU. It was built to stack on #6947's device slot table. Raghav314 says stacking on #9508 is still fine with him: our lookups need their own route, the ring's image-table map is the closest, and he offered to look at the ABI with us once we rebase (#6947 issuecomment-6329146339).

Before anyone rebases, the PM wants to know what of #7642 survives and how the shader would reach a device table without his slot table.
