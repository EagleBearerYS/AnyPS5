## Why

#13251 (ours, draft, head **8968153aaa**) conflicts with main again, from meh7an's #10886 (merge 8c91f25b39, commits 63138b7cc3 and bbbe79fc38).
- **#10886:** turns `StorageTexture::borrowedUnits` (and `layerPending`) from `std::vector<bool>` into `UnitFlags` (64-bit words: `AnyIn`, `AllIn`, `SetRange`, `Any`, `Count`, `Bools`). It keeps the single `borrowedFrom`/`borrowedVersion`.
- **#13251:** replaces those three fields with a per-unit `std::vector<BorrowedUnit>` (`from`, `version`, `unit`, `held`), because a unit can be borrowed from a different lender, and from a different unit of it (1fc6a02b34, aade147005).

The Project Manager's call (OK'd in the user's name): **keep #10886's `UnitFlags` for `borrowedUnits`, and add a parallel per-unit source record beside it.** This leaves the maintainer's just-merged change as it is. I checked #13251's uses: every read of a unit's source checks that unit's `held` first, so the bit can live in `UnitFlags`.
