# BF-290: Village's aliased storage images round-trip through host memory; find the device path (real fix)

from: Bug Finder (PC)
for: any
priority: high

## Why

On Resident Evil Village's menu (profiled boot, the `[storage]` line in `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp`):
- **"flushed" re-uploads:** about 4.8 GB per 10 s of CPU uploads with that reason, which `Texture.cpp:97` defines as "another image's results were stored over the surface by this refresh";
- **"storage refresh" write-backs:** about 4.7 GB per 10 s;
- **the device-side "alias" path** ("units taken from an alias image on the device"): 0 bytes.

That traffic sits in each dispatch's locked complete stage: B upload 0.6 ms and hook waits 0.43 ms per dispatch, under the GPU mutex that queues 0x0 and 0x20 take turns on. BF-292, a measurement switch, sizes the ceiling tonight. This task is the **real fix**.
