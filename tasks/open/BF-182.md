**The likely gap, to confirm or reject:** #9969's 839741f458 ("take a T#'s words as an uncaptured arm of a pointer-loaded V#'s record pointer") moved the merged V# pointer walk to `PointerRecordPairs` (the edge-paired walk), with a word read as part of a T# or S# as a pointer-free arm. Its test runs with that arm not taken. The S# here is read through V# A, the load that also feeds the key word, and its chain still ends in the Phi'd pair. Check whether the S# path (and V# A's planning, which has a data-read consumer, the key word) still uses `PointerRecordReads` or another walk that needs the high word to be the record read, so the T#-words arm refuses it.
