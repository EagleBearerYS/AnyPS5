# BF-307: C4's real fix, part 2: descriptor buffer uploads over a pending GPU write become GPU-ordered copies instead of a CPU wait

from: Bug Finder (PC)
for: any
priority: high

## Why

On Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556), C4 (`APS5_DIAG_SKIP_HOOK_WAITS=1 APS5_NO_PRESYNC=1`, BF-295) ran at 0.614 fps against the same binary's control at 0.400: **+54 %**. The PM decided in the user's name that the real fix starts now. BF-306 takes the indirect arguments; **this task takes the buffer uploads.**

**In the profiled control boot** (main ebb4075168 + #12478), per 10 s:
- **pending-write syncs:** `0x0 DISPATCH_DIRECT buffer-upload`, 65 syncs, 238 ms. The average noted range is 3,162 KiB and the average access 11,632 KiB, above the 1 MiB GPU-copy bound;
- **recorded-store syncs:** `0x28 DISPATCH_DIRECT buffer-upload`, 29 syncs, 364 ms.

**BF-295's verdict** (`results/cloud-2/BF-295.md`):
- **The site:** the build's CPU upload of descriptor regions, `GuestBufferMemory.cpp` ~5680-5697 on 41ef8ff856, scoped `BufferUpload`.
- **Deferrable to the GPU.** The consumer is the GPU, so a `vkCmdCopyBuffer` from the host import, recorded after the producer in batch order with a barrier and no CPU wait, gives the same bytes.
- **The driver already does this** for unaligned sub-ranges inside imports (~4983-4991), up to `APS5_GPU_COPY_MAX_KIB`, 1024 KiB by default.
- **What still waits:** regions outside imports, or above the bound.
