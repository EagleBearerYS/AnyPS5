# BF-293: ceiling switch C2, a dispatch's complete stage outside the GPU mutex (measurement only)

from: Bug Finder (PC)
for: any
priority: high

## Why

This is tonight's top priority. The PM decided it in the user's name, because the user wants real speed gains.

- **The measurement:** on Resident Evil Village's menu (profiled boot, `[lock]` and `[dispatch]` lines), queues 0x0 and 0x20 are the critical path.
  - Each waits **2.1–2.3 s per 10 s** for the GPU mutex, mostly behind the other's dispatches: 0x0 behind 0x20's dispatches 2,185 ms, 0x20 behind 0x0's 1,685 ms.
  - Each holds the mutex about 1.1 ms per dispatch, over about 2,600–2,700 dispatches per 10 s.
- **What fills that hold** (`[dispatch]` phase totals over 74,000 dispatches): `resources: complete (wall)` 72 s, which is B upload 44 s plus B images 24 s, then `revalidate` 30 s and `hook waits` 32 s. `resources A (unlocked)` is only 3 s.
- **Prior work:** BF-284 (fork `perf/agc-preresolve-outside-gpu-mutex`, c336ba2e77, in this base) moved only the key, the lookup and stage A out of the lock for *draws*. It found that stage B can't leave the lock without a texture-cache locking redesign.

We want the **ceiling**: Village's menu fps if 0x0's and 0x20's dispatch resource work ran concurrently instead of taking turns.

## Do

- **Base:** fork `diag/village-preresolve-on-fjseries-50c9bee85c` = **41ef8ff856**.
- **Branch:** fork `diag/village-ceiling-c2-on-41ef8ff856`, **one** commit, with the user's noreply author and committer. Push to the fork only. It never becomes a PR.
- **The switch:** `APS5_DIAG_UNLOCKED_DISPATCH_COMPLETE=1` (presence). Unset, it's exactly the base.
- **With it set,** a dispatch's resource preparation, meaning the complete stage (`buildComplete`: image lookups, `UploadFinish` / guest-memory upload, descriptor writes) and the hit's `Revalidate`, must stop holding the GPU mutex, so two queue workers can do it at the same time.
  - **Where to start:** look first at `VulkanDevice::PrepareDispatch` / `VulkanDevice::Dispatch` (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp`) and where the worker takes `GuestMemory::GpuMutex()`.
  - **What it may break:** it's allowed to be unsafe for rendering correctness.
  - **What it mustn't do:** crash on a plain data race. Containers the complete stage shares (texture caches, the storage registry, the resource cache, the import set, the recorder's pending structures) must not be mutated concurrently without a lock.
  - **The cheapest safe-from-crash form:** give those shared structures their own small mutexes, or one new `CompleteMutex` per structure family, taken only by the complete stage while the switch is set. The GPU mutex then no longer covers it. Lock-ordering deadlocks are worse than slowness; document the order you use.
  - **The recording** of the dispatch (commands, barriers, submit) stays under the GPU mutex.
- **No change under `core/shader/recompiler/`.**
- **No comments in code.**
