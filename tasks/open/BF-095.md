# BF-095: Lane B: port fable-void's plan retention onto today's main (a resource plan keeps only the IR values its evaluator reads)
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: high

## Why

Lane B's goal is Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) at ≤ ~16 GB private at its menu. It's ~21.4 GB with #7644.
- **The target:** resource plans are 1.40 GB of Village's 7.4 GB of shader data (lane B's census).
- **The cause:** main's `ownPlanValues` (`core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceMaterializer.cpp:2155` on today's main) copies the whole argument closure of every plan root: descriptor sources, SRT reads, control-flow conditions and fills.

fable-void's fork (https://github.com/fable-void/AnyPS5, branch `wip/new-gpu-path`) copies only what the plan's evaluator can read. PM-078 (results/cloud-1/PM-078.md, port item 1) ranked it the cleanest and largest single win in his push.
- **His numbers:**
  - "a title that registers about 5,700 shaders at load held 24.1 M copied IR values in its plans (about 5.4 GiB by value size), of which 2.1 M were reachable from descriptor sources, SRT reads and fills";
  - on his largest corpus, plan values 1,376,358 → 1,071,151 and private bytes 731.5 → 673.0 MiB;
  - "the plans of 314 programs hold 40 thousand values instead of 837 thousand".
- **The commits:**
  - 3728570f6: copy conditions only as far as `Evaluator::Reads` goes;
  - cb13fecfe: drop conditions the evaluator can never decide;
  - cca1f5a8d: below a condition, follow only what the evaluator reads;
  - 6d2ad7ee8: `SharedSpirv::Mapped()`, a report of mapped SPIR-V, which picks clean.
- **What PM-078's trial picks found** (on 10-18's main 195d0d08a): 3728570f6 conflicts in its three source files; cb13fecfe only in its test file, ShaderMemory.cpp; cca1f5a8d in CMakeLists.txt and the ResourceMaterializer.cpp hunk that builds on 3728570f6.

**Main has moved under it:**
- acee01a4e6 (Mehran, "perf(shader): compile resource plan walks instead of interpreting them", 2026-10-17) changed how plans are evaluated;
- c00b0dfb9f (MarcZX100, "avoid retaining IR after plan cache misses") and f95dc97134 (#6178, "release unused registered shader resource plans") changed what's retained.

The port must follow main's compiled walks: "what the evaluator reads" now means what the compiled walk reads.
