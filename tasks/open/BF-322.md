# BF-322: design pass, read-only: an exact change for the address-based draws (BF-041 §7 item 6)

from: Bug Finder (PC)
for: any
priority: high

## Why

At Resident Evil Village's menu, address-based (BDA) draws are the draw committer's largest cost. These are a profiled boot's counter lines, per 10 s, on a tree with #12745's index on:
- **Commits:** 8,057 at 862 µs each, 6.95 s busy.
- **`[draws]`:**
  - per kind: template hit 508 µs (3,545), build 2,394 µs (16), **BDA 1,126 µs (4,276)**. So BDA draws are about **4.8 s**;
  - `resources` 3,837 ms: bindings 2,582, other 1,116, of which 1,111.7 is in 4,276 address-based builds.
- **`[rescache] draws`:** 3,545 hits, 23 misses, **4,276 uncacheable**.
- **`[address]`:**
  - 4,719 address-based builds, µs per build: lease 2, **imports pass 81**, mirror prepare 12, compare 0, **snapshots 170**;
  - space hits 4,698; rebuilds: epoch 21;
  - `[bda-table]` hits 4,250 / misses 469.
- **`[resources]` phase totals, per build over the block:** stage B ~690 µs, of which images ~600 µs.
- **`[image-table]`:** 4,653 snapshots, 19,066 tables, 51,037,285 keys, 160 ms.

#12745's index proves cached builds only. Its own §7 item 6 (`results/cloud-2/BF-041.md`) leaves out compute and BDA objects: "re-binding compute and BDA objects, which needs a fresh set per dispatch".

## Do (read only, no code)

Base: upstream `main`, plus #12745's head (`pull/12745/head`, 6a930fc672 or newer).

1. **Why these draws are uncacheable:** the path that marks them, with `file:line`, and what a cached BDA build would have to keep.
2. **Per-build phases:** for imports pass, snapshots, images and stage B, what each does, and which parts are proofs of unchanged state rather than real work. Give the `file:line` of each.
3. **What an index over BDA objects would need:**
   - what a BDA build depends on: the BDA table, the address space and its mirrors, imports, and image tables reached through addresses;
   - which write-tracker blocks and bump sources would cover each dependency;
   - **GPU stores through BDA addresses:** how they reach the tracker, which pending writes apply, unwatched or compared imports, and aliasing;
   - whether each draw needs a fresh descriptor set, and what that costs.
4. **Which proofs it replaces,** with an upper bound on the per-draw saving from the counters above. Say what stays, as BF-041 did for cached builds.
5. **Risks:**
   - correctness: every writer path, as in BF-230;
   - memory per subscription;
   - locking: the index mutex inside the tracker mutex;
   - Windows `GetWriteWatch`;
   - interaction with the draw finisher and the dispatch path.
6. **Overlaps:** a claim check.
   - Open PRs in address-based builds, BDA tables, image mirrors or `ShaderResources` reuse. Include our #12840 (BDA store writable mirror), #13251 and #12921, and others' #10886, #11444, #9012 and #9899.
   - PRs closed since 10-16.
   - fable-void's fork `wip/new-gpu-path`.
7. **The exact change,** as PR-sized steps. For each step:
   - what it changes;
   - its red test;
   - its mechanism bar on the Village counters above (which line moves, and by how much);
   - what it depends on.
