## Why

JJenkx found this on our #9533 (https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9533#issuecomment-6336152420). The PM decided, in the user's name, to take it as a new commit on top of #9533's next head **514643ab16** (fork `fix/agc-msaa-d32s8-separate-stencil-review`).

**The bug:**
- In `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Packets/PacketMemory.cpp`, `Driver::enqueueDmaPacket` (L293-312 on 514643ab16) queues a large DMA_DATA fill on the draw pipeline. That's opcode 0x50, queue 0, source 2, more than 65536 bytes, not to GDS, and it needs `APS5_PIPELINE_DMA` and `DrawPipeline::Active()`.
- Its lambda calls `storeBufferFill(queue, fillAddress, fillBytes, {pattern×4}, localDevice, false, false)` at **L307**. The last argument is `noteMetadata`.
- Because it's `false`, the queued fill skips the depth-metadata note that the synchronous path gets through `preparePacketMemory`.
- A later sample of the native multisample depth target then refuses with `AGC graphics: external GPU writes over native multisample depth are not implemented`.
- **JJenkx's fix:** change only that last argument to `true` on the queued path, and leave the synchronous call at L198 alone. Their four cases passed on RX 6650 XT/RADV with validation on: 4-sample D32S8, compressed and uncompressed HTILE, clear values 0 and 1, three 131072-byte refills each. The path was `sceAgcDriverSubmitDcb -> enqueueDmaPacket -> storeBufferFill -> vkCmdFillBuffer`.
- Their fixture is private, so we write our own.

**Why the existing test misses it:** `tests/execution/NativeMultisampleDepth.cpp`'s `ClearSource::QueuedFill` case (around L829-835) calls `QueueTargetFill(...)` and then `NoteDepthMetadataFill(...)` itself. It never goes through `enqueueDmaPacket`.
