# BF-131: follow a conditional pointer chase through a V# behind a pointer-keyed record pointer (closure entries), stacked on #9457
from: Bug Finder
for: any
priority: normal

## Why

#8939 (ours, draft) takes lane-mask arms and a merged high word in a pointer-keyed record pointer. #9457 (ours, draft, stacked on #8939, upstream `refs/pull/9457/head`, head **c00cb0f875** at posting; use its latest and say which) follows pointer hops behind such a record pointer.

A tester's retail run of Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart (PPSA01474) on #8939 gets past its old stop. It now stops at compute `LoadAddressU32@pc=0x10b84`. The tester replayed the shader with `APS5_DUMP_IR` through `agc_shader_replay`, which reproduces the refusal exactly. They traced the walk in ResourceTracker.cpp. Their reading, which is all the evidence there is (no shader dump comes with this task):

- **#8939's edge pairing holds:** every (low, high) pair the walk pops is consistent.
- **The final pointer is a Phi of three sources:**
  - **R:** the record pair `ReadConstBuffer@0x1aa8`, dwords 2/3 (offsets 8/0xc) of a 16-byte record, stride 16 (`ReadFirstLane(...) << 4`). It is accepted first and fixes `lowReads`.
  - **H1:** `ReadConstBuffer@0xfd94` at offsets 0/4, both words from one read, through a V# loaded at `@0xfd88` from offsets 0x60..0x6c of a pointer that is a Phi of R.
  - **H2:** `ReadConstBuffer@0x10350` at offsets 0/4, the same shape, through a V# loaded at `@0x10338` from pointer+0x60, where that pointer is a Phi of R and H1.
- **What the shader does:** it chases the pointer conditionally up to twice, P -> V# at P+0x60 -> dwords 0/1 of that buffer -> next P. It then loads the T# at the final P+0. So the final pointer is R, H(R) or H(H(R)).
- **Lane-mask and immediate words** are skipped as intended. One join has its low and high Phis in different blocks, and there the walk falls back to unpaired.
- **Where it refuses:** H1 and H2 fail `PointerRecordRead` (ResourceTracker.cpp:891-899 at c00cb0f875). Their offset is 0 against the record's 8, and their buffer differs, so `SameRecordColumn` fails and `TryMakeIndirectColumn` refuses.
- **Why #9457's hops don't cover it:** its hop loop (`TryMakeIndirectColumn`, :964-990) walks outward only while each word is a direct `GetAddressResource` read at an immediate offset of the next pointer. A Phi at the descriptor pointer stops it at the first step, and these hops go through a V# (4 words at P+0x60, then a buffer read at 0/4), not a plain address load.

## What to build

1. **Tracker: chase arms.** In the pointer-keyed walk (`PointerRecordPairs` and its callers), recognise an arm whose low and high words are a buffer read at offsets 0 and 4 of one buffer handle. That handle's V# must be the 4 words loaded through `GetAddressResource(P)` at an immediate offset (0x60 here; keep it general), where P resolves to the record pointer R or to another chase arm.
   - Record each distinct chase site as a hop of a new kind, {V# offset, read offset}.
   - The depth is the number of distinct chase sites on a path. Refuse a chase that reaches itself through a Phi cycle (a loop, unbounded), and cap the depth at a small constant; 2 is needed here, so pick a cap like 4.
   - A merged pointer whose arms are R and chase arms of R becomes one pointer-keyed column with the chase hops attached. #9457's buffer-handle refusal stays as it is.
2. **Materializer: closure entries.** For each record's pointer R, also publish H(R), H(H(R)) and so on up to the column's depth as pointer-keyed entries, each with its T# at P+0 (the column's offset).
   - H(P) is computed like this: read the 4-word V# at P + V# offset, take its base address (V# dwords 0/1, as the driver decodes a V# base), then read the 2 words at base + read offset.
   - Reads outside the snapshot fault "outside the snapshot", as #9457's hops do.
   - Entries that repeat a pointer are kept once.
   - Keep the disk-cache format consistent: bump it if the plan serialisation changes.
3. **Debt entry** (`docs/dev/debt/...`, where #8939 and #9457 list theirs): the depth cap, and loops refused.
