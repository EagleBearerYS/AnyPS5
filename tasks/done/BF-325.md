## Why

Address-based (BDA) draws are the draw committer's largest cost at Resident Evil Village's menu: 4,276 per 10 s at 1,126 µs each, 4.8 s of its 6.95 s. The designs this task implements are:
- **BF-322** (`results/cloud-3/BF-322.md`): S1 and S2;
- **BF-323** (`results/cloud-5/BF-323.md`): S2m, and why it's exact.

**What changed since we closed #6636 (10-21):**
- **Why we closed it:** #6636 reused 0 of 17,613 draw builds at the menu (q6636p). BF-105 showed why: 2,185 of 2,295 proofs failed on snapshot **addresses**, the per-draw constant rings (0x50/0x70 bytes) in 0x230200000+0x30100000 and 0x260400000+0x1a300000.
- **What BF-323 found since:** those snapshots lie inside the build's address space and are **read live**. All 1,235 of qbf105's sampled `[snapshots]` refusals say "(read live) … the build reads it through its address space", and 0 say copied. Their bytes reach the shader only through data words (`FlattenedSrt`, `ShaderData`, push constants), never through table entries.
- **So** a reused build can take them per use through the existing `MovedReadOnlyBuffers` → `PrepareDrawBindings` → `AllocateRewritten` path.
