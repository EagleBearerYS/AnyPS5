What it does:
- **Before:** on Windows, #6060 and the 1 MiB settle floor never settled host imports under 1 MiB. They left the write watch, so every texture over them was compared on each use. Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) maps its GPU heap in 64 KiB pieces.
- **Now:** such an import stays compared until a batched read on the settle queue completes, then `Rewatch` returns its blocks to the write watch after the fence. `SetHostImportDeferredSettles` switches it in-process; `drainSettles` runs on a device change.
- **The debt line it implements** (`docs/dev/debt/libSceAgcDriver.md`, the "Settling without blocking the importing thread" sentence).

**Known and owned elsewhere** (not part of this review):
- In the first scene, `Rewatch` stamps every block of a settled import as a CPU store and bumps the unwatch serial, so derived data is re-uploaded under import churn. The Bug Finder is profiling it (bf-settle) for a follow-up.
- The overlaps with #5905, #6457, #6479, #6472 and #6499 are in the description.
