## What to build (new branch feat/trophy-unlock-popup, on a merge of the two bases)
- **When it shows:** when the trophy listener reports an unlock, show a small pop-up for about 5 seconds, drawn in the presentation pass with the overlay's existing drawing code: rectangles and the self-written bitmap font, adding glyphs for the needed lowercase or symbols if the font lacks them.
- **Contents:** "TROPHY UNLOCKED", the trophy's name if known (PM-017 will provide names; until then show the id), and its grade if known.
- **Placement:** a corner, the opposite one from the overlay's by default; `ANYPS5_TROPHY_POPUP=0` turns it off.
- **Brightness:** fixed and moderate on HDR10, as the overlay does.
- **Several unlocks at once** queue, one after another.
- **Cost:** none when no pop-up is showing. No commands, the same rule as the hidden overlay.
- **Separate from the overlay:** it works whether or not the performance overlay is shown.
