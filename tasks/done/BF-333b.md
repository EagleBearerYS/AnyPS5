## Why

BF-333 (`results/cloud-3/BF-333.md`) built "a window with no `drawHit || verifyHit` counts as low" on eaafdf934b. It stopped correctly, because the rule breaks `StageHitsKeepTheCacheLookedUp`: 103 draws whose keys miss but whose stages are reused through data candidates. Those draws are real reuse.

**The distinction both that case and Village need**, read at fork `prm/12478-test-cost` eaafdf934b:
- **Data candidates** (`dataCandidates[i]`) are filled in `Execution/src/Driver/Draw/DrawLookup.cpp` ~240-262. They're only stage variants of a same-shape cached draw that `validateVariant` found `Equal`, with equal user words in their used positions.
- **A candidate means reuse.** In `Draw.cpp` ~924-930, a candidate with the matching `pushOffset` is reused (`reused[i] = true`), unless `verifyDrawDataHits()` is set. So a lookup with candidates is real reuse.
- **`drawKeySeen(drawKey)` is only a sighting:** the key was seen before, with no reuse yet. It's what keeps a repeated scene looked up while it warms up (c7981bcb3b).
- **In Village's menu,** all ~5,000 lookups per 10 s have no entry, there are 0 hits, and inserts evict at the 256 MiB budget. Whether its "useful" lookups come from sightings or from candidates isn't in the profiled lines.

**The rule for this task:**
- a lookup is a **real** use if `drawHit || verifyHit || any dataCandidates[i] non-empty`;
- a window is **low** if `usefulLookups < minimumHits` (today), **or** it had no real use. Sightings alone no longer keep it out of low.
- So, unlike BF-333, a data-candidate reuse counts as real. BF-333 found that `dataEntry != nullptr` would also keep every case green, but it's broader than a reuse: a same-shape entry can exist with no `Equal` candidate. Use the candidates.
