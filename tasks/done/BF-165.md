## Why

BF-164 (cloud-3, results/cloud-3/BF-164.md) extended #5640's framer on fork `feat/relinker-frame-frameless-leaves-next` (head **a665ef2dfb**):
- the per-leaf shift (deepest below-rsp slot, rounded to 16; 0x80 for `lea` below rsp or indexed operands);
- leaves that allocate with `sub` after their pushes.

Its evidence is structural only, and it left two gaps:
1. **The single-step execution test (its step 2) was not written.** Nothing yet shows that a framed leaf survives a write below `rsp` at run time.
2. **Its zlib sample is still skipped.** `inflate_fast` and `inflate_table` (clang 18 -O2) now pass the prologue and exit checks but are still skipped with "rsp operand that cannot move with rsp". The cause is 8-bit displacements past 0x7f after the shift, and `(%rsp)` without a displacement. In its mixed sample, 7 of the 9 remaining "cannot move" skips are disp8 overflow at shift 0x80.

Grand Theft Auto V (PPSA04264)'s `inflate_fast` is the target (#9136, #361). tibzejoker, who tests on GTA V, may hit the same operand forms.

## Do (on `feat/relinker-frame-frameless-leaves-next`, new commits on a665ef2dfb; don't rewrite it)

1. **Execution test:** `test(relinker): ...`, as BF-164's task described. Linux x86-64 only; guard it so it is skipped or not built elsewhere.
   - **What it runs:** synthetic leaves are framed by `FrameRedZoneFunctions`. Their sites and displacements are materialised the way `Conversion.cpp` writes them, in an executable buffer that holds both the code and its stubs.
   - **How it clobbers:** under single-step (`EFLAGS.TF`), a `SIGTRAP` handler fills `[rsp-0x80, rsp)` of the interrupted context with a pattern at every instruction, as Windows' exception dispatch does.
   - **What it requires:**
     - each unframed leaf returns a wrong result under the clobber (the red control) and the right result without it;
     - each framed leaf returns the right result under the clobber.
   - **Cases:** #5640's frameless shape; a depth-shifted leaf; a positive disp8 moved in place; the `lea`-below case at 0x80; the `inflate_fast`-shaped `sub` leaf from `framesLeavesThatAllocateBelowTheirPushes`. Each must do real work, such as a loop over its spilled slots, so that a clobbered slot changes the result.
   - Run `--repeat until-fail:20`.
2. **Stub sites for operands that can't move in place:** `feat(relinker): ...`.
   - **Which operands:** in a leaf that is otherwise framable, an rsp operand that can't keep its encoding after the shift:
     - a disp8 that overflows;
     - `(%rsp)` with no displacement (mod 0, base rsp);
     - `(%rsp,%idx,s)` forms.
   - **What happens to them:** instead of skipping the leaf, the instruction moves to a stub, re-encoded with a disp32 (or disp8 when it now fits) moved by the shift, followed by a jump back. This is the same `TrampolineSite` machinery the prologue and exit stubs and `--to-intel`'s trampolines use.
   - **The site:** it covers that instruction plus following instructions until it is at least 5 bytes.
   - **Site rules:**
     - no branch target inside the site except its first byte;
     - no branch, `ret`, or instruction with a rip-relative operand among the covered instructions, unless you relocate rip-relative displacements for the stub address. Say which you chose;
     - no overlap with another site, including the prologue and exit sites.
   - **Re-encoding:** handle legacy, REX and VEX forms. EVEX stays refused.
   - **When no site fits,** the leaf is skipped with a new reason that names the cause (for example "rsp operand that cannot move and has no room for a stub").
   - **Tests:**
     - structural: the stub bytes for disp8 overflow, `(%rsp)`, and a VEX `vmovups` to `(%rsp)`;
     - execution: an `inflate_fast`-shaped leaf whose locals include `(%rsp)` and `0x60(%rsp)` with a 0x20 shift, plus a whole-0x80 leaf with `0x8(%rsp)`. Each is red unframed and green framed under the clobber.
3. **The framer's writers in `GamePatch::CheckOverlaps`:** `fix(relinker): ...`. BF-164 noted that main's check sees `--to-intel` trampolines and relinker patches but not the framer's stub sites or moved displacements. Pass them in, so that a game patch inside a framed leaf is refused like any other overlap. Add a test.
4. **Counts:** framed and skipped counts on BF-164's zlib and mixed samples, before and after step 2. Say whether `inflate_fast` and `inflate_table` are framed now.
5. **Docs:** update the debt entry and the `USAGE.md` row for the stub sites.
