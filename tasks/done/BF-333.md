## Why

Our #12478 (head 167947744a) bypasses the draw cache while fewer than 5 % of its lookups are useful.

**What Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556)'s profiled menu shows:**
- **The cache never hits.** Over 60 blocks of 10 s: 0 hits in every block, ~290 k lookups, 255 of 256 MiB held, and nearly every insert evicts.
- **The bypass never engaged in that window.** It engaged 12 times, all during loading.
- **Why it stays off:** the path calls `drawCacheBypass.Note(drawHit || verifyHit || any dataCandidates non-empty || drawKeySeen(drawKey))` (`Execution/src/Driver/Draw/Draw.cpp` ~660). In this menu the draw keys repeat, so `drawKeySeen` holds or a candidate variant exists, but no candidate ever matches: the per-draw data words move on every draw. So "useful" stays above 5 %, and the low-window streak never starts.

**The change the PR's owner (AnyPS5 PR Manager) asked for:** a window with no real hit (`drawHit || verifyHit`) among its lookups counts as low, even when sightings or candidates made its lookups "useful". A window with any real hit keeps today's rule.
