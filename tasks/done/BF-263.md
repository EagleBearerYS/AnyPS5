## Why

BF-262's line ran on the PC on Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556), static menu, fork `diag/agc-collect-failures-v4` 34ca984534 (qd262).
- **What fails:** about 600 fast proofs per 10 s fail their collect as `outside`, and each falls back to a full walk of about 3.2 ms. Over minutes 10-15 of the menu that is about 58 s of 300 s of wall time.
- **Why the collect fails:** every one of those ranges was unwatched by a host import (`store-unwatched 0`, `host-region 0`, `never-watched 0`, `other 0`). Two kinds of range:
  - `import-unwatched`: `ImportWatched` without stays-watched (`0x20ff800000`, `0x20ffc00000` and `0x2100000000`, each `+0x10000`);
  - `unwatched`: the re-`Unwatch` in `refreshImports`' check lambda (`GuestBufferMemory.cpp:2354` at 34ca984534, the symbolized `+0x32954d`).
  - The same run logs `host imports under 1024 KiB: … 18067 kept compared under a storage image`: `rewatchSettledImports` skips the re-watch while `StorageTexture::AnyLiveOverlaps`.
- **Main's full walk already trusts a key that holds here:** `[textures] untracked snapshots 355890 kept by the write-watch hint, 9 compared`. In the menu, `forgets` don't move (15,099 at both of the last two lines).
- **Open PR #6192** (TheAggie, stacked on their #6187, both conflicting) makes `fastRevalidate` accept an untracked surface by `untrackedRangeStamp` = {collect epoch, `NewestStamp`, `ForgetStamp`, recorder write note}.
  - Village advances the collect epoch about 80 times a second (798, 800 and 795 per 10 s), with under one collect-failing proof per epoch. So that stamp would expire before almost every proof.
  - The maintainer side (oneandonlydean, 2026-10-25, on #6192) said the epoch scope is deliberate, because a CPU write to untracked memory can become visible after any ordering point. They also said "A variant that keeps the proof when the surface's own stamps haven't moved is worth measuring."
  - This task builds that variant on top of #6192, keeping its commits and its author.

     - State the race argument in the result.
