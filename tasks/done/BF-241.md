## Source and behaviour
- **Bindings, edges, repeats, priority order and pages:** shadPS4 **30f4170896**, `src/core/libraries/ime/ime_ui_shared.cpp:805-887`, `ime_dialog_ui.cpp` and `ime_kb_layout.cpp`, as BF-240 §0 and §4 list them.
  - Cross activates the selected key, armed only after a release.
  - R2 is Done.
  - Square is backspace with repeat, and L1+Square clears in either order.
  - Triangle is space with repeat, but not while L2 is held.
  - L1/R1 move the caret with repeat.
  - L2 cycles lower → upper (one-shot) → caps lock. It's a press edge, re-armed at analog ≤ 0.20, and disabled on the Symbols page.
  - L2+Triangle toggles Symbols, in either order.
  - L3 toggles Specials, but not on Symbols.
  - Circle cancels.
  - The D-pad and left stick move on the grid and wrap (stick deadzone 0.24, trigger 0.50).
  - ▼/▲ move the caret to the end or start.
- **Pages:** Latin lower and upper, Symbols 1 and 2, Specials lower and upper, exactly as BF-240 §4 prints them from the source's `KEY(...)` tables.
  - The locale currency cell follows the source.
  - The Done label comes from `Param::enter_label`.
  - Leave the ⌨, "..." and R3 cells empty.
  - Leave out R3's mode, the right-stick drag, the prediction pane, the top ✕ panel and copy/paste.
- **Repeat timing:** the source uses ImGui's default repeat delay and rate. Use ImGui's documented defaults (`KeyRepeatDelay` 0.275 s, `KeyRepeatRate` 0.050 s), and name them as such in the debt entry.

## Look
Plain and legible; no animations, gradients or fades.
- **Panel:** centred in the bottom half of the frame.
- **Text field:** the dialog's current text, with a visible caret bar.
- **Key grid:** 10 columns. Each key shows its label, and the selected key has a clear border.
- **Button-hint badges** on the keys the source badges: L2, L2+△, L3, △, □, L1, R1, R2.
- **Size:** a glyph pixel should be large enough to read at 1280×720 and 1920×1080. Scale with the frame height, e.g. about 3 screen pixels per glyph pixel at 1080p.
- **Palette:** high contrast in both sRGB and PQ output, through the existing `OverlayPalette` conversion.
- **Glyphs:** a 5×9 cell (two rows above for diacritics), hand-made in the repo. They cover printable ASCII, every non-ASCII character on the source's pages, the shift, backspace and arrow symbols, and a fallback box for anything else, such as CJK in pre-filled text.
  - No third-party font data: AGENTS.md would make that a submodule.
  - Nothing may reach the performance overlay font's throw.

## Tests, as BF-240 §6
- **The pad keyboard model:** a table of edge sequences → actions. It covers:
  - every binding above;
  - the priority order;
  - L2's cycle and its one-shot upper case;
  - L2+△ and L1+□ in both orders;
  - D-pad wrapping;
  - seeding: Cross, □ and the D-pad held at open give nothing until released.
- **The hand-off:**
  - while the dialog runs, `scePadReadState` and the `scePadRead` queue are neutral, with Intercepted and still connected, while the dialog's text changes;
  - after R2, the game doesn't see R2 until it's released;
  - after Term, ForceClose or Abort, the capture is released and real input flows.
- **Guest API:**
  - typing then R2: Finished, endstatus 0, the text and a NUL in the buffer, the cap kept without splitting a surrogate pair;
  - Circle: endstatus 1, buffer unchanged;
  - Abort: endstatus 2;
  - Term and ForceClose mid-entry: back to None, capture off;
  - mixed host-keyboard and pad input gives one text.
- **Overlay layout and glyph coverage,** as in commit 5.
