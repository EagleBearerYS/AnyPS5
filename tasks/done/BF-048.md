## Why

The AGC redesign is approved (`pr-drafts/arch/redesign.md`, "Lanes"; the user, 2026-10-16 21:54 PT). Lane A moves texture binding to run time:
- **Shaders:** read their T#/S# from guest memory and look them up in a device image table that indexes one descriptor heap, bound once per batch.
- **The CPU:** stops resolving texture tables per draw. Resident Evil Village's menu scans ~5,800 image-table keys per draw today.

**This task is the driver half,** ported from fable-void's `wip/new-gpu-path` (head ed8f2d7a2e) onto current upstream main, next to today's path:
- a new switch, `APS5_GPU_PATH`, off by default;
- with the switch off, main is unchanged;
- the CPU path stays the fallback per program.

**The shader half is BF-049,** in the recompiler, in parallel. The two meet at the table's ABI (`core/shader/recompiler/.../DeviceImageTableAbi.hpp` on his branch): agree on it through the result files, or take his ABI as is.

His branch: `git fetch https://github.com/fable-void/AnyPS5.git wip/new-gpu-path`. **Fetching, building and porting his branch needs the user's OK in your own chat.** The PM is asking for it. Don't start without it.

## What to port

- **Descriptor heap:** 429a168dc5, e48ea473d0 (`Graphics/src/DescriptorHeap.cpp`). An update-after-bind heap.
- **Device image table:** fa070261ae, d35b01a5b8, 81119eea49, 1ffc203905, a0080950fe, 91a822cd0e (`Graphics/src/DeviceImageTable.cpp`, `DeviceImageKeys`):
  - a GPU-hashed 15-word key, with up to 16 probes;
  - views made on a worker pool;
  - the miss ring for unnamed keys.
- **The per-entry check:** 0171a4ac80, so only entries a draw names are checked.
- **Their tests** from his branch, adapted to main.

They lean on his residency and pre-pass code. Port the minimum each needs, and list what you stubbed or replaced with main's equivalents. For example: main's texture cache supplies the images, and his `ExpectedDeviceImages` pre-pass is replaced by a CPU pass over the T# table only when the table's memory changed (main's write tracking).
