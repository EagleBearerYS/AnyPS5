from: Bug Finder
for: any

## Why

Both were addenda to BF-024, posted after it was claimed, so BF-024's result doesn't have them. Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) needs both for its next profiled look:
- **Revalidate:** at the menu the resource cache's Revalidate runs ~447 times per second at ~362 µs per call. That is 8.1 s over 50 s of a profiled run, about 16% of queue 0. About 11% of the calls take the full walk, almost all for `no record`. The line gives one total time, so it can't say whether the full walks are the cost.
- **Non-vertex path:** non-vertex draws are ~47% of the title's CPU-path indirect packets at the menu and ~70% in the first scene. The `[draws]` line and `APS5_TRACE_INDIRECT_DRAWS` don't say which kind they are, and that decides which half of stage 1 to build next.

## What to build

On fork branch `exp/village-fast5-diagnostics`, from `exp/village-fast5-draw-parameters` a38c704ea, two commits:
1. **Revalidate time by outcome.**
   - `countRevalidate` in `Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp` (~:2462) adds every call's time to one `nanoseconds` total. Keep three more totals: calls that passed fast, full walks that passed, and failed calls.
   - A fast pass that fails and then takes the full walk counts as "full". If the code makes that hard, count fast-then-full apart.
   - Print them after the counts on the `[rescache] revalidate` line, as `time: fast X ms, full Y ms, failed Z ms`.
2. **Non-vertex indirect draws by kind.**
   - Where an indirect draw gets `Path::NonVertexPath` (`ClassifyIndirectDraw` or its caller on this tree), count it by what made it non-vertex: mesh/NGG front stage, tessellation, geometry shader, rect list or another primitive type the vertex path refuses, or other (name the condition).
   - Print it right after `non-vertex path K` in the `[draws]` line, for example `(mesh a, tessellation b, geometry c, rect list d, other e)`.
