# BF-249: draw-cache bypass follow-up: count stage hits in the window, and pack the window counters into one CAS

## Why

Our draft-to-be PR "perf(agc): bypass the draw cache while its hit rate is under 5%" is fork branch `perf/agc-draw-cache-low-hit-bypass-main` = `3e4d8e25f2` on upstream main `04fefebe90`. It ports bcortezf's closed #5404 (they are co-author).

oneandonlydean reviewed #5404 on 2026-10-17 (https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/5404#issuecomment-6243432597) and asked for two things this head doesn't do yet:
1. **Stage hits.** `useDrawEntries` also gates `stageEntries` (`Draw.cpp`, `tail.stageEntries = useDrawEntries && …`). A bypassed draw therefore also skips stage reuse, but the window counts only whole-draw hits: `drawCacheBypass.Note(drawHit || verifyHit)` after `lookupDraw`. A scene whose whole-draw keys miss while its stages hit would be bypassed and lose the stage reuse that works.
   - **His options:** measure what the bypass switches off (whole-draw plus stage hits), or bypass only the whole-draw lookup and insert.
   - **Take the first one:** a lookup counts as a hit for the window when the whole draw hit, or when at least one of its stages was reused from the cache (the stage hits the `[draw-cache]` line reports as "stage hits by variant rank"). Find in `lookupDraw` / `matched` where a stage hit is known.
2. **The race.** `DrawCacheBypass::Note` uses `fetch_add` on `lookups` and `hits`, then `exchange` and `store`, which can race between queue workers. Pack the window's lookups and hits into one 64-bit atomic and update it with one compare-exchange loop, so a window closes exactly once. `left` may stay a separate atomic.
