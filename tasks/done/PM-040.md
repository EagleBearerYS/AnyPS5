#6060 ("keep Windows host imports of write-watched private memory watched") merged into upstream main at 01:54 PT, 2026-10-15. Writable private imports of 1 MiB or more now stay watched on Windows when the probe passes, and they're vouched through stamps with no compare.

PM-037 (results/cloud-2/PM-037.md §1, gap 1) found the hole that this exposes on main: **`MarkWritten` and `storeOwn` stamp nothing for a store over a partly watched range** (GuestMemory.cpp:1433-1436 and 1388-1391 at the time). So every write-watch consumer can read replaced bytes as unchanged:
- snapshot `UnchangedSince`;
- BDA table keys;
- `StorageTexture::blocksKept` and `evictStale`.

The fix exists on fork `followup/pm028-partly-watched-stamps`: 89343ba7f3 (`fix(agc): stamp the watched blocks of a partly watched store`, GuestMemory.cpp +19) and b7c33252c6 (`test(agc): check the stamps of a store into partly watched memory`).
