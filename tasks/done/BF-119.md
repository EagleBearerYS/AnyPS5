## Why

Four titles stop on one planner family (issue #6191).

**Gran Turismo 7 (PPSA01317).** frankea, https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/issues/6191#issuecomment-6322547617. 28 of the World Map's 29 refused compute shaders stop here.
- **The refusal:** `GetImageResource dword 0 is not a valid runtime value; chain: LoadAddressU32@pc=0x00002404(GetAddressResource(LoadAddressU32@pc=0x000020ec(GetAddressResource(Phi(Phi(ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x00000160(GetBufferResource(ReadConst, ReadConst, ReadConst, ReadConst), ShiftLeftLogical32(ReadFirstLane, Immediate)), CompositeExtractU32x4(Ballot(LogicalAnd), Immediate)), ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x00000160(...)), ...`
- **The shape:**
  - a record `s_buffer_load_dwordx4` from an SRT V#, keyed `ReadFirstLane(...) << 4` in a waterfall loop;
  - record dwords 2-3 are a pointer P1. Its words are Phis of the record read under several keys plus a `Ballot` lane-mask word;
  - P2 = `s_load_dwordx2 [P1 + 0]`, the pointer hop;
  - T# = `s_load_dwordx8 [P2 + 0x340]`.
- **Why it refuses:** in #8939's `TryMakeIndirectColumn` (`core/shader/recompiler/Optimization/src/ResourceTracker.cpp`, ~line 991 at 8704f73b79), `if (!hops.empty() && (buffer || merged)) return false;` refuses any hop once the record pointer is merged (pointer-keyed).
- **Why the rest should already work:** everything after the guard appears to support it.
  - `ResourceMaterializer::ReadColumn` stores `records.pointers[record]` before it applies `column.pointerHops`. So the snapshot is keyed by P1, and the T# is read after the hops.
  - `plan.parentPointer = pointer` is the innermost (record-level) pointer, which the SPIR-V `LookupTablePointer` compares against.
  - `ShaderDiskCache`'s check (`column.pointerKeyed && (!column.indirect || column.buffer || parent)`) doesn't forbid hops.

**Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (PPSA03016 / PPSA08338).** hugodearaujo, https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/8939#issuecomment-6326819824.
- **The refusal:** `ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x8560(GetBufferResource(LoadAddressU32@pc=0x854c(GetAddressResource(ReadConstBuffer@pc=0x1fc4, …`
- **The shape:**
  - a record from an SRT V#, keyed by `readfirstlane << 4`;
  - dwords 2-3 are a pointer;
  - a V# loaded at that pointer + an immediate;
  - the T# read at a **fixed** offset of that V#'s buffer, `ReadConstBuffer(V#, 0x0)`, with no second key.
- **Why it refuses:** `TryMakeTableColumn` refuses because `MatchAffineOffset` fails on the immediate offset. The existing fixture `agc_driver_pointer_vsharp_select_table` reads its T# under a second key, which is why it passes.
- **hugodearaujo's diff (on a7d1e62b53's `TryMakeTableColumn`).** A fixed offset under a pointer-loaded V# becomes a one-key child: key 0, stride the descriptor size, addend the immediate, on the nested path only. The replay gets past 0x8560.
