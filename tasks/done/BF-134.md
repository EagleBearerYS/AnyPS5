# BF-134: #9533 after BF-130: prove a drawn-over stencil plane by its later fill, fix the D32S8 shadow barrier, then a stencil-aspect sampled view as a follow-up

## Why

BF-130 (`results/cloud-3/BF-130.md`, fork `fix/agc-msaa-d32s8-separate-stencil-review` **8ba6321221**, on #9533's 5daa8cb7a9) makes the D32S8 import take a stencil plane aliased by a host-only R8 colour target from that target's known fill or clear, and refuse otherwise.

A second tester ran #9533's 5daa8cb7a9 on PPSA03208 (2 samples, 1920x2160, D32S8 with depth-only HTILE, stencil plane at 0x1546eb0000). Their tree also carried a local colour-guard retire like #8223's. From their run and #8223's trace of the same title, the sequence is:
1. a 2-sample colour target over the plane's memory is **drawn once**;
2. the D32S8 depth target is bound, and the import runs;
3. a whole-HTILE fill, then a **whole stencil-plane fill**;
4. a compute program samples the surface.

Two consequences:
- **At step 2, BF-130 sees the alias as "drawn" and refuses for good.** The title would stop at BF-130's new refusal, although step 3 proves the plane. That has to change before the head goes to testers.
- **At step 4, the program samples three times.** Two reads are of the depth plane (0x1544ed0000, format 22, 2 samples, 2D MSAA, tile 4, no metadata), and they pass. The third is of the **stencil plane: base 0x1546eb0000, format 5 (R8_UINT)**, 2 samples, 2D MSAA, tile 4, no metadata. Its T# words were `1546eb00 c0500000 021bc1df e1810924 00000000 00700010 00000000 00000000`. `CheckResource` refuses it with `native multisample depth sampled identity or format mismatch`. `SampledAccepts` already knows a stencil view (R8_UINT at `stencilAddress`) on the single-sample path, but the multisampled path never reaches it.

BF-130's validation run also found `VUID-VkImageMemoryBarrier-image-03320` in every D32S8 test. `createSampleShadow` (DepthSurface.cpp:1100) moves a D32S8 sample shadow to GENERAL with the depth aspect only.

## Part A: on BF-130's head, as new commits (branch `fix/agc-msaa-d32s8-separate-stencil-review`)

1. **A drawn or unknown alias leaves the plane unproven, instead of refusing.**
   - At the import, a stencil plane whose alias's newest content is Drawn or Unknown, or whose format or sample count doesn't match, is recorded as unproven. Its host stencil isn't initialised from it.
   - **Proving it:** a later whole-plane fill (#9533's 90d652a1ed path, `NoteDepthMetadataFill` over the plane) proves the plane from the fill byte, with no guest read. If the alias absorbed the same fill, its byte can confirm it. The host stencil aspect is then cleared to that byte, as a proven fill already does.
   - **Refusing:** any use that reads the stencil before a proof refuses. That covers a sample of the target, and a draw with the target bound and a stencil test or stencil write, if the draw path can see that cheaply; otherwise the first sample. The refusal text is BF-130's stencil alias text, and the stderr line names the alias's state.
   - A Fill, FastClear or Clear alias with the matching format and samples keeps BF-130's behaviour: proven at the import from the alias byte.
   - **Tests** (`--aliased-stencil`, extended):
     - an R8 target drawn into over the plane, then the import, then a whole-plane fill of 0x5a, then a sample: imported, and stencil EQUAL 0x5a hits;
     - drawn and no fill, then a sample: refused with the stencil alias text;
     - BF-130's cleared cases unchanged.
     - **Red:** with the change reverted, the drawn-then-filled case refuses at the import.
2. **The sample shadow barrier.** When the shadow's host format has stencil, give its transition to GENERAL `VK_IMAGE_ASPECT_DEPTH_BIT | VK_IMAGE_ASPECT_STENCIL_BIT`. The depth-only copy into the shadow stays as it is.
   - Rerun BF-130's item-3 validation command on the new head, for the D32S8 tests. Show that `VUID-VkImageMemoryBarrier-image-03320` is gone and nothing new appears.

## Part B: the stencil-aspect sampled view, as a follow-up branch stacked on part A

Push it as `feat/agc-msaa-d32s8-stencil-view`, on part A's final head.

- **`CheckResource`:** also accept a T# whose base is the surface's `stencilAddress`, with format 5 (R8_UINT), the surface's extent and samples, 2D MSAA, the target's tile mode and pipe/bank XOR, and no metadata. This is the stencil plane read. Any other format at that base, or metadata, still refuses with the old text.
- **The view:** for that read, give the reader a stencil-aspect view (`VK_IMAGE_ASPECT_STENCIL_BIT`, 2D MS) of the host D32S8 image, or of the sample shadow when the shadow is in use. Check what the shader binding expects for an R8_UINT multisampled T# (a uint sampled image), and that a stencil-aspect view fits it. Say what you found.
- **The shadow:** when a stencil read uses the sample shadow, copy the stencil aspect into it as well as the depth.
- **The unproven plane from part A:** a stencil-plane read of an unproven plane refuses with part A's text.
- **Test:**
  - Draw known stencil reference values into a D32S8 native multisample target, per pixel and ideally per sample.
  - A compute program then reads the stencil plane through an R8_UINT 2D MSAA T# at the stencil address, and the depth plane through the depth T# in the same dispatch, like the title's three reads.
  - Check every sample's value.
  - Use 4 samples on lavapipe, and add the 2-sample case for the PC.
  - **Red:** without the change, the stencil read refuses `native multisample depth sampled identity or format mismatch`.
- **Debt:** update the native depth entry for what's now sampled, and what isn't (stencil through other formats, metadata).
