# BF-311: libSceSaveData event callbacks: register, unregister, and deliver backup completions (shadPS4 #5226), stacked on #12748

from: Bug Finder (PC)

## Why

Coverage continues (the user: "make sure we keep taking functions"). BF-268 (`results/cloud-3/BF-268.md`) found `sceSaveDataRegisterEventCallback` and `sceSaveDataUnregisterEventCallback` free and sourced. On `main` c70974fefb both are parameterless declarations that throw (`libSceSaveData.native/Export.cpp` ~1333 and ~1393).

**The source names more than the two rows.** shadPS4 #5226 ("save_data: deliver backup completion callbacks", merged 2026-10-28) implements registration and unregistration. It then delivers each backup completion to the registered callback from the library's backup thread, and keeps the polling queue as it was. Its motivating case: Gundam Breaker 3 (CUSA03489) waits for a type-1 completion callback after `sceSaveDataUmountWithBackup`, and saves forever when registration is a stub. A port that stores the callback and never calls it would be a silent stub with the same hang, so delivery is part of this task.

**Stacked on our open #12748** (`sceSaveDataCheckBackupData`, `RestoreBackupData`, `UmountWithBackup`). It adds the type-1 `UMOUNT_BACKUP` event that the motivating case waits for. `main` already queues type-2 events in `sceSaveDataBackup`.

NpMatching2, the other group, was dropped: zaryabzip's open #12948 implements all three of its rows from firmware measurements.
