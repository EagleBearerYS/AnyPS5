## NOTE (added by the PC, 10-31 ~08:50 PT): a PS5 measurement for `pthread_getaffinity_np` and `cpuset_*`

rooklz measured the console's libthr on a PS5 on #9156 ([first](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9156#issuecomment-6390891261), [correction](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5/pull/9156#issuecomment-6391607043)). The console's own answers outrank FreeBSD's where they differ:

- sizes below 8 give `ERANGE`, and so do sizes from 24 up. Sizes 17 to 23 weren't measured, so leave them unclaimed in tests.
