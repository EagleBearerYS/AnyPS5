## Context

PRM-021 (`results/cloud-3/PRM-021.md`, section "Found on main while reading") found this by reading main 3f171d03e0:
- Under `APS5_PIPELINE_DISPATCH=1` (opt-in; off by default), an item whose lookup was a **data hit** keeps `snapshots` that point into `dispatch`'s local `liveWords` (Dispatch.cpp:222).
- On a data hit, `lookupDispatch` copies the variant's words into the caller's `liveWords`, patches the data words, and builds the regions over that buffer (DispatchLookup.cpp:122-125). `captured` takes those regions (:137), and `snapshots` are spans over `captured` (Dispatch.cpp:406).
- The queued item's closure captures `snapshots` but not `liveWords` (:447), and `dispatch` returns right after enqueuing it. So the spans dangle.
- The item reads `snapshots` when `PrepareRecipe` misses (template or objects gone, device changed), when `DispatchRecipe` asks for a rebuild, when it falls back to `Dispatch`, or under `APS5_VERIFY_RECIPES`.
- The proposed fix is one line: capture `liveWords = std::move(liveWords)` in the closure at :447. Moving a vector keeps its buffer, so the spans stay valid.
