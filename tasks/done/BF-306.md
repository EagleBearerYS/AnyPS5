## Why

On Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556), C4 (`APS5_DIAG_SKIP_HOOK_WAITS=1 APS5_NO_PRESYNC=1`, BF-295) ran at 0.614 fps against the same binary's control at 0.400: **+54 %**, the highest ceiling tonight. The PM decided in the user's name that the real fix starts now.

**This task is one site:** indirect draw arguments. In the profiled control boot (main ebb4075168 + #12478), per 10 s:
- the largest pending-write sync is `0x0 DRAW_INDEX_INDIRECT indirect-args`: **28 syncs, 760 ms**, on queue 0x0, which is on the critical path;
- `[syncwait]` has indirect-args at 25-29 waits, 188-273 ms.

**BF-295's verdict** (`results/cloud-2/BF-295.md`):
- **Deferrable to the GPU** where the GPU indirect path applies (`Draw.cpp` ~1804 and ~1948 on 41ef8ff856: `vkCmdDraw*Indirect` from the import).
- These syncs are CPU reads of arguments (`Pm4.cpp` ~814, ~886, ~895, ~1001; `VulkanDevice.cpp` ~5122) that a batch still in flight writes.
- BF-287's census: Village's 835 menu draws are all indirect, 483 on the CPU path and 226 on the GPU path.
