## What
BF-217 (`results/cloud-5/BF-217.md`, "Findings") measured that `core/libs/prx/libSceNgs2/PendingExports.cpp` is compiled into libSceNgs2.native under `CXX_VISIBILITY_PRESET hidden` (`libSceNgs2.native/Ngs2Library.cmake:21`) without `#pragma GCC visibility push(default)`. On a Linux build its object marks its functions `GLOBAL HIDDEN`, and `nm -D` on `libSceNgs2.prx` doesn't list them. So, like the unbuilt `Export.cpp` files BF-219 fixed, these exports are counted by `progress.py` but never exported, and a title importing one can't resolve it.

The library's other sources export through `#pragma GCC visibility push(default)` (`src/System.cpp:262`, `src/Rack.cpp:178`, `src/Voice.cpp:398`, `src/Atrac9.cpp:405`). But `check_conventions.py` rejects new `#pragma GCC visibility` lines, which is why BF-219 gave `libSceNgs2.native/Export.cpp` a per-source `-fvisibility=default` instead.
