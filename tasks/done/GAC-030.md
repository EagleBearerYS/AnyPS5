## Addendum 1 (coordinator, 06:50 PT): exactness first, before step 1

The user's rule reached the coordinator after you claimed this. It comes before step 1's fallback:

0. **Exactness first (the user, 2026-10-15: "We want to publish all our fixes. Just making sure we hit the root causes").** An approximation isn't the root-cause fix. Before the fallback in step 1, find the exact or most faithful way to reproduce the console's per-pixel pattern on a device with a 1x1 grid, and build that if it's possible. Weigh at least:
   - **Two passes by row parity:** draw once with even rows' pattern and once with odd rows', each pass covering only its rows. Coverage, depth and sample indices then come from the real rasterizer.
     - How is a pass kept to its rows before depth and stencil writes? A discard is only safe without forced early fragment tests.
     - Cost: twice the vertex work for the affected draws.
   - **Remap the sample index on reads:** with pixel (0,0)'s pattern, every position's coverage and depth are exact, but odd rows store them under swapped indices.
     - Readers that see the index (sample-indexed fetches and loads, FMASK, copies) would swap it back on odd rows.
     - Needs: per-image tracking of the emulated pattern.
   - **Shader-side reconstruction:** conservative rasterization, coverage computed at the console's positions with `gl_SampleMask`, depth exported per sample, attributes interpolated at offsets.
     - Exact, but very costly; say why it is or isn't viable.
   - **Interpolation-only (`InterpolateAtOffset` at the console positions):** exact attributes, but coverage and depth at edges still follow the host pattern.
   - **What the console does:** which consumers read Village's checkerboard targets by sample index, and what the approximation then shows (odd rows' two samples swap, so a checkerboard resolve swaps two full-resolution pixels).
   - **The choice:** pick the most faithful approach that is feasible at an acceptable cost, measured. Ship the pixel-(0,0) fallback only where exact behaviour is impossible on the host, with a debt entry and the reason in the PR. If the exact path is built, the fallback may remain for devices that can't run it, behind the same decision and documented.
