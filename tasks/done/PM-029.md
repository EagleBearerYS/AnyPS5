## Context
- **FSR 1** is built and tested, on fork `feat/agc-fsr1-presentation-upscaling`. EASU runs only when the window is larger than the title's display buffer.
- **The titles present 3840x2160 buffers:** Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) and Animal Well (PPSA22520), so FSR never upscales.
- **PM-015** added `ANYPS5_OUTPUT_RESOLUTION` (fork `feat/videoout-output-resolution`). It changes `sceVideoOutGetResolutionStatus`, `GetOutputStatus(+WithVrr)` and the resolution class.
  - Main already reports 1920x1080 and class HD before registration.
  - Village still registers its display buffers at 3840x2160 with the option at 1280x720 or 2560x1440: "display buffers registered at 3840x2160 although ANYPS5_OUTPUT_RESOLUTION reports ...".
- **Hollow Knight: Voidheart Edition** presents 1920x1080 (#5593), and Unity titles may follow the system resolution.
