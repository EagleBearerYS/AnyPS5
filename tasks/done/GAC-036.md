- **What it means:**
  - The storage image is created (and uploaded) before its memory is imported. The import comes at the draw.
  - The frame's read is the first refresh after that import, and it finds the surface "changed". So the draw's results are dropped, and the CPU's bytes (0x40 from the test's `memset` before the draw) win.
  - Nothing wrote the surface between the draw and the read.

## The suspected cause (to confirm or refute)

The coordinator's reading of main's `GuestMemory.cpp`:
- `ImportWatched` (about line 1325 on 0029e62f97) walks the range's pending write-watch hits **before** the import and stamps them as CPU writes: `before = walkWrites(tracker, first, stop, StampKind::Cpu, true)`.
- On Windows, hits are stamped when a walk reads them, not when the CPU writes. So the test's `memset`, made before the image's upload, may be stamped only at that import walk, with a generation newer than the image's.
- The refresh's `UnchangedSince` then fails, `scanBlocks` marks the unit, and `Texture.cpp`'s drop rule (`guest memory changed while GPU results were pending`) keeps the CPU's blocks.
- On Linux the watch stamps at the write, before the upload, so the order is right.

**If that's the cause, titles hit it too:** a CPU-written surface is uploaded as a render or storage target, imported later at a draw, and its pending GPU results are lost at the next refresh. That would lose rendering on Windows. The fix matters on its own, not only for the DCC test.
