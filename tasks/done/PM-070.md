## Why

PM-069 (results/cloud-4/PM-069.md on cloud-results) found no blocking issue in the output-resolution branch, with one exception. Its finding 1: the refusal also applies to titles that never declare resolution detection, and a console probably never refuses those.
- KytyPS5 at b82512be06 (`VideoOutGetOutputStatus`) reports the lower resolution class only when param.json's `attribute3` has bit 2 (0x4) set. Its comment: "Primary output reports 4K unless param.json Video-out Info enables resolution detection".
- If that is the console's rule, a console set to 1080p tells a title without the bit that the output is 4K, and accepts its 4K buffers.
- Today's branch refuses exactly those titles. Resident Evil Village is one (attribute3 0x8460440, bit 2 clear; PRM-036). PM-069 showed that such a title then stops (`sceVideoOutSubmitFlip`), fails (`sceVideoOutSubmitEopFlip`) or shows black (GPU flips).

The PR will offer the user both versions: A, today's branch, and B, this task. B is the recommended one.
