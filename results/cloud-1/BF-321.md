## 3. The gaps and their bumps

**Gap 7 and 8, the depth bump (commit 1):**
- **New lookup source:** `WriteBumpSource::Depth`, plus `GuestMemory::NoteDepthRegistered(address, bytes)`. `APS5_TABLE_INDEX_BREAK=depth` drops it, and the `[rescache] write index` line counts it.
- **Where it fires:** `DepthSurfaceView` (`DepthSurface.cpp:2214`, :2244 at the head), when it creates a surface or brings one back from retired. It bumps over:
  - the depth plane, with `MultisampledDepthBytes` for multisampled surfaces, so textures they overlap are covered too;
  - the stencil plane;
  - HTILE.
- **When it doesn't fire:** binding an already-registered surface again (checked in the test).
- **Lock order:** it runs under `GpuMutex` and `surfacesMutex`, and the index mutex is a leaf (`bumpSubscriptions` calls nothing back).

**Gap 9, the re-bind rule (commit 1):**
- An element that `reresolveElements` re-binds to a depth owner, a storage view or a DCC surface now joins `perElementTextures` (`ShaderResources.cpp:3775` at the head). That is the class `subscribeIndex` gives such elements.
- Compared elements are excluded: they are re-resolved on every proof anyway. A first version without that exclusion sent table-index `pending (landed)` to fallback on every draw. The exclusion fixed it.

**Gap 2, the released-texture rule (commit 2):**
- `subscribeIndex` gives a texture that is already `Released()` the per-element proof (`ShaderResources.cpp:3606`). That proof fails it `Replaced`, as the ordinary fast proof does.
- Until now only the audit dropped such a template. It isn't a bump, but it is one condition and exact.

**The skip (commit 2):** `if (fast && !indexProved && TemplateAuditDue(lastFullWalk))` (`ShaderResources.cpp:4065`).
- With the index off, a fallback, or a template never subscribed, the audit runs as before.
- The ordinary fast path is unchanged.

**Left as is, for the PC:**
- **A compute or address-based template can't be re-bound in place** (`reresolveElements` :3747 returns false). So when a lookup bump finds a new answer, the index falls back to the ordinary fast proof.
  - Import and release are still caught there: the re-lookup released the bound snapshot, and the ordinary proof fails `Replaced`.
  - **A depth registration under a compute template isn't caught.** The ordinary proof has no depth-at-base check at 6a930fc672 (that's #11444's refusal), so only the audit, still on for fallbacks, catches it.
  - A follow-up could make that fallback a definite failure; I didn't widen the change.
- **Row 18:** representation only, not closed.
