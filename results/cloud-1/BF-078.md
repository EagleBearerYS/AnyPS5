# BF-078: does main's draw-relocation alias map keep evicted draw-cache entries alive? (cloud-1)

**Base:** upstream main **195d0d08a** (2026-10-18 15:01 +1300, "Merge pull request #7334"). Synthetic Village-shaped harness only; no game data.

**Machine:** cloud-1: Intel Xeon @ 2.80 GHz, 4 vCPUs, 15 GiB, Ubuntu 24.04, gcc 13.3.0, wild 0.10.0, lavapipe from Mesa 25.2.8. CI's Linux configure with `-DANYPS5_ENABLE_SPIRV_TOOLS=ON`, Release. Runs as root. heaptrack 1.5.0 (installed from Ubuntu's archive for this task).

RESULT:
- **Yes, main retains evicted entries through relocation aliases, for ordinary GPU-path draws.**
  - **Churn** (8 object sets: 6800 distinct draws a cycle over the 4096-entry cache):
    - Aliases learned at about 75k draws keep **3359 entries alive that the cache has evicted**, and RSS steps from 373.1 to 428.0 MiB (+54.9 MiB).
    - At about 150k draws more are learned. At 175,100 draws, **4607 aliased entries, all outside the cache**, and RSS is **450.6 MiB**, against **375.9 MiB with `APS5_NO_DRAW_RELOCATION=1`** (+74.7 MiB). The second run: 4659 and 450.8 MiB.
    - The cache itself stays at 4096 entries in both.
  - **Churn with relocation off:** no aliases, RSS flat at 371.8–375.9 MiB from 15k draws to the end.
  - **Hit** (`VILLAGE_SETS=4`: 3400 distinct draws, which fit): 3384 entries, no eviction, 639 aliases all holding entries still in the cache, RSS flat at 359.2–360.6 MiB from 15k draws, 95% of lookups hit (162,268 of 170,004).
  - **heaptrack** (churn against churn with relocation off, held at the end of 175,100 draws): **71.33 MiB more held with relocation on.**
    - 48.80 MiB is compiled results' binding contents (`DescriptorBindingBuilder::Populate` from `materializeResult`).
    - 7.74 MiB is `DrawDecode`, and 7.49 MiB is `cacheDrawStages`'s variants.
    - The rest is the result memo, `tagVariantRuns`, `insertDrawEntry` and resource builds.
  - **The holder** is the one BF-074 found: `drawAliases` (DrawRelocate.cpp:171–172) holds a `shared_ptr<DrawEntry>` per alias, up to 16384. The cache's eviction loop and `eraseDrawEntry` remove an entry from `drawCache` and `drawOrder` only.
- **Fix: `fix/agc-draw-cache-release-aliases` at 1a3a4c6b0**, two commits on upstream main 195d0d08a.
  - **ec501c5ee** `feat(agc): count the draw-cache entries relocation aliases hold, and those no longer in the cache`: `Driver::DrawCacheSize()`, and the counts on the `[draw-relocate]` profile line.
  - **1a3a4c6b0** `fix(agc): release a draw-cache entry's relocation aliases when it leaves the cache`:
    - `DrawEntry::aliases` records the aliases learned for an entry.
    - The eviction loop and `eraseDrawEntry` erase the aliases still holding the entry.
    - `insertDrawEntry`'s replacement of an existing key and `lookupDraw`'s variant rotation point them at the new entry.
    - It is BF-074's 0b0862738 applied to every cached draw, not only CPU-path ones.
    - Diff against main: 7 files, +207 −1. The fix commit alone is 6 files, +176, of which the new test is 150 lines.
- **With the fix** (`exp/draw-alias-retention-fixed` 39eee41f8, the harness branch plus the fix commit):
  - **churn:** RSS flat at 372.6 → 378.7 MiB from 15k to 175,100 draws (380.5 in the second run). Aliases are 0 outside the cache at every point. They appear in the two learning bursts (957 and 2476 at 70–76k draws, then 1482, 1731 and 216 at 140–150k), all holding entries in the cache, and fall to 0 as those entries leave.
  - **hit:** flat at 359.6–363.0 MiB, with 629 aliases, all in the cache.
- **What it costs:**
  - **Relocation hits:** 0 in every Village arm before and after (12,288 relocation lookups in churn, ~4,200–5,200 in hit), so the fix loses no hit on this harness. A donor that left the cache can no longer serve a relocated hit; this harness never got one from such a donor.
  - **The `[draw-relocate]` line is not printed here.** It prints only under `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW` once 100,000 relocation lookups are reached, and the harness peaks at 12,288. The footprint line reads the same counters (`drawRelocationCounters.lookups` and `.hits`) cumulatively.
  - **Time per draw** (wall median, two runs each, µs): churn main 393.2 / 383.2 against fixed 379.5 / 372.4; hit main 220.4 / 284.6 against fixed 302.3 / 297.0. Main's own two hit runs differ by 64 µs, so I make no speed claim either way.
- **Test: `agc_driver_draw_cache_aliases`** (tests/execution/DrawCacheAliases.cpp, in the fix commit).
  - 8 data values, each drawn at 4 addresses, through a 4-entry draw cache (`APS5_DRAW_CACHE_ENTRIES=4`). Relocation learns an alias for each value, and the cache evicts the older entries.
  - It checks that entries were evicted, that aliases were learned, that none holds an entry outside the cache, and that the newest value at a fifth address still hits through its alias.
  - **Green** (fix head): "with graphics pipeline libraries: 4 aliases holding 4 entries, 4 entries in the cache, 5 evicted, 14 relocation hits, none outside the cache; the newest data at a new address still hit".
  - **Red** (the four `releaseDrawAliases`/`moveDrawAliases` calls removed, the rest kept): "AGC graphics: with graphics pipeline libraries: 4 draw-cache entries that left the cache are still held by relocation aliases (8 aliases holding 8 entries, 4 entries in the cache, 5 evicted, 14 relocation hits)", rc=1.
- **Checks on the fix:**
  - **CI Linux mirror** (switch unset): **1410/1416**. The 6 failures are this machine's: `guest_datagram_*`×4, `guest_tcp`, `guest_named_shm_concurrency`. The 2 unexpected skips are `guest_sce_net` and `savedata_memory_growth_read_failure`, as on every run here.
  - `check_conventions.py --base upstream/main` passes on all three branches, with 0 added `//` lines.
  - **ec501c5ee alone** builds and passes the 10 `^agc_driver_(draw_cache|mesh_draw_relocation|draw_)` tests.
  - The mirror and red/green ran on 044599c04. That is 1a3a4c6b0 before I corrected one number in its message; the tree is identical.
