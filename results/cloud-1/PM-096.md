## Short version

The mechanism works end to end on lavapipe:
- A 3840x2160 colour target at `APS5_RENDER_SCALE=0.5` renders into a 1920x1080 image. Its texels are **byte-identical** to the same command stream rendered natively into a 1920x1080 target (8,294,400 bytes, FNV `cb5346473b2c3a9d`).
- Demotion back to native works and is logged once, with the reason.
- With the switch unset nothing changes: no surface scales, all counters stay at zero, and the validation layer shows the same VUIDs as main.

**The frank finding:** v0 scales colour only, and refuses any draw that also binds a depth surface. In a real 3D title the main scene pass binds depth, so its 4K targets will demote at the first scene draw. Only depth-less passes (post-processing chains, UI composites) will stay scaled. Expect `DepthAttachment`, and after it `FragmentPosition`, `TexelFetch` and `WriteBack`, to dominate the counts on Planet of Lana, RE Village and Animal Well. Real fps gains need step 2.

## Design note

### Rule (one generic rule, no title special cases)

A surface renders scaled only when **all** of these hold:
- `APS5_RENDER_SCALE` is set to a valid value: `0.5`, `0.75`, any decimal with up to 4 digits, or `N/D` with `0 < N <= D <= 64`. A value equal to 1 means native.
- At least one video-out buffer group is registered. The rule uses the largest registered extent.
- The colour target's extent is at least that video-out extent in **both** dimensions.
- The target is a resident `StorageTexture` that is 2D, single-mip, single-layer and single-sample.
- Its storage format supports `BLIT_SRC|BLIT_DST`.
- It has no attachment proxy.
- It was **never demoted before**. The registry is keyed by address and extent, so demotion is permanent.

A draw renders scaled only if **every** colour target it binds passes that rule, and also:
- it has no depth attachment;
- `state.samples == 1`;
- `APS5_DUMP_TARGETS` is not set;
- its pixel shader does not read the fragment position (`FragCoord`).

Otherwise, every scaled or scalable target of the draw is demoted, with the reason.

Scaled extent: each dimension becomes `max(1, round(length * N / D))`. Depth surfaces are **not** scaled in v0 (see step 2).

### Hooks (files and functions)

**`Graphics/include/RenderScale.hpp`, `Graphics/src/RenderScale.cpp` (new, 247 lines)**
- Switch parsing and the video-out extent.
- `ScaleExtent`, `ScaleViewport`, and `ScaleScissor` (floor/ceil, clamped to the scaled render extent).
- Per-reason demotion counters and the demoted-surface registry.
- `ReadsFragmentPosition`: scans SPIR-V for a `BuiltIn FragCoord` decoration.
- `AgcDriverNoteVideoOutExtent_nid_postfix`.

**`libSceVideoOut/src/Buffer.cpp:65`** (in `CommitBufferGroup`)
- Reports each registered buffer group's width and height to the driver.

**`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp`, in `StorageTexture`**
- `RenderScalable` (:1872), `RenderScaleTo` (:1885), `DemoteRenderScale` (:1893).
- `swapImage` (:1920):
  - creates a new image with the original create info and the new extent;
  - blits the content (linear filter where the format allows it), recorded in the active recorder or in a waited batch;
  - retires the old image and all its views;
  - recreates the default view;
  - bumps `version` and `PendingSerial`.
- Retired images are destroyed in `release()`. That way view handles cannot be recycled while the framebuffer cache can still hold them.
- `ReadImageTexels` (:1903): image readback used by the tests.

**`Graphics/src/Draw.cpp`**
- `renderScaledState` (:2241) runs after the targets are bound (:2415). It does three things:
  - scales the targets;
  - rebinds their attachment views (`rebindTargetViews` :2219);
  - returns a copy of the `State` with `renderExtent`, `viewport` and `scissor` scaled.
- The framebuffer uses the scaled extent (:2616).
- `Draw` passes the scaled state to `recordPreparedDraw`, so `Pipeline::Begin` sets the scaled viewport and scissor as dynamic state. Viewport and scissor are already dynamic, so no new pipelines are needed.
- If resource resolution demotes one of the draw's own targets, for example a feedback storage or texel-fetch read, every target in the draw is demoted and the state falls back to native (:2428).
- While the switch is on, draw recipes are skipped (:3026, and `recipeOut` is not filled), because recipes cache attachment views.

**`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp`**
- Sampled-texture cache entries remember the `VkImage` they view (:939).
- A hit on a swapped image is dropped (:874), and so is a stage-A fast-path record (:4618).
- Sampling a scaled target through **normalized** coordinates therefore uses the scaled image, unchanged.

**`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp`**
- The direct present blit and the dump blit take the resident image's real extent (:3346, :3456, :3478), so a scaled display buffer is blitted to the window as today.
- The converting present path demotes (:3094).

**Recompiler (19 lines)**
- `ImageResource.fetched` and `ImageResource.queried`, set in `ResourceTracker.cpp:1860-1861`.
- The per-element `DescriptorBinding::imageFetched` and `imageQueried`, filled in `DescriptorBindingBuilder.cpp:475-476`.
- Serialized in `ShaderDiskCache.cpp`. `FormatVersion` goes from 44 to 45 and the layout guards are updated.

### Demotion reasons, detection points and counts

| Reason (enum) | What it covers | Detection point(s) | Fired in the tests |

**Known gaps in detection, for step 2:**
- A demotion that fires on a draw's own target **after** its views are bound is handled at :2428. A demotion during recording (after that check) is not handled.
- Texel offsets and `textureGather` offsets in texels are not detected.
- The LOD/derivative change from rendering at a lower resolution is not compensated (no LOD bias).

### Why unset is bit-identical to main

- Every new branch in the code is gated by `ActiveRenderScale()`, which is empty when the switch is unset. The exceptions are gated by `scaledExtent != 0`, which only `RenderScaleTo` can set, and only from a draw that passed the active switch.
- The sampled-cache image check always compares equal when no image is ever swapped.
- The present extent equals the descriptor extent, which `ResidentPresentable` already required to match the buffer.
- The recompiler change adds two metadata flags. It does not change generated SPIR-V.
- The one observable difference: the shader disk cache `FormatVersion` moves from 44 to 45, so existing caches rebuild once. That is a cache format change, not a rendering change.

## Known costs and limits of v0

- **No VRAM saving yet.** A scaled surface keeps its native image as a retired image until the surface is released. Retiring it earlier needs batch-completion destruction and a framebuffer-cache purge.
- **Demotion upsamples** the scaled content with a linear blit. A surface demoted mid-frame loses detail for that frame and keeps it native from then on, and its guest bytes are an upscale, not a native render.
- **Draw recipes are off** while the switch is set, which costs CPU per draw.
- **The pixel position is not rescaled,** so any pixel shader that reads SV_Position demotes. Many fullscreen passes do.

## Step 2: what it would add, ordered by expected payoff

1. **Depth surfaces at the scaled extent.**
   - Where: DepthSurface.cpp, a separate 2.6k-line system. Image creation is at :264/:291. Plane copies, sampled shadows (:959), sample copies (:506), stencil copies (:1027) and plane copies to guest memory (:1203) each need to become scale-aware or a demotion point.
   - Without this, 3D scene passes cannot scale. **It is the gate for any fps gain.**
2. **`FragCoord` rescaling in the recompiler.**
   - Divide SV_Position.xy by the scale through a specialization or push constant, where the pixel builtins are read (Translation/src/InstructionTranslator.cpp:385-418).
   - Then fullscreen passes that compute UV = position / screenSize stay scaled.
3. **Integer coordinates rescaled instead of demoted.**
   - Scale `image_load` coordinates and `resinfo` results per binding (imageLoad, imageGetResinfo in ImageInstructions.cpp:122ff), and unnormalized samplers.
   - The new `fetched` and `queried` flags already say which bindings need it.
4. **Compute.**
   - Scale dispatch grids for passes whose grid matches the surface, or keep demoting (`ComputeWrite`). PS5 post-processing is compute-heavy, so this decides RE Village.
5. **CPU readbacks without demotion:** a temporary native upscale for the write-back, so screenshots and CPU reads keep the surface scaled.
6. **Free the native image** to get the VRAM saving.
7. **Recipes and draw reuse** with scaled views.
8. **LOD bias** of log2(scale) for sampled scaled surfaces, and texel offsets and gathers.
9. **Presentation:** a scale-aware converting path, and FSR on present (PM-004's FSR1 work).

## Go / no-go for step 2

**Go, with a narrow first target and a measurement gate.**

What argues for go:
- The mechanism (swap, rebind, scaled dynamic state, demotion, cache invalidation, present extent) works.
- It is byte-exact against native on lavapipe, and it adds no VUIDs.

But v0 alone will give little or no speedup in the three titles: their main passes bind depth, and their post passes read position or use compute and texel fetches. I'd do step 2 in this order:

1. The user runs this branch on Planet of Lana, RE Village and Animal Well with `APS5_RENDER_SCALE=0.5` and collects the `[render-scale]` lines. That gives the real reason distribution and shows that nothing breaks while everything demotes.
2. Depth scaling plus `FragCoord` rescaling, as step 2a. Estimate: 1-2 weeks, mostly in DepthSurface's copy paths.
3. Only if 2a's counts show compute or texel fetch as the next blocker: integer-coordinate rescaling in the recompiler (step 2b), the riskiest part.

**No-go** on rescaling integer coordinates first. It is the largest recompiler change, and it pays nothing until depth scales.
