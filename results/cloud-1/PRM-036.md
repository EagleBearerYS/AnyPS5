RESULT:
- **Why titles ignore the setting:** the branch changes only what two status calls *report*: `sceVideoOutGetResolutionStatus` and `sceVideoOutGetOutputStatus`'s class. Every other answer a title can act on is the same as on main, and above all **`sceVideoOutRegisterBuffers2` takes any size** (step 3, branch table).
  - The three titles evidently don't size their buffers from the reported status. On main, which reports 1920x1080 and the HD class until buffers are registered, they register 3840x2160 anyway.
  - So they pick 4K on their own: hard-coded, from an output-mode probe, or by trying 4K and falling back if refused. Nothing in our libraries ever tells them 4K is refused.
- **The likely sequence:** "register 3840x2160, fall back to 1920x1080 if the console refuses it". It is the one public console behaviour that turns a 4K title into a 1080p one. SharpProspero documents that `sceVideoOutRegisterBuffers2` refuses a size the console is not set up for with 0x80290005 (INVALID_RESOLUTION), and that only 1920x1080 is accepted on every console. Planet of Lana's 1080p-then-4K pattern fits it, or a separate early 1080p set. Which calls the titles really make is not known here (below).
- **The change:** fork **`followup/videoout-output-resolution-prm036`** at **bfc348462a**, one commit on 35f4fe60a0. With the setting, `sceVideoOutRegisterBuffers2` refuses main-output buffers wider or taller than the setting, other than 1920x1080, with `VIDEO_OUT_ERROR_INVALID_RESOLUTION`. An attribute change and PS4 display buffers of such a size stop with an error naming the setting, because the console's answer to them is unknown. Without the setting nothing changes.
  - The new test `guest_video_out_output_sequences` (4 settings) plays every sequence and checks each answer.
  - The existing `guest_video_out_output_resolution_2560x1440` now expects its 3840x64 registration to be refused, and then registers 2560x64.
  - `check_conventions` passes.
- **CI Linux job:** registration "935 test executable(s), 0 built and not run as recorded, none new".
  - Branch head: **1262 passed, 74 skipped, 6 failed / 1342**.
  - Main 0906c64501: **1260 passed, 66 skipped, 6 failed / 1332**.
  - The same 6 fail on both: the 5 IPv6 tests and the root-only `guest_named_shm_concurrency`. check_skips flags the same two baseline skips on both.
  - The 8 extra skips are the new and changed display tests, which skip with "no display or Vulkan device" on a headless runner, as the branch's existing guest VideoOut tests already do on main.
  - Under Xvfb all 32 `video_out_*` and `guest_video_out_*` ctests pass or skip for their usual display reasons, and the 9 output-resolution and sequence tests pass x10 (90/90).
- **Step 5:** see the sentence below.
- **To settle it:** one boot of each title with `APS5_TRACE_CALLS=libSceVideoOut` on 35f4fe60a0, and one on bfc348462a with `ANYPS5_OUTPUT_RESOLUTION=1920x1080`.
  - The call list says whether they ask anything at all, and whether they register at 4K with `sceVideoOutRegisterBuffers2` or change to it with `sceVideoOutSubmitChangeBufferAttribute2`.
  - The second run shows what each does with the refusal: falls back to 1080p, stops on our error, or ends.
  - Please also send each title's param.json `attribute3` value. KytyPS5 treats its bit 2 as "resolution detection enabled".

## Step 2: the likely sequence

- **Not the reported status:** on main the title is told 1920x1080 and the HD class before it registers anything, and still registers 3840x2160. On the branch, 1280x720 and 2560x1440 with the HD class don't change that either. A title that sized its buffers from `GetResolutionStatus` or from `GetOutputStatus`'s class would have followed (the "status" and "class" sequences below show what it would register).
- **Output modes carry no size we know of.** SharpProspero's `VideoOutOutputMode` names only Default (1) and 119.88 Hz (0xF), and its refresh rates are 59.94 and 119.88 Hz. Our console measurement (cadcbfd3a, firmware 13.60) shows modes 4, 7, 8, 12-14, 16, 17 and 19 exist and were unsupported on that display. Their meaning is not public, so a title probing a 4K mode cannot be answered "no" without guessing what the mode is. One known title does branch on a probe: our debt entry records PPSA12544 probing `0xd000000a`, getting 0x8029001E and "selecting 3840 pixels".
- **The output-mode event** carries the mode, never a size, and fires at once when added. It cannot tell a title that 4K became available.
- **The register-time refusal is the one public mechanism.** SharpProspero's `DisplayDevice.Open` remarks: "The output takes a fixed set of sizes and refuses anything else: 1920x1080, 3840x2160, 720x480 and 720x576, or a width that is a multiple of 32 from 1280 to 1888 with a height sixteen-ninths narrower than it… Of those only 1920x1080 is accepted on any machine; the rest need the console set up for them, and are refused with the same answer as a size that is not on the list at all", with `VideoOutInvalidResolution = 0x80290005`. A console title written for both 4K and 1080p consoles has to survive that refusal, and "try 4K, else 1080p" is the obvious way. Lana's early 1080p set followed by a 4K set is consistent with it, or with a fixed-size boot screen.
- **Most likely:** a title that tries 3840x2160 and falls back on INVALID_RESOLUTION. That's my reading, not a measurement.
- **The other model is KytyPS5's:** `VideoOutGetOutputStatus` reports UltraHd4K unless the title's param.json attribute3 bit 2 enables "resolution detection". If that's the console's rule, a title without the bit is always told 4K and always renders 4K, and only the register-time refusal can move it at all.
- shadPS4 (PS4) only reports its configured size through `sceVideoOutGetResolutionStatus` and never refuses a size. PS4 titles commonly follow that call, which these PS5 titles do not.

## Step 3: what each sequence gets

Harness: `core/libs/tests/GuestVideoOutOutputSequences.cpp` (new, on the follow-up branch), run with the setting unset, at 1280x720, at 2560x1440 and at 3840x2160. Each sequence opens the main output, plays its calls, unregisters and closes. 0x80290016 is UNSUPPORTED_OUTPUT_MODE.

The sequences:
- **status:** `GetResolutionStatus`, then register at its size.
- **class:** `GetOutputStatus`, then register at 3840x2160 for class 2, else 1920x1080.
- **modes:** `IsOutputSupported` for modes 1, 0xF, 0xD000000A, 4, 7, 8, 0xC, 0xD, 0xE, 0x10, 0x11, 0x13, then `ConfigureOutput(1)`.
- **fallback:** register 3840x2160; if refused, 1920x1080.
- **two sets:** set 0 at 1920x1080, then set 1 at 3840x2160.
- **change:** register 1920x1080, then `SubmitChangeBufferAttribute2` to 3840x2160.

## Step 4: the change

**Branch:** `followup/videoout-output-resolution-prm036` at **bfc348462a**: "fix(videoout): refuse display buffers larger than ANYPS5_OUTPUT_RESOLUTION", on 35f4fe60a0. It is one commit with its tests; no comments in code; unsigned Yunus Shaikh, no trailers; `check_conventions --base HEAD~1` passes.
- **`libSceVideoOut/src/Buffer.cpp`:**
  - `exceedsConfiguredOutput` (:26): with the setting and on the main bus, a size wider or taller than the setting, other than 1920x1080.
  - `sceVideoOutRegisterBuffers2` returns `VIDEO_OUT_ERROR_INVALID_RESOLUTION` for such a size (:173-179) and logs once.
  - `refuseUnknownResize` (:33) throws for such a size in PS4 `sceVideoOutRegisterBuffers` (:223) and in `sceVideoOutSubmitChangeBufferAttribute2` (:261). The console's answer to those is not public, so they refuse instead of guessing.
- **`include/VideoOutDriver.hpp`:** `VIDEO_OUT_ERROR_INVALID_RESOLUTION = 0x80290005` (:31), the value SharpProspero and shadPS4 (`ORBIS_VIDEO_OUT_ERROR_INVALID_RESOLUTION`) name.
- **Tests (same commit):**
  - `core/libs/tests/GuestVideoOutOutputSequences.cpp`, registered as `guest_video_out_output_sequences_{unset,1280x720,2560x1440,3840x2160}` with the existing tests' environment, `RESOURCE_LOCK videoout_app0` and skip code 77.
  - `GuestVideoOutOutputResolution.cpp`'s 2560x1440 case now expects its 3840x64 registration to be refused, then registers 2560x64.
- **`docs/user/USAGE.md`:** the output resolution section says what is refused, what stops, and the new log line.
- **`docs/dev/debt/libSceVideoOut.md`:** a new entry with SharpProspero's list and quote, what is inferred, and KytyPS5's competing model:
  - the rule "no larger than the setting" is an inference;
  - which console setting "set up for" means is unknown;
  - 2560x1440 is not on SharpProspero's list, and is taken here at the 2560x1440 and 3840x2160 settings;
  - the attribute change and PS4 cases throw.

**What a title following the fallback sequence registers:** 1920x1080 at the 1280x720, 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 settings, and 3840x2160 at 3840x2160 or unset. It never registers 1280x720 or 2560x1440 unless it sizes from `sceVideoOutGetResolutionStatus`, which these titles do not. For the FSR scaler that is still a display buffer smaller than a 1440p or 4K window.

## Step 5: what the setting cannot reach

ANYPS5_OUTPUT_RESOLUTION cannot reach a title that always registers 3840x2160 and stops or ends when that is refused, a title that renders at 4K and scales internally to whatever buffer it is given, or a title that picks its size from an output-mode probe we cannot answer; and a title that falls back from 3840x2160 falls back to 1920x1080, so the 1280x720 and 2560x1440 settings give a smaller buffer only to a title that sizes its buffers from sceVideoOutGetResolutionStatus.
