## The rename

| Before (99af947b1) | After (082c3268a) |
|---|---|
| `GuestArena::GuestArenaSetHostWriteObserver_nid_postfix(void (*observer)(void* pointer, std::size_t bytes))` | `GuestArena::GuestArenaSetHostWriteHook_nid_no_patch(void (*hook)(std::uintptr_t address, std::size_t bytes))` |

- **The suffix.** `_nid_no_patch` is right for this function. CONTRIBUTING.md: "Helper symbols that must not become NIDs use the `_nid_no_patch` or `_nid_no_patch_cut` suffix". A host-side hook setter is such a helper. The `_nid_postfix` I first gave it would have made it a NID.
- **What did not change:**
  - `GuestArenaBeginHostWrite_nid_postfix` still calls the hook for every non-empty range, before the Windows view handling.
  - The driver still registers the hook in `Driver::Driver` and clears it in `~Driver` (`Execution/src/Driver/Lifecycle.cpp:143`, `:90`).
  - `PrepareHostWrite` still returns early under `OwnHostWrite` (the macOS import window) and for zero bytes. Otherwise it calls `FlushGpuWrites` under `ReadSiteScope(ReadSite::Store)`.
  - The test only follows the names, in its `--cost` mode.
  - The debt entry needed no change, since it names no function.

- **`core/libs/prx/libc/src/GuestArena.cpp`, two hunks:**

  - After `GuestArenaWriteWatched_nid_postfix`, it keeps `GuestArenaSetHostWriteHook_nid_no_patch`, then #6234's `GuestArenaSetHostReadHook_nid_no_patch` and `GuestArenaPrepareHostRead_nid_no_patch`. The bodies are as each PR wrote them.
  - `GuestArenaBeginHostWrite`'s hook call (ours) merged without a conflict.
- **`docs/dev/debt/libSceAgcDriver.md:75`:** both PRs rewrote the pending storage-image entry. The resolution keeps ours and inserts #6234's sentence right after our host-write sentence: "Explicit-size file writes (`write`, `pwrite`, `writev`, `pwritev`, AIO and `fwrite`) synchronize their source ranges through the [host-read hook](…GuestArena.cpp); other host readers are not covered."
- **No conflict in `Execution/src/GuestMemory.cpp`:**
  - #6234 moves `readGuard` into the anonymous namespace and adds `PrepareHostRead` there, registered from `SetFlushHook` and `SetReadGuard`.
  - Our public `PrepareHostWrite` follows `FlushGpuWrites`.
  - Both paths end in the same `FlushGpuWrites`.

**Neither hook's semantics change in the merge.**
- The two hooks never run for the same call. #6234's runs where the host *reads* guest memory: the libkernel `write`/`pwrite`/`writev`/`pwritev`, AIO writes and libc's `fwrite`. Ours runs where the host *writes* it, inside `GuestArena::HostWrite`. AIO has `NativePread` under our hook and `NativePwrite` under #6234's.
- #6234's test resets the flush hook and the read guard to exercise its own guard. It does only file writes, so our hook is not on its path.

**One difference composes but is not uniform: when each hook is registered.**
- #6234 registers its host read hook from `GuestMemory::SetFlushHook`/`SetReadGuard` and never clears it.
- Ours is set in the driver's constructor and cleared in its destructor.
- Both are safe while the driver lives: `FlushGpuWrites` with no flush hook does nothing. If the pair should share a lifetime, ours could move into `SetFlushHook` beside #6234's; that is a one-line change. I kept ours as it was, as the task asked.
