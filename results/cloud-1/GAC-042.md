## Bottom line

- **The red test goes red on main**, on both address-based paths, and green with the fix.
- **The gap is wider than the task describes.** With the address-space cache (the default), the heaps never reach the exempt branch at all:
  - they live in the cached space's `base`, not in the build's `regions`;
  - UploadFinish's loop walks only `regions`, so neither a store nor a publish ever ran for them.
- **This changes how to read `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1`.** On a default boot it is a no-op for the heaps; red on main, see "Red and green" below.

- **The fix publishes, it does not store.**
  - Each address-based build makes one `AnyShadowedOverlaps` check over the span of the heaps it maps; the cached space's base is included.
  - Only when a fresh unit is found does it publish that span (`PublishScope::Whole`). Pending image results stay pending.
  - The copies count under a new publish reason, `address`, in the `[storage]` line.

## The bug, with file:line on main

- **The exemption.** `Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3733-3741` skips `FlushPending` for `addressable && region.hostBacked` regions. `FlushPending` is the call that both stores and publishes.
- **The address-space cache.** It is on unless `APS5_NO_ADDRESS_SPACE_CACHE=1`.
  - It is described at `:79-91`. `AcquireRegistered` takes a hit at `:2696-2742` and builds and publishes the space at `:2823-2887`.
  - Building it moves every in-place heap into `space->base` and leaves `regions` with the extras (`:2864-2873`).
  - `UploadFinish` walks only `regions` (`:3649`). For `space->base` it only completes pending CPU writes (`completeSpaceWrites`, `:3554-3571`) and checks the imports epoch (`:3626-3639`).
- **Where results go.** With unit shadows on, a write-back with `PublishScope::None` retiles into the shadow (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2851-2876` → `ShadowDestinationFor`). It reaches the import only through `PublishShadow`. Two stores do this:
  - "storage refresh" (`Texture.cpp:1759`);
  - "storage image creation" (`ShaderResources.cpp:974`).
- **The stage A comment.** `GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3140-3141` says "the publish happens when the region is bound, under the device lock". Until this fix, that held for descriptor regions but never for address-based heaps.

## The fix: where the publish runs, its lock and its batch

`Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp` (+13/-1; the whole commit is +160/-3, 136 of them the test):
- **`:3649-3654`, before the upload loop.** The span starts as `space->base.front().begin` .. `space->base.back().end`. `base` is sorted and non-overlapping (registered ranges; `baseOverlap` already searches it). This covers the cached path.
- **`:3743-3750`, the exempt branch.** It still stores nothing, but adds the region to the span. Non-exempt regions and `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1` keep `FlushPending`. This covers the per-build path and a dissolved space, whose regions come back with `pending` set (`dissolveSpace(true)`, `:2944-2969`).
- **`:3775`, after the loop.** One call: `StorageTexture::PublishShadowsOnly(span, PublishScope::Whole, "address-based heaps")`.
  - **Gate.** `AnyShadowedOverlaps(span)` (`Texture.cpp:3429`), without the device lock: one registry lock, and O(1) for every shadow with no live unit.
  - **Lock.** `GuestMemory::GpuMutex` (`Texture.cpp:3430`). It is recursive, and stage B already runs under it:
    - a draw's build lookups are nested in the draw's hold (`Draw.cpp:379-380`);
    - a dispatch completes stage B under the mutex (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:4586-4594`).
    - Inside `PublishShadow` come the Shadows registry mutex (a leaf) and the tracker, in the order `UnitShadow.hpp:42-44` requires.
    - The import table's mutex is not held there: UploadFinish's own holds are scoped above it.
  - **Batch.** `PublishShadow` → `publishUnits` → `recordCopies` (`UnitShadow.cpp:225-265`) records into `Recorder::Active()->Commands()`, the active recorder's open batch. `Commands()` first ends an open render pass (`Recorder.cpp:1366`). It records:
    - a barrier;
    - `vkCmdCopyBuffer` from each slab to the import;
    - a TRANSFER → ALL_COMMANDS|HOST barrier whose destination accesses include SHADER_READ;
    - `NotePendingWrites`.

    Without a recorder (tests), it uses a waited `CommandBatch`.
  - **Why the copy is ordered before the build's reads.** `UploadFinish` is stage B (`ShaderResources.cpp:1916`), and the same recorder records the build's draw or dispatch after it returns. So the copy precedes the pointer reads in submission order, and the barrier makes the bytes visible to them.
  - **No store.** `PublishShadowsOnly` never looks at pending images. Results not yet stored stay pending, as the exemption intends. The build's pipelining is untouched unless a fresh unit exists.

The reason counter (`Graphics/include/UnitShadow.hpp:51`, `Graphics/src/UnitShadow.cpp:25`, `:455`): `PublishReason::Address`, printed as `address N/X.X` in the `[storage]` line's "by reason" list.

**Why one span, not one publish per region:**
- An address-based build maps every readable GPU range of the registry, so the imports inside the span are the ones its pointers can reach.
- The code puts an address-based build at about 1200 regions (`GuestBufferMemory.cpp:80`, `:3644`). One registry walk per build is cheaper than 1200 lock-and-lookups.

## The reuse path

- **Revalidation never applies.** `noteReusable` returns early for `usesBda` (`ShaderResources.cpp:2081`). So `Revalidate` never runs for an address-based build, nor does its publish at `:2993-3005`, and every address-based draw or dispatch runs stage B and `UploadFinish`.
- **The address-space cache is the reuse that exists, and it skipped the upload loop for the heaps**, as described above. The fix covers it through the span over `space->base`. A retile does not force the slow path:
  - the space is rebuilt only on a registry generation, imports epoch or device change (`:2702-2706`);
  - `MarkShadowed` bumps only `StorageTexture`'s pending serial.
- **The dispatch cache already publishes.** It calls `FlushPending` when `AnyShadowedOverlaps(variant.runs)` (`Execution/src/Driver/Dispatch/VariantValidation.cpp:96-106`).
- **The BDA table cache** (`BdaResources`) caches the range table only, not data.

- **The heap.** A watched 256 KiB block, registered as a readable, writable GPU range and imported. A 256x256 RGBA8 `kR64KBX` storage image sits over it.
- **The retile.** The image is cleared red, then `FlushPending(..., "storage refresh", PublishScope::None)` stores it.
- **Preconditions** (the test skips if the first fails):
  - the units are shadowed;
  - the import still holds the earlier bytes (0x55).

**Checks:**
1. **Red/green.** An address-based build (`AcquireRegistered` + `Upload(true)`) runs. At its submission (`Submit` + `WaitQueue`), the import must hold the red results.
2. **Cost.**
   - A build before any retile records no copy: no pending write is noted over the heap, and the bytes stay 0x55.
   - After the publish, a second build records no copy.
   - The publish is counted as `address 1/` in `ShadowReport()`.
3. **No double store.** The image is cleared green and left pending, then built over. It must still be pending (`FindPending == image`), the heap must still hold red, and no copy may be recorded.

**A lavapipe limit, checked on main.**
- With imports forced watched, llvmpipe's copy into the import is a CPU store that the write watch sees. A later write-back then keeps those units for the CPU.
- On main, with no address-based build at all, a hook publish (`GuestMemory::Read`) followed by a flush also fails to land the flushed results.
- So the test does not assert bytes after its final flush. That flush only keeps the image from being destroyed with results pending.
- On a GPU whose imports pass the probe, a publish never stamps (`UnitShadow.hpp:29-37`).

## Cost

- **No live unit in the build's heaps** (shadows off, nothing retiled, or everything already published): one `AnyShadowedOverlaps` per address-based build, that is, one registry lock plus one O(1) check per shadow. No copy and no device lock.
- **With fresh units:**
  - Each address-based build publishes them: one `vkCmdCopyBuffer` group per slab, and an open render pass ends.
  - The same units are not copied again until they are retiled again.
  - This is the price of making pointer reads see what they would see with shadows off: shadows keep saving the stores between address-based builds, but not across one.
- **The PC measures it** with the `address N/X MiB` publishes in the `[storage]` line and the frame time (COST below).

## PR draft

Title: `fix(agc): publish unit-shadow results before an address-based build maps its heaps`

```markdown
### What
An address-based build binds the registered heaps in place and does not store the storage results pending in them: the heaps hold every pending image, and storing them all would serialize the build. With unit shadows on, a storage write-back that retiles into a shadow ("storage refresh", "storage image creation") reaches the import only through a publish: a host-facing reader, an eviction, an idle flush. Nothing published before an address-based build, so its pointer reads saw the bytes as of the last publish. In Resident Evil Village (PPSA01556) that is the title menu's last frame, published once by the idle flush at the load and shown dimmed over gameplay.

Two paths had the gap:
- the per-build path skipped `FlushPending` for the exempt in-place heaps;
- with the address-space cache (the default), the heaps live in the cached space's base and never reach the upload loop at all, so `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1` did not reach them either.

`UploadFinish` now publishes the fresh units over the heaps the build maps: the space's base, and the exempt regions of the per-build path. One `AnyShadowedOverlaps` check over their span gates it.
- Pending results stay pending; only results already stored into a slab are copied. Pointer reads now see what they see with shadows off.
- The copies are recorded into the active recorder's open batch under the device lock, ahead of the build's draw or dispatch.
- They are counted under a new publish reason, `address`, in the `[storage]` line.
```
