## #6641's shutdown against main's threads

Read at merge b6e45bb5a7 (tree 272f39faaa). Paths are under `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/`.

**What the new head changes.** It adds two commits to the series cloud-2 read at 43830b93bd.
- 1f10c8afa: `Driver::stop()` now calls `FlushAsyncLogAtEnd_nid_no_patch()` (`Execution/src/Driver/Lifecycle.cpp:134`), not the unbounded `FlushAsyncLog_nid_no_patch()`.
  - The flush is `AsyncLog::FlushFor` (`Execution/include/AsyncLog.hpp:164-173`). It polls the `queued`/`finished` atomics and sleeps for at most `EndFlushLimit` = 2000 ms (`:197`), taking no lock.
  - So a thread holding the log's mutex can't hang shutdown, which is what `--async-log-held` checks.
  - The old `Flush()` (`:157-162`) waits on `idle` under `mutex`.
- e97c9975b: `InitializeWaits()` (`:212-215`) runs before the worker thread starts (`:207-208`).

**`DrawPipeline::Stop()` can't run twice in a harmful way, and has one caller.**
- Its definition is `Execution/src/Driver/Draw/DrawPipeline.cpp:176-187`, and the only caller is `Lifecycle.cpp:122`.
- `Driver::stop()` takes `shutdownMutex` (`:105`) and returns on `if (stopped) return;` (`:106`), so its body runs once.
- Even a direct second call is harmless:
  - `Stop()` sets `closing = true` and moves `thread` out under `mutex` (`:179-181`).
  - A second call moves out an empty thread, and `!committer.joinable()` returns at `:184`.
  - After `closing`, `Enqueue` runs a commit inline on the caller (`:78-82`), so no new committer can start between the two calls (the start is at `:96-99`).
  - `run()` exits only when `closing` is set and `items` is empty (`:139-141`), so commits queued before the stop still run.
  - `agc_driver_shutdown_threads_draw_committer` checks all of this: the second queued commit ran, `Committers() == 0`, and a later enqueue ran on the caller.

**#1747's pipelined dispatch worker is stopped.**
- Pipelined dispatch enqueues on the same `DrawPipeline::Queue0()` committer (`Execution/src/Driver/Dispatch/Dispatch.cpp:447`).
- Its other uses of `Queue0()` are `Overlaps`/`Drain`/`Busy` at `:286` and `:289`.
- So its worker is the committer thread (`DrawPipeline.cpp:97`). That thread is joined at `Lifecycle.cpp:122`, after the queue workers that enqueue to it are joined (`:119-120`), and before `device.Reset()` (`:127`).

- `RecordSubmission` is called from `Queues/Submission.cpp:311` and `:366`. The queue workers that drain submissions are started at `Submission.cpp:462` and joined at `Lifecycle.cpp:119-120`.
