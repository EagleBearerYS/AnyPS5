## 3. The five features most worth porting (lanes A–D, for Village)

Ranked by expected gain for Resident Evil Village and how cleanly each ports.
- **The gain** is his own measurements from the commit bodies, not re-measured here.
- **Port cleanliness** is a trial `git cherry-pick -n` of each commit alone onto a clean upstream main 195d0d08a or lane A's `exp/lane-a-gate` 6916ae065, in a scratch worktree.

1. **Plan retention: a resource plan copies only what its evaluator reads** (lane B)
   - **Commits:** 3728570f6 and cb13fecfe (2026-10-14, before ed8f2d7a2e, so not in the 374), then cca1f5a8d and 6d2ad7ee8 from this push. About +395 −12, with tests.
   - **What it does:**
     - Main's `ownPlanValues` copies the whole argument closure of every root: descriptor sources, SRT reads, control-flow conditions and fills.
     - He copies conditions only as far as `Evaluator::Reads` goes (3728570f6), drops conditions the evaluator can never decide (cb13fecfe), and below a condition follows only what the evaluator reads (cca1f5a8d).
   - **His numbers:**
     - "a title that registers about 5,700 shaders at load held 24.1 M copied IR values in its plans (about 5.4 GiB by value size), of which 2.1 M were reachable from descriptor sources, SRT reads and fills";
     - plan values 1,376,358 → 1,071,151 on his largest corpus, private bytes 731.5 → 673.0 MiB;
     - "the plans of 314 programs hold 40 thousand values instead of 837 thousand".
   - **For Village:** plans are 1.40 GB of the 7.4 GB of shader data. This targets most of it.
   - **Port:** recompiler-only (ResourceMaterializer.cpp, SrtEvaluator.{hpp,cpp}, tests), and every file exists on main.
     - 3728570f6 conflicts in its three source files.
     - cb13fecfe conflicts only in its test file, ShaderMemory.cpp.
     - cca1f5a8d conflicts in CMakeLists.txt and in ResourceMaterializer.cpp, the hunk that builds on 3728570f6.
     - 6d2ad7ee8 picks clean.
     - Plans already in the shader disk cache keep their old contents until the plan format version changes.
   - **The cleanest and largest single win in the push.**
2. **Smaller device-table image code** (lane A, which also shrinks modules for lane B)
   - **Commits:** 1bf7ad7b9 07ec7ade4 bf9da0fb1 d143447c4 5b156a429 c1e4b1e5f 154ff157d 1a799ae87 b46caa96f 19f978cdc 2301a9d0f, plus tests 906aa3287 05500db40 and a42e22808. 15 commits, +1102 −235.
   - **What it does:** shrinks the SPIR-V of device-table image accesses:
     - lookups reused within a block;
     - mode switches inline up to 512 arms, with shared arm work;
     - 80-byte buckets read as five 16-byte loads;
     - image store records by compare/select instead of private arrays;
     - the sRGB decode computed instead of a 256-entry private table per invocation;
     - the buffer format table in one device buffer instead of every module.
   - **For Village:** the modules are 1.47 GB, and the device-table path is lane A's.
   - **Port:**
     - Picked alone onto `exp/lane-a-gate`, all 11 code commits conflict, mostly in SpirvImageEmitter.cpp and CMake. The emitters diverged.
     - 078df1e0f (bounds-compare reuse) picks clean onto main itself.
     - d143447c4 conflicts there (ImageSrgbLoad.cpp, SpirvImageEmitter.cpp), but it replaces the same 256-entry table main's `SrgbTable` builds.
     - Those two would help main's current path.
3. **Descriptors resolved once at the load** (lane A)
   - **Commits:** da80d4c85 f99bfd704 4e1fab704 aaf7e76c8 70984b658 9dcff34d3 4aac30edf 0e9a850c4 e77aa8f1c, plus tests 93c5169cd 6787148a8 26d295169 856d47e34. 13 commits, +946 −163.
   - **What it does:** emits a device-table image lookup once after the last definition of its descriptor words, and resolves a run-time V#'s page-table segment once at the V# load rather than at every access. For wave-uniform descriptors it does this in one elected lane, then broadcasts.
   - **For Village:** this is the per-access cost and SPIR-V size of lane A's run-time descriptors. It is the "resolved once at the load" item the LA-008 note named.
   - **Port:** picked alone onto `exp/lane-a-gate`, all 9 code commits conflict (SpirvImageEmitter, SpirvBda*, SpirvRuntimeBuffer). Part of it targets his BDA run-time buffers, which lane A doesn't have, so only the image-lookup half carries over directly.

4. **Work out from under the device lock** (lane D)
   - **Commits:** a7c459f68 910e5112b 81c844d03 39e8b8119 531e45035 08baca73b 244560917 d314223bd 700dbcdc9 3315263c9 c09710145. 11 commits, +2071 −111.

5. **Pipeline creation memory gate and store budget** (lane B)
   - **Commits:** 8eb794299 880521c88 ba4979e3c cbd86293b ee3f5e718 9766ccc19 39d502845. 7 commits, +1159 −42.
