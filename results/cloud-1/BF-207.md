**What each does here:**

- **`RSA_new`:** allocates the 152-byte `RSA` from the guest `malloc`, zeroed, with the default method `"Eric Young's PKCS#1 RSA"` (method flags 0) and one reference. The method's `init` sets the key flags to `RSA_FLAG_CACHE_PUBLIC | RSA_FLAG_CACHE_PRIVATE` (0x6). The method's eight slots are host functions. The private-key slots, `rsa_mod_exp` and `bn_mod_exp` throw, since no export sets a private key yet.
- **`RSA_free`:**
  - `NULL` does nothing.
  - Otherwise it drops a reference atomically, and at zero it runs `finish`.
  - It then clears and frees each `BIGNUM` as `BN_clear_free` does: `BN_FLG_STATIC_DATA` keeps the words, `BN_FLG_MALLOCED` frees the struct.
  - Finally it frees the key.
  - It throws for a key holding an `ENGINE`, ex_data, blinding, or a Montgomery context, none of which this module creates.
- **`d2i_RSA_PUBKEY`:**
  - Decodes a SubjectPublicKeyInfo with LibreSSL's decoder rules: high-tag form, long-form lengths with leading zeros, length within the input, EOC inside a definite SEQUENCE refused, tag and class checks, and the ANY rules (NULL length 0, BOOLEAN length 1, valid OID, string lengths).
  - The OID is `rsaEncryption` or `rsa`, and the BIT STRING's unused bits are masked off.
  - `n` and `e` are read as unsigned `BIGNUM`s onto a fresh `RSA_new` key.
  - `*pp` advances past the SEQUENCE. `*a` is freed and replaced.
  - BER forms that LibreSSL also accepts throw: indefinite lengths, constructed strings and parameters, and 32 or more unused bits.
- **`RSA_public_encrypt` / `RSA_public_decrypt`:**
  - Both dispatch through the key's method (`rsa_pub_enc`, `rsa_pub_dec`).
  - The default ones check the modulus limits in order: over 16384 bits, `n <= e`, and over 3072 bits with `e` over 64 bits. Then the input checks: `f >= n`, length over the modulus size, and negative length.
  - Encryption pads with PKCS#1 v1.5 type 2 (host entropy, non-zero), OAEP (SHA-1, empty label) or none.
  - Decryption checks PKCS#1 v1.5 type 1 or none. Other paddings give -1.
  - `RSA_SSLV23_PADDING` encryption, `RSA_X931_PADDING` decryption, a method with its own `bn_mod_exp`, a negative `BIGNUM` and a modulus over 8192 bits (mbedtls's `MBEDTLS_MPI_MAX_BITS`) throw.
- **`RSA_verify`:**
  - Uses the method's `rsa_verify` under `RSA_FLAG_SIGN_VER`.
  - Otherwise it checks the signature length against `RSA_size`, calls `RSA_public_decrypt` with PKCS#1 padding, then handles one of two cases:
    - **`NID_md5_sha1`:** the 36-byte case;
    - **`X509_SIG`:** the decode (no trailing data, parameter absent or NULL, the object's NID equal to `dtype`, digest length and bytes).
  - `OBJ_obj2nid` is modelled by the 15 digest objects of 2.5.4's table: md2, md5, sha, sha1, mdc2, ripemd160, md4, sha256/384/512/224, whirlpool, GOST R 34.11-94 and both Streebogs. Their DER and NIDs were printed from the real 2.5.4 library, which also confirmed that each DER occurs once in its 956-entry table.
  - So a `dtype` in that table, or a signature object in it, gives the exact answer; so does a `dtype` below 0 or at or above `NUM_NID`.
  - A `dtype` and an object both outside it throw, `NID_undef` included: real LibreSSL returns 1 for an unknown object with `dtype` 0.

**Gaps** (in `docs/dev/debt/libSceLibreSsl.md`, four new entries, each under 1,000 characters: 892, 858, 939 and 963):
- the throws above;
- the error codes LibreSSL queues and these don't;
- the Montgomery context that is never cached;
- objects added with `OBJ_create` (a stub) not being seen by `RSA_verify`;
- nothing is measured on a console.
