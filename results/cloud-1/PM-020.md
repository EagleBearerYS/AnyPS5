# PM-020: per-draw track, the next two costs after cut #1 (analysis)

Worker: cloud-1. Analysis only: no PR. There is one candidate branch on the fork, `test/agc-image-mirror-epoch-compare`.

## Bottom line

1. **CompareBlocks (6%)**
   - **What remains after cut #1.** Cut #1 removes the repeat heap walks. What it leaves is the full compare of every GPU-mapped writable image mirror (exe .data/.bss that the game mprotected for the GPU), on every address-based build. That memory has no write watch, so no CollectWrites bitmap exists for it.
   - **Candidate.** A per-mirror compare stamp skips the compare for the rest of a collect epoch unless the driver stored into the range. The stamp is `{collect epoch, NewestStamp, ForgetSerial, NewestWriteNote}`, the same key `StorageTexture::compareUntracked` already memoizes by.
   - **Bench, 16 MiB of such memory.** A later build in an epoch drops from 569 to 24 µs (cut #1 regime).
   - **Branch.** `test/agc-image-mirror-epoch-compare`, red/green below.
2. **Resources.Complete self (10%)**
   - **The address-based part is small.** After #5465 and #5542, the address-based part of stage B (UploadFinish plus the BDA table) measures 3 to 70 µs per build. That holds with 383 heap runs and up to 1024 own regions.
   - **Nothing in stage B iterates the heap runs on a space hit.** Registered ranges matter only on a BDA-table key miss and for the space's copied ranges.
   - **Where the 10% must be.** In the parts that scale with descriptors, mainly bound image-table elements: the per-element descriptor writes, the deferred image lookups, and `captureValidation`. Or in copied ranges, if Village has any.
   - **Best cut I can back with a number.** `captureValidation` is quadratic in looked-up textures, and its output is never read for an address-based build. Skipping it there is a 5-line, safe change. It saves 0.05 to 0.35 ms per build at 512 to 2048 freshly looked-up textures, more beyond.
   - **What to do first.** Split the zone (4 zones, below) before building anything bigger.
3. **Overlap.**
   - None of the open per-draw PRs (#5656, #1202, #1896, #5609, #1201, #5859, #5872, #5910) or discussion #5436 touches the writable-mirror compare, `captureValidation`, `completeSpaceWrites`, or the BDA-table path. All proposals here are new.

## Q2: CompareBlocks

**Where it runs in an address-based build**

- `AcquireRegistered`, called from `prepareAddressBindings` before stage A, ends with one `compareBlocks(blocks)` at `Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:2728`. `blocks` collects:
  - (a) Every 64 KiB block of every writable non-heap (image) mirror.
    - On a space hit this is `GuestBufferMemory.cpp:2571-2574` (`space->writableMirrors`, filled by `planSpaceMirrors` 1662-1670).
    - On a rebuild it is `acquireMirror` 1721.
  - (b) Heap blocks of runs that fail `UnchangedSince(verified)`, from `refreshHeapRuns` 1685-1698. Only the blocks `GuestMemory::ChangedBlocks` reports are added (`appendChangedBlocks` 1524-1544).
  - (c) Sparse heap blocks (`refreshSparseMirror` 1652-1660).
- In stage B, `UploadFinish` compares writable sub-range mirror regions through `refreshMirror` (3482 → 1517-1522) and sparse runs through `bindSparseMirror` (3488 → 2895).

**What it compares** (`compareBlock` 1354-1372, run by `RefreshPool` 1374-1464)

- Heap blocks get an unconditional `memcpy` guest → mirror buffer plus a generation update (1362-1366). The decision was already made by the write-watch bitmap.
- Image blocks get a `memcmp` against the mirror's shadow, and a copy into buffer and shadow when they differ (1367-1371).
- Sparse blocks get a copy or a zero fill.

**Why it still costs after cut #1**

- Cut #1 makes later builds of an epoch hit the collect memo. They stamp no new CPU writes, so (b) shrinks to blocks the driver stamped (GPU write-backs, labels). That happens once per epoch at most, plus driver stores.
- (a) is untouched by cut #1. Image ranges are never write-watched:
  - On Windows they are `MEM_IMAGE`, and GetWriteWatch only covers `MEM_WRITE_WATCH` allocations.
  - On Linux the image is not registered with `GuestWriteWatch`.
  - So every build memcmps all of them.
- Image ranges are registered with `gpu=false` (libc `GuestAllocations.cpp:205`, Windows; `:279`, Linux). They enter the address space only once the game mprotects them with GPU flags (`DirectMemory.cpp:626`/`1075` → `GuestAllocationsProtect` 516-522). Whether Village does is the first thing to check; see "How to tell" below.

**Could a CollectWrites dirty bitmap skip blocks?**

- For (b) it already does: `appendChangedBlocks` adds only `ChangedBlocks` blocks.
- For (a) there is no bitmap to use: the memory is outside the write watch.
- What can be used is the ordering rule the collect memo already relies on (`Execution/include/GuestMemory.hpp:84-98`): a guest store made while a submission runs may be seen at the next ordering point. The driver's own stores are never hidden, and outside the tracker they are logged by `noteUnstampedStore` (`Execution/src/GuestMemory.cpp:936-945`, reached from `storeOwn` 1383 and `MarkWritten` 1427) and read by `NewestStamp` (1507).
- `StorageTexture::compareUntracked` (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2106-2142`) already skips an untracked compare by exactly this stamp (2125).

**Candidate on `test/agc-image-mirror-epoch-compare`**

- Each `ImageMirror` keeps `compared = {CollectEpoch, NewestStamp(range), ForgetSerial, NewestWriteNote(range)}` from its last whole compare.
- On a space hit, `prepareRefresh` (sync plus flush hook) still runs for every writable mirror. The blocks are appended only when the stamp differs or the epoch is 0.
- The stamp is stored only after `compareBlocks` succeeds.
- A thread that never bumps its epoch, `APS5_NO_COLLECT_MEMO=1`, an odd `ForgetSerial`, or any driver store or write-back over the range all make it compare again.
- The behaviour change is the memo's: a guest store into GPU-mapped exe data between two builds of one epoch is seen by the next epoch.

**Bench** (µs per build; "later" means builds 2..50 of an epoch)

| Setup | Acquire, first build of an epoch | Acquire, later builds | Total, later builds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 383 runs × 128 KiB, no image range | 566 | 583 | 607 |
| 383 runs × 128 KiB, 16 MiB image range | 1332 | 1255 | 1317 |
| same, with the candidate | 1635 | 643 | 677 |
| 48 runs × 1 MiB (memo fits = cut #1 regime), no image range | 787 | 22.6 | 29.1 |

- 16 MiB of image compare costs about 500 to 670 µs per build here: memcmp at ~30 GB/s across 4 threads.
- With 383 runs the 64-entry FIFO memo (`Execution/src/GuestMemory.cpp:1068`) thrashes, so first and later builds cost the same. That is the cost cut #1 targets.
- In the cut #1 regime, the image compare is about 95% of what later builds still spend in `AcquireRegistered`. The candidate removes it.
- First-build numbers are noisy (7 samples per run).

**Estimate for Village**

- Saving per draw ≈ the image share of CompareBlocks × (1 − epochs/builds).
- If the 6% (~2 ms per draw at 34 ms) is mostly image blocks and an epoch holds 4 or more address-based builds, that is about 1.5 ms per draw, or 4 to 5% of draw time. With 2 builds per epoch it is about half that.
- If the 6% is heap blocks, cut #1 already removes most of it and the candidate adds about nothing.

**How to tell** (`APS5_PROFILE_DRAW=1`)

- The `[buffers] image mirrors: N ranges (X MiB, W writable, H heap Y MiB) … blocks compared, copied … unwatched heaps U` line (1876). X − Y MiB are image mirrors.
- The `[address] … compare C us (B blocks, K copied)` line (2499).
- B ≈ (writable image MiB × 16) per build means (a) dominates.
- `[guestmem]` `CollectEpochBumps` against address-based builds gives builds per epoch.

**Second CompareBlocks cut (not built, smaller)**

- After a heap mirror's write-back (`WriteBack` 4124, heap branch 4151-4156), the driver-store stamp makes the next build re-copy those blocks from guest memory (b). The mirror already holds the GPU's bytes.
- Recording the write-back's store generation in `mirror.generations[block]`, when the store's own CPU walk stamped nothing newer, would skip that copy.
- It saves one 64 KiB memcpy (~5 to 10 µs) per GPU-written heap block per build. It needs care, because other driver stores into the same block must still refresh it.

**Heap runs (~383).** On a space hit, nothing in stage B iterates them. All per-run work happens before stage B, in `AcquireRegistered`. It is not only the collect:
- `refreshHeapRuns` 1685-1698 calls `prepareRange` per run: `GuestMemory::Accessible`, the flush hook, and `Recorder::PendingWriteOverlaps`. The last is a linear scan of every open and in-flight write (`Recorder.cpp:2807-2815`), so each build pays runs × W.
- 2542-2546 stamps `lastUse` on every direct base region under the imports mutex.

**Registered ranges.** They enter stage B only through a BDA-table key miss (`AddressRanges` plus `hashRanges` plus table buffer) and through copied ranges (O(bytes), every build).

**Descriptors.** These scale: the deferred image lookups, the descriptor writes, `finishImageTables`, and `captureValidation`, which is O(textures × lookups).

- `resolveImageTables` at 6% (~2 ms per draw) suggests image tables of hundreds to thousands of elements per draw. When they are resolved fresh, `captureValidation` alone is 0.15 to 0.35 ms per build at 1 to 2K textures, and grows with T × min(T, 1024).
- For an address-based build it is wasted. `noteReusable` makes the object non-reusable right after (`usesBda`, 2012). The records are read only by `Revalidate`/`ProveCurrent` (2740), and those run only on cached objects. Caches and recipes take `Reusable()` objects only: `Draw.cpp:2187`, `2608`; `Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:4552`, `4584`, `4708`. Address-based draws never enter them (`NeverReusable`, `Draw.cpp:1279`).

**Proposed cuts for Q1, ranked by expected saving per draw.** Item 1 is likely the largest but is unmeasured. Item 2 is measured and ready to build.

1. **Image-table descriptor writes (needs the split to size).**
   - Every build writes every bound table element into a fresh set (1853-1936), even when `FindReusedTable` proved the same table (4107-4123).
   - With T of several thousand, the driver's `vkUpdateDescriptorSets`, at an assumed 50 to 200 ns per image descriptor on desktop drivers (not measured here: the mock and lavapipe are not representative), would be 0.2 to 1+ ms per draw. That is the only stage-B item I can see reaching ms scale without copied ranges.
   - Cut: key a written descriptor set, or a separate table set, by the reused tables' identity and skip the writes.
   - Design-level: the set layout carries per-build buffers (BDA table, fault buffer).
   - Related to the set recycling in #1201/#1202/#1896 (`DescriptorCache::AllocateRewritten`/`Recycle`), but that is in `PrepareDrawBindings`, not `buildComplete`. New for this path.
2. **Skip `captureValidation` for non-reusable builds (small, safe; not built).** Move the non-reusable test ahead of the capture in `noteReusable` (2007-2012):

   ```cpp
   void ShaderResources::noteReusable() {
       reusable = false;
       directRegions.clear();
       if (guestMemory.HasCopiedWrites() || HoldsLease() || usesBda || !multisampleSources.empty() || (bda != nullptr && LoopGuardEnabled())) {
           lookupLog().clear();
           validatedTextures.clear();
           return;
       }
       captureValidation();
       ...
   ```

   - It saves the whole scan per address-based build: 0.05 to 0.35 ms at 512 to 2048 fresh lookups (about 0.7 to 1 ms extrapolated at 4096), and about 30 µs for a 2048-element reused table.
   - A one-pass map in `captureValidation` would also help reusable builds: 150 → 26 µs at 1024.
   - New: no open PR touches `captureValidation`/`lookupLog`.
3. **Copied ranges, only if Village has them.** Check `[buffers] … unwatched heaps U` (nonzero means heap ranges that overlap arena host regions are copied, reason "outside the write-watched arena", 2642) and the `[buffers] … outside imports (MiB)` copy line.
   - Each build allocates a buffer per range and reads it in full, in stage A or B (see the table above). I have no faithful per-MiB number from the mock.
   - Cut: keep them as mirrors compared under the same epoch stamp as the branch.
   - New.
4. **`completeSpaceWrites`.** It copies all pending recorder writes every build. That is cheap unless W is in the thousands; size it with the split first.

**The split to run first.** Add four Tracy zones inside Resources.Complete:
- `UploadFinish` (1847)
- `BdaResources` plus `finishImageTables` (1849-1851)
- the descriptor block (1853-1937)
- `noteReusable` (1951)

The `[resources]` phase totals cannot separate these on their own, because stage A also adds to "guest memory upload" and "descriptors" (1639, 1748). Only "images" and "bda" are stage-B only.
