## Step 3: the merge 32d5555820

`git show --remerge-diff 32d5555820 -- core/shader/recompiler/RuntimeAbi.hpp docs/user/USAGE.md`:
- **RuntimeAbi.hpp:** the conflict was main's `SamplerHeapCapacity = 64u` against the PR side's `= 32u`, which the PR inherited from its base 2fab88d102. The resolution keeps 64 and then the PR's lines unchanged: `GpuImageHeapSlots = 4096u`, `GpuSamplerHeapSlots = 256u`, `GpuDataSet = 2u`, `HeapSlotWord`, and the `GpuLookup*` constants and `GpuLookupHash`.
- **USAGE.md:** main's "Texture cache budget" section stays whole, followed by the PR's "Draw pipeline" and "GPU descriptor heap" sections. Nothing was lost.

**Main's raise from 32 to 64** is f7b2ebd8b ("fix(shader): allow 32 sampler pairs per shader"). It also moves `RuntimeAbi::Version` from 12 to 13, `sizeof(ShaderData)` from 14528 to 16064 and `ExportMappingsDword` from 3624 to 4008. Checked against PR 6947's diff (`git diff 2fab88d102 fab8dc64b0`, 14 commits):
- **The sampler capacity:** the PR never names `SamplerHeapCapacity`. Its sampler heap is sized by its own `GpuSamplerHeapSlots` (256), both in its descriptor set bindings (`heapBindings ... Abi::GpuSamplerHeapSlots`) and in its slot allocation (`heldSamplers.size() < Abi::GpuSamplerHeapSlots`).
- **The metadata layout:** the PR reaches `ResourceMetadata` through `sizeof(Abi::ResourceMetadata)` and `offsetof(Abi::ResourceMetadata, …)`, and positions through `ImageMetadataDword()` and `SamplerSlotDword()`, so main's larger sampler block moves with it.
- **Old literals:** none of 14528, 3624 or a sampler 32 appears in its diff. Its literal 32s are a page-pool refill loop (ShaderMemory) and bit widths.
- **The version:** the PR does not change `Version`, which stays at main's 13, and adds no layout `static_assert` of its own.
- The binding allocator's checks (`samplers.size() > RuntimeAbi::SamplerHeapCapacity`, BindingAllocator.cpp:103, :173) and the device profile's room (ShaderResources.cpp:4244-4245) are main's and use the new 64.
