## 3. The cut

**Commit:** 33f3b3105 `perf(agc): prove an unchanged untracked storage image from its compare stamp`.
- 4 files, +72 −20: `Graphics/include/Texture.hpp`, `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp`, `tests/execution/DccKeysFirstWrite.cpp`, `docs/dev/ENVIRONMENT.md`.
- Author and committer Yunus Shaikh, unsigned, no trailers.

**Mechanism** (`core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/Texture.cpp` at 33f3b3105):
- **Where the full path started:** `StorageTexture::Refresh` (`:2040`) tries `refreshProved()` (`:2045`) before the full path.
  - On main, `refreshProved` returns false whenever `refreshProof.generation == 0`.
  - `takeRefreshProof` (called when a refresh ends unchanged, `:2214`) refused to take a proof for a write generation of 0.
  - So an image over untracked memory never had a proof.
- **`untrackedStamp` (`:2506`)** is factored out of `compareUntracked` (`:2468`). It returns the memo's stamp, which `compareUntracked` now also uses:
  - the untracked hint or the collect epoch;
  - `GuestMemory::NewestStamp`;
  - `GuestMemory::ForgetSerial`;
  - `Recorder::NewestWriteNote`.
- **`takeRefreshProof` (`:2368`)** now takes an *untracked* proof only when all of these hold:
  - the write generation is 0;
  - the switch is on (`UntrackedRefreshProofs`, `:1678`);
  - the image has no DCC keys;
  - `untrackedSettled()` holds;
  - the current stamp is cacheable and equals `comparedGuestBytes`, the stamp of the compare that just answered "unchanged".
  - The proof stores that stamp (`RefreshProof::untracked`, `RefreshProof::compared`, `Texture.hpp:573-574`).
- **`untrackedSettled()` (`:2383`)** requires all of these:
  - no unwatch-kept units, hand-overs or handed units;
  - no saved stored-save ticket;
  - `CollectWrites` is still 0, so the range is still untracked;
  - `originalHolds`.
- **`refreshProved()` (`:2341`)** branches on the proof:
  - **An untracked proof** goes to `untrackedProofStands()` (`:2390`). That re-checks `untrackedSettled()`, then flushes GPU writes over the range with the image exempt, exactly as `compareUntracked` does. Then it requires `originalHolds` and an equal, cacheable stamp.
  - **A tracked proof** keeps main's `CollectWrites`/`UnchangedSinceAll` check (`:2358`).
  - **Both** keep the pending-serial check against other images' pending results.
- **The result:** the refresh is proved under the same conditions in which `compareUntracked`'s memo would answer "unchanged". It skips `FlushPending`, the collect, `ProvedKeys`, the block generations, `ChangedBlocks` and the compare.
- **Behaviour:** the outcome is unchanged, so this is a speed change. The switch `APS5_NO_UNTRACKED_REFRESH_PROOF=1` is kept as an escape hatch and documented at `docs/dev/ENVIRONMENT.md:320`.

**Test:** `tests/execution/DccKeysFirstWrite.cpp:604-611`, in `RunUntrackedReuse`, ctest `agc_driver_dcc_keys_first_write`.
- The existing case imports an unwatched image and refreshes it three times without a store.
- It now also requires `StorageTexture::RefreshesProved()` to rise by at least 2 over the refreshes after the first.

## 5. Next cut

**`Recorder::NewestWriteNote` (`Graphics/src/Recorder.cpp:3094` on 33f3b3105)** scans the open batch and every in-flight batch's write notes linearly.
- **How much it scans:** about 250 in-flight batches per call on lavapipe, from the 375 batches seen per draw over 1.5 calls.
- **Who calls it:** the cut's proof check calls it on every proved refresh, and `compareUntracked` on every compare.
- **What it costs:**
  - at `--cost 20`, ~2.3 µs per draw on the floor arm and 3.9 on arm A, both profiled;
  - in an earlier perf profile (`perf record -e cpu-clock`), 4% of committer CPU at 40 frames and 23% at 200 frames, with the floor's frame time growing from 48 to 80 ms.
- **The fix:** a per-range index of the newest note, updated in `noteWriteOn` and dropped when a batch retires, would make the call O(1).
- **Size:** that is small, so on its own it would not reach 20 µs.

**After it, by size on the real path:**
- worker decode, 10.5–17 µs per draw and malloc-heavy. It only helps once the committer is no longer the bottleneck.
- the committer's recording (pass begin/continue and push constants, 7–8 µs on the floor arm).

**On arm A,** the image-table and descriptor `resources` path (~320 µs per draw) dwarfs the per-packet tail. That needs the BF-037 heap ceiling route, not a tail cut.
