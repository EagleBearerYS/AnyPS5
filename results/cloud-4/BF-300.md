**"now"**

- Title CPU stores, shader stores, copies and fills do not count.

**The store history ("stores newest first")**
- `Driver::labelStores`, an `std::unordered_map<std::uint64_t, std::array<LabelStore, 4>>` (`Driver.hpp:463`; `LabelStoreHistory = 4` in `Execution/include/Driver/Synchronization/LabelStores.hpp:9`).
- **Key:** the 4-byte-aligned guest address of each dword.
- **Capacity:** the newest 4 stores per dword (`std::shift_right` in `Synchronization/LabelHistory.cpp:16-21`), for an unlimited number of dwords.
- **Eviction:** none. Nothing erases or clears `labelStores` anywhere in the tree.
- **Filter:** `KeepsLabelStore` (`LabelStores.hpp:12-14`) drops stores longer than 64 bytes or not 4-aligned.
- It is fed only by `noteLabelStore` (`LabelHistory.cpp:12`), from the 9 call sites in step 2. **"none recorded"** therefore means: no recorded writer has stored to this dword since the driver started.
- The waiter can end from it: `storedSince` (`LabelHistory.cpp:70-90`) takes a recorded store with stamp > `received` whose value satisfies the wait (`WaitMemory.cpp:141, 150, 346`).

**"recorder: table miss (refusal 0)"**
- This is not the store history. It is the GPU recorder's pending-label table: `VulkanDevice::PendingLabel` → `Recorder::PendingLabel` → `lookupLabel` (`Graphics/src/Recorder.cpp:3428-3500`).
- The table holds label stores recorded into GPU batches (`Recorder::NoteLabel`, from `Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:2582`) and queued labels (`NoteQueuedLabel`, `Recorder.cpp:1357`).
- An entry goes when its batch is retired (`removeLabels`, 4271-4293) or when overwritten (3417).
- **"miss"** means no store to this dword is waiting in an unfinished batch.
- **"refusal 0"** is `LabelRefusal::None` (`Graphics/include/Recorder.hpp:330`, names in `Execution/include/Driver/Synchronization/WaitMemory.hpp:13`): a plain miss, not a refused late entry.

**"write generation N seen M"**
- N is `Recorder::writeGeneration` (`Recorder.cpp:519`, read at 1262). It is bumped only by GPU bookkeeping:
  - every write the recorder notes into an open batch (`noteWrite` 2990, `noteWriteOn` 3018);
  - every close of a label group (`CloseLabelGroup` 1354).
- It does **not** count CPU write-watch events.
- M is the waiter's last consulted generation (`WaitMemory.cpp:200, 246-293`). **N == M** means the recorder noted no GPU write since the waiter last looked at its table.

**The other fields**
- **"open batch writes it":** `Recorder::OpenWriteOverlaps` (3137).
- **"snapshot write overlaps":** the published union of in-flight batches' write ranges (`Recorder::SnapshotWriteOverlaps`, 1407).
- **"pending completion labels":** labels stored by completion actions.
- **"write-back completions":** copied-buffer and storage write-backs queued as completion actions (`OnComplete` 2971-2976; decremented at retire, 4134/4279).
- **"completions pending":** either of the two counts above nonzero (`DeferredLabels.cpp:79-81`).
- **"queue 0 dormant":** queue 0's worker parked idle (`Queues/QueueWorker.cpp:204-228`).
- **"label due":** the label-flush deadline passed (`DeferredLabels.cpp:73-77`).
- **"earlier write by queue Q opcode O at A+L":** the ring `writeHistory()` (16384 entries shared by all queues, `LabelHistory.cpp:123`). It is filled by `traceLabel` (92-121) for every executed RELEASE_MEM, WRITE_DATA, COPY_DATA and DMA_DATA target (`PacketExecution.cpp:278, 366`). WRITE_DATA and COPY_DATA are entered with length 4 whatever their size; COND_WRITE and DUMP_CONST_RAM are not entered.

The slot was freshly taken from the title's pool: values of ±0x20, −0x1920 and so on look like free-list links to neighbouring nodes. ## 4. The recorded row (+81.5 s, label 0x1020fad980)

**The history:** a pair from queue 0x0, DMA_DATA 0 (stamp 232049, W6 or W7) then RELEASE_MEM 1 (232050, W1-W5). The same pair came 45,419 stamps earlier (186630/186631). That looks like one use of the slot per cycle.

**The wait:** submitted at 233456, 1,406 stamps after the 1. The slot held 0x21036100 at the check and still at timeout. No store after 232050.

**Does our queue 0x0 RELEASE_MEM land when recorded?** Only with outcome 1. With 0 it lands when its batch runs; with 5 at the batch's retirement (step 2). At timeout the table missed, so no store of this dword was still in an unfinished batch. If the 1 went to the GPU, its batch had finished by then: it landed, unless it landed into memory the guest address no longer maps.

**Why the 1 does not end this wait:** `storedSince` takes only stores with a stamp greater than `received` (`LabelHistory.cpp:77`). Both recorded stores are older than the submission.

**So, for this row:**
- The 1 belonged to the slot's previous use.
- Between 232050 and 233456, something the history doesn't record replaced it with 0x21036100: a link word, so most likely the title's CPU (W16) freeing the slot to its pool.
- The slot was then taken again, without being initialized, for this wait. That wait needs the next DMA 0 / RELEASE 1 pair, which never came within the timeout.

**Could our queue 0x20 reach the wait after the title has already reused the slot?**
- Our submit returns after enqueue (`Submission.cpp:467-470`) without waiting for execution, as on hardware. The title's CPU then runs freely. Nothing in the driver lets queue 0x20's later packets (a label or an end-of-pipe signal after the wait) run before `waitMemory` returns: the worker executes packets in order (`PacketExecution.cpp:454-457`).
- For this wait, though, the reuse is visible at the title's own submit (outcome "checked", value 0x21036100), before queue 0x20 had the packet. For a direct submit, that makes the reuse earlier than anything our queue could be late for.
- The REWIND-tail exception from step 3 applies here too.

**Verdict:**
- **(b)** for the overwrite of the old 1, by an unrecorded writer, most likely the title's CPU. L2 confirms it with a CPU stamp on the block between the stamp-232050 store and the check.
- For the timeout itself, the same as the other rows: the next producer was not issued. Can't tell from code; L3 tells.
- (a) excluded. (c) excluded for a direct submit, open for a REWIND tail (L1).
