## Step 2: the bound (3797ec87b)

**Accounting:**
- **What is counted:** each CPU-path indirect entry is marked (`DrawEntry::cpuIndirect`) when it is inserted. It counts:
  - its variants' `variantBytes` (`ownBytes`);
  - its decode, every distinct `RecompileResult` (struct and vectors), every distinct SPIR-V module (`spirv.Words()`) and every distinct `ShaderMemory` (pages, `ShaderMemory::HeldBytes()`).
- **Refcounting:** the shared parts are refcounted across all CPU-path entries (`cpuIndirectPins`), so a result or module that several entries share is counted once. Bytes are added when the first entry pins it and removed when the last one goes.
- **Snapshots:** each entry keeps a snapshot of what it pinned, so the release matches the pin even if a variant changes later.

**Admission:**
- An insert pins the new entry and releases the one it replaces.
- If the total would then exceed the budget, both steps are undone and the entry is refused (`cpuIndirectRefused`). Nothing else is evicted for it.
- The draw itself still runs on the uncached CPU path, the same as with the switch off.

**Sweep:**
- Every lookup advances a serial, in both the register-key path and the classic lookup.
- A hit stamps the entry's `lastUse`.
- On a CPU-path insert, at most once per quarter of the idle window, entries idle for more than 4 × `APS5_DRAW_CACHE_ENTRIES` lookups (16,384 by default) are dropped (`cpuIndirectSwept`).
- A cycle hits all its resident entries every pass, so they never go idle. When the draws change, the old entries go idle and make room.

**Default budget: 768 MiB.**
- **The harness:** its 2,550-entry, 850-draw cycle measured 46 MiB, so it fits whole under the default with 0 refusals. Any budget at or under 1 GB gives at most 1 GB there.
- **Village:** with the PC's figure of about 325 KB per entry, 768 MiB holds about 2,400 entries. That is close to the harness's 3 × 850 cycle.
- **Why 768 and not 1,024:** it leaves a quarter for what the count leaves out, such as allocator overhead and pipeline objects, against the ≤1 GB aim.
- **Overriding it:** `APS5_CPU_INDIRECT_CACHE_KIB` (KiB).

**Other changes:**
- `APS5_PROFILE_DRAW`'s CPU-path line now also gives the entries held, the bytes against the budget, and the refusals and sweeps.
- With the switch off, no entry is marked, so nothing is counted, refused or swept. Erase and LRU eviction now share one helper (`dropDrawEntry`) and behave as before.

## Step 3: the test and its red/green

**`agc_driver_indirect_draw_cache_budget`** (`tests/execution/IndirectDrawCacheBudget.cpp`, registered by the execution-test glob):
- **Settings:** `APS5_CACHE_CPU_INDIRECT=1`, `APS5_CPU_INDIRECT_CACHE_KIB=64` and `APS5_DRAW_CACHE_ENTRIES=64` (an idle window of 256 lookups).
- **The cycle:** 48 distinct CPU-path indirect packets (the fixture's pushed vertex program, which differ in a third user word). Every draw must draw the reference pixels.
- **The two checks:**
  - after every cycle, the counted bytes must be ≤ the budget;
  - after 2 warm-up cycles, each of 3 cycles must make at least budget ÷ (bytes per entry) − 2 hits.
- **Then a new set of 48 packets runs for 12 cycles:** its last cycle must reach the same hit count. That needs the old cycle's idle entries swept.

**Red:** 3797ec87b with the bound's enforcement removed (the sweep call and the refuse-and-undo block in `insertDrawEntry`, the counting kept):

> `1/1 Test #501: agc_driver_indirect_draw_cache_budget ...***Failed` — "the CPU-path indirect draw-cache entries of scene 1 hold 208864 bytes in 48 entries, over the budget of 65536 bytes"

**Green:** 3797ec87b as committed passes, printing:
- "a cycle of 48 CPU-path indirect draws over a budget that holds 13 of them kept its hits (13 of 48 per cycle) within the budget"
- "a new cycle replaced the idle entries of the old one and made 13 hits per cycle"

**The hit rate against the budget on the harness cycle** (exp/bf058-measure 4612e14d6, `DRAW_RECORD_COST_INDIRECT=1 DRAW_RECORD_COST_GPU_SYNC=1`, 6 warm-up and 30 measured frames, or 12 for 2,048):

| draws per frame (cycle) | budget | entries held, counted bytes | refused | hits over the measured frames | budget ÷ cycle | median µs per draw packet | RssAnon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 850 (2,550) | default 768 MiB | 2,540, 45.99 MiB | 0 | 99.4% | whole cycle fits | 73.0 | 135,708 kB |
| 850 (2,550) | 24 MiB | 1,320, 23.99 MiB | 13,363 | **51.8%** | 24 / 46.0 MiB = 52%; 1,320 / 2,550 = 51.8% | 99.5 | 135,556 kB |
| 2,048 (6,144) | default 768 MiB | 4,096 (the entry bound), 74.05 MiB | 0 | **0%** (22,749 inserts) | the byte budget does not bind; the entry LRU thrashes | 146.7 | 189,936 kB |
| 2,048 (6,144) | 40 MiB | 2,207, 39.99 MiB | 17,869 | **35.9%** | 2,207 / 6,144 = 35.9% | 111.2 | 136,028 kB |

**A gap the bound leaves:** the existing 4,096-entry LRU still misses on every draw of a cycle over 4,096 entries when the byte budget doesn't bind first. In the harness that happens at 18.5 KiB per entry, as 4,096 × 18.5 KiB = 74 MiB is under 768 MiB. With Village's estimated 325 KB per entry, the byte budget binds first at about 2,400 entries. I left the entry bound's LRU as it is, because it is main's behaviour for every draw.
