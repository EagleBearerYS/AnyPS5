## What the line gains

Everything is behind `APS5_DRAW_KEY_CENSUS=1`. With it off, the new hooks are one check of the census's static flag each, as BF-287's are; there are no clock reads and no lock.

1. **Uncacheable by reason.** `resolveDrawResources` counts each uncacheable lookup once, under the first reason that applies, in the task's order: `not recordable`, `no variant id`, `bda pagetable`, `fault buffer` (a FaultBuffer binding under the loop guard), `switch` (`APS5_NO_DRAW_RESOURCE_CACHE` / `APS5_NO_TEXTURE_CACHE`).
   - `not recordable` is split by the first condition `prepareDraw` finds false, the conditions its `recordable` is computed from (`Graphics/src/Draw.cpp`):
     - `sync draws` (`APS5_SYNC_DRAWS`, `RecordDraws()` false);
     - `no recorder`;
     - `dump target` (`DumpTargetLimit() != 0`);
     - for the first color target that is neither resident nor multisampled, why it is not resident: `resident targets off` (`APS5_NO_RESIDENT_TARGETS`), `no detiler`, `resident linear off` (`APS5_NO_RESIDENT_LINEAR`), or `linear target not imported` (no existing host import, and the range not watched or its import refused).
   - These are the only ways that target loop leaves a single-sample target without a resident image.
2. **Their cost.** Two `steady_clock` reads around the `ShaderResources` construction, census only.
   - `build us:` gives the total per reason for the line's frame (or window);
   - `cacheable misses` gives the same for cacheable lookups that built;
   - `hits` is from the key's computation through `Revalidate` and the moved-buffer check.
3. **Who they are.** Every 10 s, the 8 VS/PS code-address pairs with the most uncacheable lookups, with their counts and reasons.
   - **Where it prints:** on a `10 s window N` line it is appended to that line. While frames flip, it prints as its own `[draw-key] 10 s window N: uncacheable shader sets …` line every 10 s, because `10 s window` lines otherwise appear only when nothing flips.
   - **Where the addresses come from:** `Graphics` does not know a program's guest address, so the draw path records program → `binary.codeAddress` while the census runs. It does so where it sets a stage's program: the normal stage list, the mesh-barycentric replacement, and the per-draw program copies of CPU-path indirect draws.
   - **Unknown programs:** a program recorded nowhere (another path) prints as `?`, and all such programs are counted together.
4. **The rest of the shape key.** For draws whose shader set repeats the previous frame's but whose shape key does not:
   - `NoteDrawKeyCensus` lists the shape key's own registers: the `DrawKeyRegisters` masks of the context, shader and user-config banks, without the user words, so exactly what `RegisterStateKey` fingerprints for the shape.
   - The census compares them with the draw at the same position among that shader set's draws in the previous frame, and prints the top 8 as `ctx|sh|uc 0xOFF:count`.
   - Storage is capped at 4,096 draws a frame, 256 per shader set.
5. **Consistent counts.** The resource hit, miss and uncacheable counts are updated together with the new fields under the census lock. So in every line the reasons add up to `uncacheable`, the conditions to `not recordable`, and the timed misses and hits to the counted ones.

  - the not-recordable conditions add up to `not recordable`;
  - the timed misses and hits equal the counted ones;
  - register comparisons ≤ shader-set-only repeats ≤ shader-set repeats;
