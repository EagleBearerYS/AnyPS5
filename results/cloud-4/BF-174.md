# BF-174: unwatched depth-plane compares on a frame-shaped stream (cloud-4)

## Answer

- **The bounded head (e35998524) is not negligible.** At 1920x1080 with K = 4 it costs **+3.5 ms per frame** of driver bind work over main, for 6 compares per frame:
  - 4 from the writing binds;
  - 2 in the store, one of which main already makes.

  With K = 12 it costs +9.3 ms. BF-171 unbounded (1a4f9e022) costs +6.7 ms at K = 4 and +12.2 ms at K = 12.
- **A writing-case bound was needed, and it is built** on fork `fix/agc-depth-plane-unwatched-writes-bounded2`, head **b6003ec4b**.
  - It brings the frame to **3 compares per frame for every K**: the first writing bind of each present plus the store's 2.
  - Cost over main: **+1.4 to +1.65 ms** at 1080p and +0.55 to +0.66 ms at 720p.
  - It keeps every listed case green, including (a3) `write-order` and main's `partial_store`.
- **It is still over 0.5 ms per frame at 1080p.** Two whole-plane compares per frame remain beyond main's one, about 0.75 to 0.95 ms each for an 8.8 MB plane on this VM:
  - the first depth-writing bind of each present;
  - the store's sync.

  The task keeps both: the store still compares, and the first writing bind's compare is what lets the frame's first draw see a CPU write made before it. See "What is left" below.

## The harness

**Plane and frame.** A D32 plane (`kZ64KBX`) at 1280x720 or 1920x1080 is stored once with depth 0.5 (`StoreHalf`); after that store it is outside the write watch. Each frame then runs:

1. **Pre-pass:** K packets. Each is a new collect epoch with one depth-writing bind or draw (LESS_OR_EQUAL at 0.25, depth write on).
2. **Store:** in a new collect epoch, `FlushDepthPlanes(plane, bytes)` with the default (`Overlapped`) scope. That is the call a texture lookup over the whole plane makes (`ShaderResources.cpp:1140`). It is timed.
3. **Wait:** a `WaitIdle`, timed as a GPU wait.
4. **Read-only passes:** 4 packets, each a new collect epoch, binding the plane read-only (LESS_OR_EQUAL, no write).
5. **Wait and present:** a `WaitIdle`, then `Recorder::CountPresent()`.

**Counters.** Compares are `StorageTexture::UntrackedCompares()` deltas around each phase, and syncs are `DepthPlaneStoreCounts().syncs`. Each run is 5 warm-up frames and then 60 measured frames, summarised as medians.

**The same frame runs as two streams:**
- **binds:** each packet is the `DepthSurfaceView(context, target, written)` call a draw makes (`Draw.cpp:2388`, `:3025`), under `GpuMutex`, as `--bind-cost` does. This is the driver work the task asks for. GPU waits (the `WaitIdle`s) are timed separately.
- **draws:** each packet is a real `device.Draw` through `DepthFastClearHarness::Record`.
  - On lavapipe a 1080p draw with a guest-memory color target does about 12 to 17 ms of work inside the `Draw` call, so this stream's "without waits" figure is mostly lavapipe.
  - It is reported for context only. Its compare counts match the bind stream exactly.

**A CPU-write frame comes last.** A CPU write of 0.125 into 4 texels is made before the pre-pass. Its first draw must pass at texels − 4, and the store must keep the 0.125. Main reports "lost" there and exits 1, by design.

**Three details decide what the counts mean:**
1. **The store needs its own collect epoch.** `compareUntracked` memoizes per collect epoch (`Texture.cpp:2551`). In the first version the store shared the last pre-pass packet's epoch, so its sync compare was a memo hit and was not counted. A real store comes from another packet (the SSAO dispatch), so it now bumps the epoch.
2. **The store needs a wait after it.** A store's bytes become the saved bytes only when its batch completes (debt entry "Pending storage-image results over memory outside the write watch").
   - Without a wait, the bind stream's read-only binds on BF-171 found nothing to compare (0 of 4). The draw stream compared 3 of 4, because lavapipe completed the batch.
   - A real GPU finishes the store during the next packets, so a timed wait follows the store. Both streams now agree.
3. **Main's store already compares once.** `FlushPending` reaches `StorageTexture::writeBack` and then `writeBackLayers`, which compares the stored units with their saved bytes before writing them back over memory outside the watch (`Texture.cpp:5455`, not memoized). Every build shows it as one of the store's compares.

**How the four builds were made.** They are the same harness with `DepthSurface.cpp` swapped, as BF-173 did for its mutants:

- **BF-171:** 1a4f9e022's file.

## The writing-case bound (b6003ec4b)

**The rule, in `DepthSurface.cpp` on b6003ec4b:**
- **The flags:** `DepthSurfaceView` calls `SyncFromMemory(mirrorResource(target), !depthWritten && !cleared, depthWritten && !cleared)` (`:2050`). That is `binding` for read-only binds and `writing` for depth-writing binds that don't clear.
- **The skip:** for a plane outside the write watch, `WrittenUnits` returns "nothing written" without comparing when either holds (`:865`):
  - `binding` and the plane was compared earlier in this present (e35998524's rule);
  - `writing` and `unwatchedWritten` equals this present.
- **The mark:** a writing bind that compares sets `unwatchedWritten = present` (`:867`). Both `ClearStale` overloads reset it (`:817`, `:821`).
  - So the skip means: a depth-writing bind of this present compared, the plane was marked all stale right after it, and no unit has been stored, reloaded or retired since.
- **Still compare, unchanged:**
  - stores (`FlushDepthPlanes`' sync);
  - retirements (`retireStoringStale`, `OverwrittenInMemory`);
  - binds that clear;
  - the first depth-writing bind of each present;
  - the first depth-writing bind after any store.
- **The cost it removes:** K − 1 compares per frame.

**The ordering this gives up, written into a new debt entry ("Depth planes outside the write watch", `docs/dev/debt/libSceAgcDriver.md:87`, 886 characters):**
- **The case:** a CPU write made between two depth-writing draws of one present is collected only at the store.
- **The effect:** the later draw tests against the GPU's depth, and at the store the units the CPU wrote keep the CPU's bytes over that later draw's depth. This is the "CPU write after the GPU's writes in the same present" case the task named.
- **Also in the entry:** e35998524's read-only rule, which was not described anywhere.

**Tests,** new cases in `DepthPlaneBufferMemory.cpp`, registered in the existing `agcUnwatchedDepthCase` loop:
- **`agc_driver_depth_plane_unwatched_write-bound`** (`WriteBound`, `:319`). Eight `DepthSurfaceView(…, true)` binds per run, each in a new collect epoch, must compare:
  - 1, then 0, in one present;
  - 0 in 8 read-only binds after them;
  - 1 in the next present;
  - after a store (which must compare), 1 again.

  Output on b6003ec4b: `8 depth-writing binds per run, each in a new collect epoch: 1, 0 (same present), then 0 in 8 binds only testing depth, 1 (next present); a store: 2 comparisons, then 1 in 8 depth-writing binds`.
- **`agc_driver_depth_plane_unwatched_write-after-store`** (`WriteAfterStore`, `:259`): a depth-writing draw, then a store, then a CPU write, then a depth-writing draw, all in one present. The draw must see the write (pass LESS at texels − 4).
- **`agc_driver_depth_plane_unwatched_write-after-read`** (`WriteAfterRead`, `:270`): a depth-writing draw, then a present, then a read-only draw (which compares), then a CPU write, then a depth-writing draw. The draw must see the write.

## What is left above 0.5 ms, for the PM's decision

- **The floor:** bounded2 makes 2 whole-plane compares per frame that main doesn't, however large K is:
  - the first depth-writing bind of each present;
  - the store's sync.

  At about 0.75 to 0.95 ms each for 1920x1080 D32 on this VM, that is about 1.5 to 1.9 ms per frame. At 1280x720 it is about 0.6 ms.
- **Dropping either one changes behaviour:**
  - **Without the first writing bind's compare:** a CPU write made between frames is first seen at the store. The pre-pass draws then test against stale depth, and the CPU's units win over the whole pre-pass. That is the CPU-write frame's first-draw check, red.
  - **Without the store's compare:** a CPU write after the pre-pass in the same present is overwritten by the store. The task keeps it.
- **Not built, only observed:** the store compares the same bytes twice in one `FlushDepthPlanes` call. One compare is the sync's (`DepthSurface.cpp:2142`, memoized). The other is main's `writeBackLayers` compare (`Texture.cpp:5455`, not memoized), made after the surfaces lock is released.
  - Sharing them would save one compare per frame (about 0.75 to 0.95 ms at 1080p) and leave bounded2 at about +0.75 to 0.95 ms. That is still above 0.5 ms at 1080p.
  - It changes main's own store path, so I didn't make it.
- **The conclusion:** with compares that read the whole plane, no bound that keeps the store's compare and one compare per present gets under 0.5 ms at 1080p on this machine. Going lower needs a cheaper way to detect writes, such as a narrower compare or a write hint, not a tighter bound.
