## 1. The fix

- **The eviction.** `insertDrawEntry` (`Draw/DrawCache.cpp:178`) now loops while `drawCache.size() > drawCacheEntries() || (drawCache.size() > 1 && drawCacheVariantBytes > DrawCacheBytes())`. That is the dispatch cache's condition (`Dispatch/DispatchInsert.cpp:128`).
  - The loop body is unchanged: variants unaccounted, `traceKeysEvicted`, the `drawShapes` entry dropped if it names the victim, `drawOrder`, `drawCacheEvictions`, `counters.evictions`.
  - An eviction counts as a byte eviction when the entry count was already within the cap (`overBytes`). Those increment `drawCacheByteEvictions` (process total) and `DrawEntryCounters::byteEvictions` (per report).
  - `size() > 1` keeps the entry just inserted, which is at the LRU front. A single entry bigger than the budget therefore stays, as in the dispatch cache.
- **`DrawCacheBytes()`** reads `APS5_DRAW_CACHE_BYTES` once. The default is 256 MiB, and 0 keeps it, as with `DispatchCacheBytes()`. It is a public static, like `DispatchCacheBytes()`.
- **The `[draw-cache]` lines:**
  - profile: `... 126 entries (252 variants, ~254.3 MiB of a 256.0 MiB budget), 1473 evictions in total (1473 over the byte budget), ...`;
  - trace: `..., %zu entries, %llu evicted (%llu over the byte budget)`.
- **`Driver::DrawCacheSize()`** returns `{entries, variantBytes}` under `drawCacheMutex`. It is the twin of `DispatchCacheSize()`, for the test.
- **The `ENVIRONMENT.md` row:** `APS5_DRAW_CACHE_BYTES`, integer, `DrawCache.cpp`, 268435456.
- **No debt entry.** Nothing here is unimplemented or guessed. It is a bound with a documented knob.
- **Shared budget or a second one? A second one fits better:**
  - The caches have their own mutexes (`dispatchCacheMutex`, `drawCacheMutex`) and their own LRU lists. One budget would need a cross-cache victim policy, plus a lock or atomic across both inserts.
  - With one budget, a phase heavy in one kind of work (compute post-processing, or a draw-heavy menu) would evict the other cache's entries. One cache's churn would become the other's misses.
  - Worst case with two budgets is 512 MiB, and each is tunable. A shared budget only wins when one cache sits nearly empty while the other wants more than 256 MiB. Nothing measured here shows that.

## 2. `variantBytes`: what it covers

Before, it counted the **sizes** of `words`, `shared`, `runs` and `captured`, plus `sizeof(DispatchVariant)` and `vertexInfo`. After, it counts:

| held by a variant | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `runs`, `words`, `shared`, `captured` | size | capacity |
| `dataPositions`, `dataSlots`, `runBases`, `shaderDataPatches`, `spans` | not counted | capacity |
| `memory` (`ShaderMemory`) | not counted | `ShaderMemory::Bytes()`: object, `initial`/`pages`/`supplied`/`spilled` capacities, `pages.size() × sizeof(Page)` (4 KiB words + 3 word masks, ~4.4 KiB per page) |
| `sizeof(DispatchVariant)`, `vertexInfo` | yes | yes |

- **Draw variants keep no `ShaderMemory`.** `memory`, `captured` and `spans` are set only in `Dispatch/DispatchInsert.cpp:56-66`, under `APS5_DISPATCH_STAMP_VALIDATE`. For those dispatch variants, the pages were the bulk and were uncounted: a 2 MiB capture keeps about 2.2 MiB of pages.
- **No cached variant changes size after insert.** I checked:
  - `recordStageDataPositions` resizes before the insert;
  - `applyShaderDataPatches` only `clear()`s, which keeps the capacity;
  - relocation copies are new objects;
  - a stamp-validated dispatch's `ShaderMemory` is only read after `insertDispatch` (it is parked for the device run).

  So the add and the remove compute the same number.
- **Measured per draw variant** (scratch `fprintf` in `accountDrawVariant`, not committed):
  - **2 MiB table, records back to back (the test's table):** before 2,105,744 B, after 2,114,976 B (+0.4%). 524,296 words at capacity 524,304; 513 runs at capacity 1,024; 513 run bases, which were not counted before.
  - **Stride-48 table, 32-byte records (the test file's `WideStride` table; 32,768 records):** before 1,579,408 B, after 2,106,272 B. **The old count covered 75%:**
    - `words` holds 393,224 words in a capacity of 524,304: `tagVariantRuns` grows it with a `resize` per region and no `reserve`;
    - 385 runs sit at capacity 512.
  - **Vertex variant:** 1,400 B before and after.
- **Still not counted:**
  - `compiled` (shared with the recompile memo), `shader`, `recipe` and `vertexInfo`'s inner vectors;
  - the `DrawEntry` and its `DrawDecode`;
  - the `SrtGuestWords` blocks: `shared` only holds weak pointers to them, while a `ShaderMemory`'s `supplied`/`spilled` lists keep them alive, shared with the materializer;
  - allocator overhead.
- **Follow-up, not in this change:** `tagVariantRuns` (`Draw/DrawRelocate.cpp:87-109`) could reserve the total word count first, as `DispatchInsert.cpp:33-34` does. On the stride-48 table that cuts a variant's `words` from 526,328 to 393,224 words of capacity (−25%).

## 3. The test

**Where:** `SrtMovingPerDraw` in `tests/execution/ImageTableCacheBounds.cpp`, beside `SrtMovingPerDispatch`. It runs in the same no-argument invocation, ctest `agc_driver_image_table_cache_bounds`. No new ctest name, so no WIN32 `PATH` change.

**Setup.** It reuses the 2 MiB table: 65,536 records × 32 bytes over 17 surfaces.
- Pixel shader (20 words, assembled with llvm-mc 18, gfx1030 wave64):
  - load the heap V# and the S# from the SRT in `s[0:1]`;
  - `v_mbcnt_lo` → `v_readfirstlane` → `s_lshl 5`;
  - `s_buffer_load_dwordx8` T#, then `image_sample_lz`, then `exp mrt0`.

  The index comes from a lane value, so the capture takes the whole table.
- Vertex shader and context are `DrawCacheReuse`'s `TriangleVertexCode` setup.
- The color target is the dispatch case's output page.

**Keys.** 64, as 64-byte SRT slots over the dispatch case's 4 KiB SRT area. Key *k*'s heap V# starts *k* records later, with *Records − k* records, so every key captures its own ~2 MiB and relocation cannot pair them.
- Each key is drawn twice, because a key is inserted on its second sighting (`admitDrawKey`).
- `SetBudgets` sets `APS5_DRAW_CACHE_BYTES=16777216`, as it does `APS5_DISPATCH_CACHE_BYTES`.

**Checks:**
- after every draw, `DrawCacheSize().variantBytes <= DrawCacheBytes()`;
- the color is 3, from the T# surface;
- ≥2 entries kept and ≥32 inserts;
- inserts × average entry size > 2 × the budget, so the entry cap alone would have kept over twice the budget;
- `byteEvictions >= 1` and `byteEvictions == evictions`.

**Hit after eviction.** All records are rewritten to surface 9 and key 0, long evicted, is drawn:
- it must be looked up absent and inserted again, and write 9;
- a second draw of key 0 must hit and write 9.

**Departure from the task text.** It asked for keys enough that the entry cap alone would keep "well over 256 MiB". I kept the dispatch case's in-process 16 MiB budget, so the case runs in seconds. The entry cap alone would keep 64 × ~2.0 MiB ≈ 129 MiB, 8× the test's budget. At the default 256 MiB budget it would take over 128 keys and 256+ draws of 2 MiB captures.

**Green, on 8aa9d0a44:** `image table draw cache bounds passed (64 inserts, 57 evictions, 57 over the byte budget, at most 7 entries holding 14521 KiB)`, then `image table cache bounds tests passed`. Exit 0, with ANYPS5_REQUIRE_VULKAN=1, GALLIUM_OVERRIDE_CPU_CAPS=avx, ANYPS5_NO_SHADER_CACHE=1 and APS5_NO_PIPELINE_JOURNAL=1. The whole binary takes 3.3 s; CTestCostData had 0.26 s before this case.

**Red, on 8aa9d0a44 with only the loop condition put back to `while (drawCache.size() > drawCacheEntries())`, test unchanged:** exit 1 with `AGC graphics: image table draw cache bounds: after draw 15 the draw cache kept 8 entries holding 16580 KiB, over its budget of 16384 KiB`.
