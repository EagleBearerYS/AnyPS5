### The reader commit (692a02868)

`TrophyPackage.cpp` `ParseTrophyPackage` now:
- builds the fallback list as `std::array{language, "en-US", defaultLanguage}`;
- skips an empty candidate, and one that repeats an earlier candidate;
- stops at the first that has a `tropmeta_<tag>.json`;
- otherwise names, joined by a new `alternatives` helper ("a, b or c"), the candidates it tried. The default keeps its label `its defaultLanguage "<tag>"`.

`<algorithm>` is added for `std::find`. The fallback order is unchanged.

| console language, defaultLanguage | message (PM-073) | message (now) |
|---|---|---|
| de-DE, ja-JP | for de-DE, en-US or its defaultLanguage "ja-JP" | the same (the existing check) |
| en-US, en-US | for en-US, en-US or its defaultLanguage "en-US" | for en-US |
| en-US, ja-JP | for en-US, en-US or its defaultLanguage "ja-JP" | for en-US or its defaultLanguage "ja-JP" |
| de-DE, en-US | for de-DE, en-US or its defaultLanguage "en-US" | for de-DE or en-US |

**Tests:** `tests/TrophyPackage.cpp` checks the last three rows by exact equality.
- With PM-073's `TrophyPackage.cpp` swapped in, `np_trophy2_package` aborts with "a missing text named en-US more than once".
- With the new one, it passes (0.46 s).
