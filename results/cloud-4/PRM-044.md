**A created context with a readable `app0/sce_sys/trophy2/trophyNN.ucp`** (NN its service label). The first query reads the file, up to 256 MiB, and prints one `[trophy] …: N trophies, M groups, texts in <tag>` line. Then:

- **`GetGroupInfoArray`:** every group from the offset, where main gave one placeholder.
- **`GetTrophyInfo`:**
