What it does:
- When a title unlocks a trophy, a pop-up shows for 5 s: `TROPHY UNLOCKED` over `TROPHY <id>`, such as `TROPHY 12`. The name and grade will show once PM-017 provides them.
- It is drawn in the corner opposite the performance overlay's, in the overlay's font, scale and palette.
- It works whether or not the overlay is shown.
- Several unlocks queue.
- `ANYPS5_TROPHY_POPUP=0` turns it off.
- While no pop-up is due, the present records exactly what it did before.

## 1. Design

File:line references are at f2e8ad52e, under core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/.

**From an unlock to the queue** (Execution/src/TrophyPopup.cpp)
- `ListenForTrophyUnlocks()` (:172) registers one listener with `NpTrophy2AddUnlockListener_nid_no_patch`, the hook PM-016 added for this follow-up.
  - VulkanDevice calls it as soon as its presentation pass exists (Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:1851). That covers every title that opens a window, whether or not the overlay is ever shown.
  - A function-local static makes it run once per process.
  - With `ANYPS5_TROPHY_POPUP=0` it registers nothing.
- PM-016's store calls the listener only for a new unlock, that is, when `NpTrophy2RecordUnlock_nid_no_patch` returns true.
  - It calls it on the game thread that posted `_UnlockTrophy`, outside the store's locks.
  - Unlocking the same trophy again shows nothing.
- The listener (`queueUnlock`, :39) only pushes `TrophyPopupEntry{trophyId, name, grade}` onto the queue, under the queue's mutex. Until PM-017's trophy data exists, the name is empty and the grade 0, so the pop-up shows the id.
- The queue (`TrophyPopups()`, :167) is allocated once and never destroyed. So no static destructor can race with an unlock posted during exit.

**Queue and timing** (`TrophyPopupQueue`: Execution/include/TrophyPopup.hpp:42; TrophyPopup.cpp:139-160)
- `Push` appends the entry and sets an atomic `pending` flag.
- `Current(now)` returns the front entry.
  - Its 5 s (`ShowFor`) start at the first present that asks for it, not at the unlock. So an unlock that arrives while no frame is being presented is still shown for the full 5 s.
  - After 5 s the entry is popped, and the next entry's 5 s start at that same present.
  - When the queue is empty, `pending` is cleared.
- So simultaneous unlocks show one after another, 5 s each, in unlock order.
- The caller passes the time in (`steady_clock`), which is how the tests drive the queue with a fake clock.

**Present path and idle cost** (VulkanDevice.cpp:2965-2967, :3059, :3097-3105)
- Each present reads `TrophyPopups().Pending()`, a single relaxed atomic load. The present takes the queue's mutex and asks for the current entry only when that flag is set.
- `decorated` is true when the overlay is visible or a pop-up is current.
- When `decorated` is false, the present is recorded exactly as before:
  - the same final layout for the presentation pass (:3059);
  - the same barrier on the blit path;
  - no pop-up command, allocation or lock.
- When `decorated` is true, one `PerformanceOverlay` object draws. It is created on first need, as before. It lays out the panel (only if the overlay is visible), then the pop-up, and records both into the swapchain image in one call.
- So a pop-up shows with the overlay hidden. With the overlay shown, both are drawn, and by default they sit in opposite corners.

**Several layouts in one record** (commit 68aa32dc2; Execution/src/PerformanceOverlay.cpp:802-893)
- A new overload, `Record(…, std::span<const OverlayLayout> layouts)`:
  - copies every layout's rectangles into the slot's vertex buffer, which grows to the larger of the total and 1024 rectangles;
  - creates one image view and one framebuffer;
  - records one LOAD/STORE render pass per layout. Each pass's render area and scissor are that layout's bounds, and it draws that layout's instanced quads with `firstInstance` at the layout's offset in the buffer.
- Only the first pass starts from the caller's initial layout, and only the last pass transitions to the final layout. In between, the image stays COLOR_ATTACHMENT_OPTIMAL.
- Because the render area and scissor bound every pass, nothing outside the pop-up's rectangle (or the panel's) is ever written.
- The two existing `Record` overloads now build their one layout and call the new overload, so the overlay alone records the same commands as before.
- `PanelLayout(extent)` is the layout the 8-argument `Record` used to build internally.

**Lines, text, size and corner** (TrophyPopup.cpp:45-137)
- Lines:
  - `TROPHY UNLOCKED`;
  - the name, or `TROPHY <id>` when the name is empty or blank;
  - `PLATINUM`, `GOLD`, `SILVER` or `BRONZE` for grade P, G, S or B, and no line for any other grade.
- Names pass through `TrophyPopupText`:
  - at most 32 columns; a longer name keeps 29 characters plus `...`;
  - ASCII control characters become spaces;
  - ASCII characters with no glyph become `?`;
  - each UTF-8 character (2 to 4 bytes, validated) becomes one `?`, and each invalid byte its own `?`.
- Size, in units of the scale:
  - width: 6 per column of the longest line, minus 1, plus 3 padding on each side;
  - height: 9 per line, minus 2, plus 3 padding above and below.

  With two lines at scale 2 this is 190x44.
- Scale: the overlay's own rule, max(1, (height + 240) / 480), lowered until the pop-up and its margins fit. That gives 2 at 720p and 1080p, 3 at 1440p, and 5 at 2160p.
- Position: 4 × scale from the corner, as the panel, so 8 px at 1080p. If the window is smaller than the pop-up, it is clamped to 0,0.
- Colors: the overlay's palette. The background rectangle is black at 60% alpha and the text is white.
- Corner: opposite the overlay's (`ANYPS5_OVERLAY_POS`), whether or not the overlay is shown. The overlay defaults to the top left, so the pop-up defaults to the bottom right.

**Font** (commit d3761f862; PerformanceOverlay.cpp:277-423, `AppendOverlayLine` at :425)
- Before, the 5x7 font had digits, `A` to `X` without `J` and `Q`, `.`, `:`, `/` and space.
- Added: `a` to `z`, `J`, `Q`, `Y`, `Z`, and `- + , ; ' " ! ? & ( ) # % _`. That is 44 glyphs, 80 in all with space.
- `findGlyph` returns a pointer or null:
  - `OverlayGlyph` still throws for a character with no glyph;
  - the new `OverlayHasGlyph` asks without throwing, and drives the `?` fallback.
- The panel's text code moved into `AppendOverlayLine`, which the pop-up also uses. It still emits one rectangle per horizontal run of lit pixels.
- The existing glyphs are unchanged, and the panel's existing bit-exact render tests still pass.

**HDR10**
- The pop-up uses the overlay's palette (PerformanceOverlay.cpp:595).
- On an `HDR10_ST2084` swapchain, white is PQ code 594, which is 203 nits (the BT.2408 reference white), whatever the title's own brightness. The background is black at 60%.
- That is fixed and moderate, and `agc_performance_overlay` checks it.

**`ANYPS5_TROPHY_POPUP`** (TrophyPopup.cpp:45-56, :162-165; read once per process)
- Unset, empty or `1`: the default corner.
- `0`: off. No listener, nothing queued, nothing drawn.
- `tl`, `tr`, `bl` or `br`: that corner.
- Anything else stops the start, when the window is created, with `ANYPS5_TROPHY_POPUP must be 1, 0, tl, tr, bl or br, not '<value>'`.

**No `thread_local`**
- The diff adds none: `git diff 0d8df0889 f2e8ad52e | grep thread_local` finds nothing.
- The queue is one heap object (a mutex, a deque and an atomic) and the listener is a plain function, so `HostThreadLocal` is not needed.

**Linking**
- libSceAgcDriver links libSceNpTrophy2 PRIVATE (CMakeLists.txt:305), as PM-016 planned. libSceNpTrophy2 links only libc, so there is no cycle.
- The built `libSceAgcDriver.prx` now lists `NEEDED libSceNpTrophy2.prx` beside `libkernel.prx` and `libc.prx`, found through `RUNPATH $ORIGIN`, the same way as those two (`readelf -d`).
  - So the dynamic loader resolves it to the same libSceNpTrophy2.prx that the title's imports use, as it already does for libc.prx and libkernel.prx.
  - The one test that copies an explicit set of libraries, `loadexec_arguments`, needed libSceNpTrophy2 added to its set (section 4).

  - Mechanism:
    1. The test fills the registration-preparation pool with hardware_concurrency + 2 = 6 blocking jobs. The pool has max(2, hardware_concurrency / 2) = 2 threads here.
    2. It registers the vertex shader.

  - Evidence: with `APS5_TRACE_PREPARATION_WAIT=1`, 60 runs 4 at a time:
    - all 48 passes found the vertex preparation at "position 4 of 5", with both threads already held;
    - all 12 failures found it at "position 5 of 6" or "6 of 7", with one thread or none held yet.
  - Proposed fix, for upstream:
    - Change: the blocking jobs count themselves as they start, and the constructor waits until max(2, hardware_concurrency / 2) of them have.
    - Result: with it, 60 of 60 runs 4 at a time passed, all at "position 4 of 5".
    - I tried it locally only, then reverted it and rebuilt; it is not on the branch.

## 6. Open points

- **Names and grades.**
  - `TrophyPopupEntry` already carries `name` and `grade`, and `TrophyPopupLines` shows them, as the tests and the named PNG prove.
  - The listener fills only the id, because PM-016's `NpTrophy2Unlock` has no name or grade.
  - Once PM-017 can look a trophy up by title, label and id, `queueUnlock` (TrophyPopup.cpp:39) is the one place that needs to fill them.
  - Names wider than 32 columns are cut. Each non-ASCII character (an accented letter, or any Japanese name) shows as one `?` until the font grows.
- **HDR display.** The HDR10 colour was checked in the render test only. This host has no HDR display: the Xvfb run reports "HDR output is unavailable on this host".
- **Windows** was not built here.
  - The new link follows the existing pattern: libSceAgcDriver already imports libc.prx and libkernel.prx, and the module loader uses `LoadLibraryExW(…, LOAD_WITH_ALTERED_SEARCH_PATH)` (libkernel/Module/src/DynamicLoader.cpp:179).
  - The listener import is a `_nid_no_patch` symbol, which PM-016 exported for this use.
- **`sceNpTrophy2RegisterUnlockCallback`** is still not called (unchanged from PM-016). The pop-up does not depend on it.
