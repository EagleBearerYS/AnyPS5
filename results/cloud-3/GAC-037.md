## 0. Bottom line

The counts show the menu frame is not stuck in the pyramid's image objects. Every pyramid surface is stored and re-read every gameplay frame on the coherent "storage refresh" path.

What the counts do not show is where those stores go:
- A "storage refresh" store (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:1759`, `PublishScope::None`) is retiled into the **unit shadow** (device memory, `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2852-2870`), not into the guest bytes.
- Readers of block-unit images (refreshes, uploads) read the slab directly and publish nothing (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2249-2271`). Only a layer-model upload (`:2275`) or a host-facing reader publishes the shadow into the guest bytes.
- After the load, the counts show one store of the 1920x1080 that does publish: the single "(idle)" flush (`FlushAllPending`, `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3479-3480`, publishing at `:3278-3279`).
- The GPU is idle at the load, not in gameplay, so that one publish most likely put the menu's last frame into the guest bytes. They have held it since.

**Most likely (1): a shader that reads the pyramid through pointers sees that frozen menu frame.**
- Address-based builds map whole registered heaps in place, with neither a store nor a publish first: `Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3739` (explorer `:3654`).
- So they read the guest bytes, still the menu's last frame, while the real results go to the unit shadow and the image objects every frame.
- On screen that is a dimmed menu frame that does not fade, until something publishes (an idle flush, a slab eviction, a host-facing read).
- This is PM-021's B, sharpened. B said pending results are not stored before pointer reads. The counts show they *are* stored every frame, but into the unit shadow, so pointer reads still see the menu.

Two conditions must hold for (1), and the boot shows the first directly:
- Unit shadows must be on, which needs watched imports (`Graphics/src/UnitShadow.cpp:428-431`, `:529-530`). The boot's new store line says "into the unit shadow" or "into the import".
- A Village pass must read texture memory through pointers. The exemption's own comment assumes none does. The boot shows that address-based builds map the heap while the pyramid holds unpublished results; it cannot show the read itself. One more run with `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1` (or `APS5_NO_UNIT_SHADOW=1`) confirms it.

The other candidates, in order (section 2):
- (2) a stale object served without a refresh;
- (3) writes stored nothing before (pointer stores, read-only-marked buffers) that a later store lands over;
- (4) fill paths;
- (5) virtual aliases;
- (6) a lost CMASK/DCC fast clear;
- (7) a creation-time DCC address.

The boot in section 3 tells them apart. Section 4 lists the lines each one prints.

**A quick check on the PC:** does the ghost stay at the same strength for 20 s? If so, it is a frozen copy: (1), (2) or (5). Lost clears (3), (4) and (6) leave menu content in a surface the title keeps writing, so they would fade or break up unless that surface only accumulates. PM-021 noted "dimmed, not fading".

## 1. What the boot's counts already say

1. **Two or more objects per surface, coherent every frame.**
   - A "storage refresh" store means some image B over the surface was refreshed while another image A over it had results pending.
   - Refresh stores every pending image over the range except B itself and a borrowable alias (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:1759`), then re-reads the units that changed.
   - So both objects are current in every gameplay frame. The menu frame is not stuck in them.
2. **Where those stores go.**
   - With `PublishScope::None`, the stored blocks are retiled into the unit shadow whenever a slab takes them (`writeBackWindows` → `ShadowDestinationFor`, `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2859` [explorer `:2858`]).
   - The guest bytes (the import) get them only from a publish. Only flushes with a scope other than None publish (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3241-3245`, `:3278-3279`).
   - Device-facing readers (uploads, refreshes) read the slab (`Graphics/include/UnitShadow.hpp:20-37`).
3. **What reached the 1920x1080's guest bytes after the load.**
   - Of its stores: "storage refresh" ×1552 and "storage image creation" ×3 are both None (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:1759`, `Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:974`). "idle" ×1 is `FlushAllPending("idle")` (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:1971-1977`), which stores everything and publishes everything.
   - So after the load, the guest copy was rewritten by at most one store: the idle one, at the load.
   - Publishes without a store print nothing in that boot. One comes from a layer-model upload over the surface (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2275`); another from a host-facing read with nothing pending. The diag prints both, and an upload publish every frame would refute candidate 1.
   - The 960x540 differs. Its 134 "(written buffer range)" stores (`Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3580`, whole scope) publish it whenever a dispatch writes a buffer over it.
   - The three levels of 64 KiB or less are stored and published at every flip (`Execution/src/Driver/Packets/PacketMemory.cpp:200`, `Graphics/include/Texture.hpp:198`).
   - The 480x270 and 240x135 levels behave like the 1920x1080.
4. **Nothing host-facing ever met pending pyramid results.**
   - The pyramid has results pending almost all the time. Yet the whole boot has no "buffer fill", "packet store", "buffer copy source/destination", "imported buffer region", "copied buffer region", "sampled texture" or "memory access" store on it.
   - So no recognized fill, DMA or packet store, copy, in-place V# read or CPU access ever covered it while it was pending.
   - Whatever else touches the pyramid does so through a path that stores nothing first:
     - pointers (exempt, `Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3739`);
     - V#s marked read-only (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:3567-3576`);
     - a fill that discards instead of storing (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3786-3795`; not traced in that boot);
     - or an access while nothing is pending.
5. **No clear through the keys.**
   - The keys stay "uncompressed" throughout, at 0x207fe09000 for the 1920x1080 and at the chain's own addresses.
   - The two surfaces that go "-> 0000" are new allocations made after the load.
   - So the title does not clear the pyramid through its DCC keys, which fits mechanism A being ruled out.
6. **"(refresh)": 43 before the load, 0 after.**
   - These are Refresh storing the refreshing image's own pending units first (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:1969`).
   - In the menu, B was sometimes pending itself when A had written: two dirty objects at once. That never happens after the load.
   - This weakens candidate 2 for gameplay.
7. **Layout.**
   - Every traced chain level has an untraced surface of the same size just below it: 0x20180e0000+0x280000, 0x20185e0000+0xc0000, 0x2018760000+0x40000, and 0x20187e0000, 0x2018800000, 0x2018820000 (+0x10000 each).
   - These look like A/B pairs, with the A side lacking DCC, which would keep it out of `[dcc-keys]` lines.
   - There may also be a 1920x1080 at 0x2017000000+0x870000, which ends exactly at 0x2017870000.
   - The boot range below covers the pairs. Add 0x2017000000-0x2017870000 if the log size allows.

## 2. How a console clears or overwrites such a pyramid, what this driver can miss, ranked by fit

On a console every one of these is just memory, and the next reader sees it. A title can clear or overwrite the pyramid with:
- a compute clear or fill (a buffer-store kernel, or CP DMA_DATA);
- a CB fast clear (CMASK or DCC keys) followed by an eliminate or decompress;
- the scene's first full write with blending off;
- a copy or resolve;
- a reset through a shader constant, which involves no memory at all.

How the driver handles each:
- Attachments are always LOAD/STORE (`Graphics/src/Pipeline.cpp:370`, `:673`), and blending is on per the CB_BLENDn enable bit (`Graphics/src/State.cpp:1189`).
- So a missed clear stays visible only in a surface that accumulates onto itself.
- A frozen copy (candidates 1, 2 and 5) stays visible whatever the surface does.

### 1. Pointer reads of guest bytes frozen at the last publish (high)

The path:
- The pyramid's results reach the guest bytes only through a publish (`Graphics/include/UnitShadow.hpp:20-37`, `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3278-3279`).
- "storage refresh" and "storage image creation" stores never publish (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:1759`, `Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:974`; `PublishReasonFor` maps both to Upload, `Graphics/src/UnitShadow.cpp:454`).
- Address-based builds map the whole registered heap in place and skip both the store and the publish: `Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3733-3740` [explorer `:3648-3655`]. The comment there reads: "such shaders do not read texture memory through pointers".
- Every other in-place or CPU reader stores and publishes first:
  - non-address-based in-place regions ("imported buffer region", `Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3739`, and the reuse path at `Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:2993-3005`, which also publishes a fresh shadow);
  - "copied buffer region" (`Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3749`);
  - "written buffer range" (`:3580`);
  - "sampled texture" (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:675`);
  - the flush hook, key reads and scanout (`Graphics/src/UnitShadow.cpp:444-456`).

Fit:
- It gives a frozen, non-fading menu frame while every image object is current.
- It matches "1 idle after the load" as the last publish.
- It needs no missed clear.

It needs unit shadows on, and an RE Engine pass that reads the pyramid, or one of its A/B neighbours, through pointers: an exposure, luminance, bloom or composite pass reading raw memory, for example.

With shadows off (compare mode, or `APS5_NO_UNIT_SHADOW=1`), pointer reads see the last *stored* frame, at most a frame old. Then there is no frozen menu, and (1) is out.

(1) is also out if an object over the surface uses the layer model rather than 64 KiB block units. Each of its uploads publishes the whole surface first (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2275`), so the guest bytes would be current every frame.

### 2. A stale object served without a refresh (medium)

The path:
- `FindPending` returns the first-registered pending image of exact size (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3521-3533`).
- Sampled lookups take it if `CanCopyFrom` passes (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:610`). Otherwise they view the object for the descriptor's own key or snapshot (`:638-658`). A view is refreshed only when its memory was stamped (`:664-667`, `:723`).
- The fast revalidation skips the pending checks while the pending serial is unchanged (`:2492`, `:3029`). It accepts a foreign pending image the lookup named (`:2604-2613`).
- A write to an image that is already dirty does not bump the serial (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3152-3160`).

Fit:
- For: the 3 "storage image creation" stores on the 1920x1080 after the load (and 2 on the 960x540) mean new keys over those surfaces, so there are more objects to pick from.
- Against: "(refresh)" is 0 after the load. The refreshed object is never itself pending in gameplay, which is the condition for a stale pick.

### 3. Writes stored nothing before, then overwritten by a deferred store (medium-low)

On a console, a compute clear (or the scene's first full write) overwrites the pyramid after the menu's last writes.

In the driver, two kinds of write get no store first:
- **Pointer stores:** nothing is stored first (`Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:3739`). The stamps arrive only when the batch completes (`Graphics/src/BdaResources.cpp:307-323`), with no sync unless `APS5_SYNC_LEASE_DISPATCH` is set (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:4698`).
- **V#s the recompiler marks read-only:** no flush, no note and no stamp (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:3567-3576`).

Meanwhile, the next "storage refresh" stores A's menu-era pending results. The keep rule spares only blocks already stamped (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:4604-4617`). So a store decided before the completion stamp lands over the clear, and a unit-shadow unit stays "fresh" until that stamp arrives.

Fit:
- The storage-refresh stores run straight through the load (packet 0x15, queues 0x20/0x0), so they are what would land over such a write.
- But the next full rewrite of the surface repairs a clear lost this way. It persists only in a surface that accumulates, and the counts cannot say whether this one does.

### 4. Fill paths (low-medium)

- **An Exact fill clears only the newest object** with that base and size (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3838-3844`).
  - It first discards the other objects' pending results (`Execution/src/Driver/Memory/BufferFill.cpp:323` → `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3786-3795`). Discards are never traced.
  - Those objects keep menu texels until a stamp-driven refresh.
- **A fill racing a store already in flight:** the image is in `pending.flushing` (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3238`), which `BufferFill.cpp:323` and `:350` never scan.
  - Only the keep rule protects against the late store.
  - That rule stores generation-0 layers whole (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:4599-4602`).
  - For surfaces of 1 MiB or less (the 480x270 level and smaller), it trusts an unsynchronized compare (`:4521-4526`).
- **Fills the HLE does not take** run as dispatches (`Execution/src/Driver/Dispatch/Dispatch.cpp:83`, `BufferFill.cpp:21-109`), and then behave like 3 or like any written buffer.
- **Fills on queues other than 0** run inline (`BufferFill.cpp:413`).

Fit: the whole boot has no "(buffer fill)" store on the pyramid. But a discard followed by a clear, with nothing else pending, would leave no line in that boot either.

### 5. Virtual aliases (low; the counts cannot show it)

- Stamps and lookups are per virtual address (PM-010, PM-021 F).
- If the title writes the pyramid's physical memory through a second mapping, nothing on 0x2017870000 sees the write.
- The boot can rule it in only by elimination: every object current, no pointer build over the range, no write of any kind there at the scene change, and still the ghost. Then check for direct memory mapped twice.

### 6. CMASK/DCC fast clear with a lost eliminate (low)

- A CMASK clear on a DCC target, on the GPU path, writes nothing (`Graphics/src/Draw.cpp:261`, `write = dccAddress == 0`).
- The error surfaces only as a fatal at a later CMASK draw (`:268` → `:195`).
- The CMASK is read only for mip-0 views (`Graphics/src/State.cpp:1487`).
- Eliminate and DCC decompress do nothing on uncompressed keys (`Graphics/src/Draw.cpp:2692-2694`).

Fit: poor. The keys are "uncompressed" from start to end, no fatal was reported, and the chain levels are separate surfaces, not mips.

### 7. Creation-time DCC address on a reused object, or a history swapped by descriptor at one address (low)

- `SurfaceKey` leaves out the DCC address (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:895-898`).
- An object keeps the DCC address it was created with. Key fills match only that address (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:3919-3921`), and `MetadataMoved` evicts only in some cases (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:962-965`).
- A history swapped *by address* is two surfaces and two objects, which works.

Fit: poor. The key addresses stay the same through the load.

**Build.** Take the explorer plus the diag: `git fetch fork diag/village-ghost-pyramid`, then `git cherry-pick 9395f6d4d 4447e8d8f` onto c8b86e304b.

**Switches** (PowerShell, before starting the exe):

```
$env:APS5_TRACE_GHOST = "0x2017870000-0x2018840000,0x20af800000+0x140000,0x201abd0000+0x3c0000"
$env:APS5_TRACE_FLUSH = "1"
$env:APS5_TRACE_DCC_KEYS = "1"
$env:APS5_TRACE_FILL_COVER = "1"
$env:APS5_TRACE_SHADOW = "0"
```

- `APS5_TRACE_GHOST` takes a comma-separated list of `begin+bytes` or `begin-end` ranges. The first range is the pyramid with all its A/B neighbours. The other two are the post-load "-> 0000" surfaces, kept as context.
- `APS5_TRACE_FLUSH` and `APS5_TRACE_DCC_KEYS` keep the earlier boot's lines, for comparison.
- `APS5_TRACE_FILL_COVER` adds every fill meeting an image (first 96) and the first 16 DCC clears.
- `APS5_TRACE_SHADOW=0` adds every slab eviction and import retire, with no publish spam.
- Optional: `APS5_TRACE_GHOST_PRESENTS = "<first>:<last>"` limits the `[ghost]` lines to those presents. Every line carries its present number, so a first run gives the window.

**The run.** Use the same save and scene as before:
- about 10 s on the title menu;
- load the save;
- 20 s in the first scene with the ghost visible.

The `[ghost]` lines go to stderr (`anyps5.log` with `--windows-gui`).

**Volume.** Expect roughly 100 to 300 `[ghost]` lines per frame for the 13 pyramid surfaces. That is a few hundred thousand lines for the run, unless the presents window is set.

**What the diag prints over the traced ranges.** Each line starts with `[ghost] #<seq> present <n> t <ms> packet 0x<op> queue 0x<q>:`. The sequence number orders lines across threads.
- **Objects:**
  - `create image <ptr> surface <base>+<bytes> <w>x<h> mips .. layers .. format .. tile .. dcc 0x.. units .. block .. attachable .. upload <reason>`;
  - `evict image`, and `destroy image ... (drops its pending results)`;
  - `storage lookup ...: cached image %p` or `no cached image, making one`.
- **Writes:**
  - `write image %p ... units a+b of n version generation dirty`;
  - `draw target i ...: image %p (version), cmask 0x.. fast clear f, dcc 0x.., blend b, write mask`;
  - `copy into image %p ... from image %p`;
  - `image %p ... borrows n units from alias image %p`;
  - `build writes buffer ...` and `build reads buffer ... (read-only descriptor)`;
  - `address-based stores to ... stamped at their batch's completion`.
- **Clears:**
  - `fill ... with p0 p1 p2 p3 (queue): cover none|exact|inside|around|straddle|several|keys|layer, image %p ..., discarded, cleared on the image c` (or `refused: <reason>`);
  - `fill ... discards every pending result of image %p` or `discards the pending units inside it`;
  - `cmask ... clear recorded on the GPU into image %p (dcc ...)` and `cmask ... read on the CPU: expanded|cleared|partly cleared`;
  - `metadata pass eliminate|dcc decompress|resolve|fmask decompress over target ...`;
  - `copy src -> dst: path p (0 cpu, 1 transfer, 2 refused, 3 image copy, 8 too large)`.
- **Refresh decisions:**
  - `refresh image %p ... proved current`;
  - `refresh image %p ... unchanged by stamps|compare (keys, flushed others, alias, generation a -> b, version, dirty)`;
  - `refresh image %p ... re-read N of U units (reason), stored N pending units first, dropped N, borrowed N, keys, cpu, flushed others, tracked t compared c, version, generation, dirty`.
- **Kept or dropped results:**
  - `flush for <reason> <range> stores image %p`, `skips image %p: no pending unit inside`, and `leaves image %p pending (excepted)`;
  - `store image %p ... for <reason>: n of U units, k 64 KiB blocks left to the CPU, bytes stored, ...`;
  - **new:** `store image %p ... by block write-back for <reason>: X bytes into the unit shadow in n ranges, Y bytes into the import in m ranges`;
  - `store image %p ... drops n pending ranges: memory no longer registered`.
- **The guest bytes:**
  - **new:** `publish <reason> copies X traced bytes of <range> (n units) from the unit shadow into the import`;
  - **extended:** `address-based build maps heap <heap> in place with no store and no publish (first traced part <range>: results pending p, unpublished unit-shadow results s, writable w)`. This is printed when p, s or w is set, at most 16 per present.
- **Reads:**
  - `sample ...: cached view of image %p (version, generation), pending image found %p, keys`;
  - `cached snapshot still current`;
  - `new view|snapshot %p`;
  - `fast path keeps ...`;
  - `reuse keeps sampled ... as view|snapshot %p (version), pending registry unchanged u, foreign pending accepted a`;
  - `reuse keeps storage image %p`.

**Finding the load in the log.** It is where the first `create image` for 0x20af800000 or 0x201abd0000 appears. Both surfaces exist only after the load.

## 4. What each candidate's lines look like

The patterns below use the diag's exact text. `<A>` is the writer object over 0x2017870000 and `<B>` the refreshed one. "#a < #b" compares sequence numbers.

**(1) Pointer reads of frozen guest bytes.**
- Every gameplay frame:
  - `store image <A> surface 0x2017870000+0x870000 by block write-back for storage refresh: 0x<n> bytes into the unit shadow in <k> ranges, 0x0 bytes into the import in 0 ranges`
  - then `refresh image <B> surface 0x2017870000+0x870000 re-read <n> of <U> units (flushed) ...`. B is current.
- The last `publish ... copies ... traced bytes of 0x2017870000...` before the ghost is at the load: `publish hook ...`, from the idle flush.
- During the ghost there is no publish over the 1920x1080, or only rare `evict` or `hook` ones.
- In gameplay: `address-based build maps heap 0x<heap> in place with no store and no publish (first traced part 0x2017870000+0x<n>: results pending 0|1, unpublished unit-shadow results 1, writable 0|1)`.
- **Out if:**
  - the stores say `0x0 bytes into the unit shadow` (shadows off), or the refresh lines say `tracked 0 compared 1` (imports compared, so shadows are off; `Graphics/src/UnitShadow.cpp:428-431`);
  - or a publish over the pyramid appears every frame (for example `publish upload copies ... traced bytes of 0x2017870000...`, from a layer-model object's upload, `Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:2275`);
  - or no address-based build maps a heap holding it.
- **Confirm** with one run of `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1`: the ghost goes. `APS5_NO_UNIT_SHADOW=1` alone also removes it if the frozen bytes are unpublished shadow results, as argued above.

**(2) A stale object.**
- One of these, with `<X>`'s version stuck frame after frame while some other object's `write image` lines advance:
  - `sample 0x2017870000+0x870000 1920x1080 format 36 mips 1: cached view of image <X> (version <v>, generation <g>), pending image found <Y>, keys uncompressed`
  - `reuse keeps sampled 0x2017870000+0x870000 as view <X> (version <v>), pending registry unchanged 1, foreign pending accepted 1`
  - `sample ...: fast path keeps view <X> (version <v>)`
- Also: two `create image` lines for one surface with different mips, format or tile, both with `write image` lines, and no `store image` between them.
- **Confirm** with `APS5_NO_FAST_REVALIDATE=1`, then `APS5_NO_ALIAS_BORROW=1`.

**(3) Writes stored nothing before, overwritten by a deferred store.**
- At the scene change:
  - `address-based build maps heap ... writable 1)` (#a)
  - then `store image <A> surface 0x2017870000+... for storage refresh: <n> of <U> units, 0 64 KiB blocks left to the CPU ...` (#b > #a)
  - then `address-based stores to 0x2017870000+0x<n> stamped at their batch's completion` (#c > #b).
  - The store at #b landed over the pointer stores.
- Or `build reads buffer 0x2017870000+0x<n> (read-only descriptor)` in the scene-change dispatch, with no `refresh ... re-read ... (cpu)` after it.
- **Out if:** no `address-based stores to` line and no read-only buffer over the pyramid near the load.
- **Confirm** with `APS5_SYNC_LEASE_DISPATCH=1` together with `APS5_BDA_FLUSH=1`, or with `APS5_ALL_BUFFERS_WRITTEN=1`.

**(4) Fill paths.**
- `fill 0x2017870000+0x870000 with 00000000 ... (queue 0x0): cover exact, image <A> layer ..., 1 other images (...), 1 images' pending results discarded, cleared on the image 1`, after which another object `<B>` over the same memory shows one of:
  - `refresh image <B> ... proved current`;
  - `unchanged by stamps`;
  - a `sample ... cached view of image <B>` with no `re-read`.
- Or a race: a `flush for ... stores image <A>` (#a), then a `fill` (#b > #a), then that store's `store image <A> ... 0 64 KiB blocks left to the CPU` (#c > #b).
- Or `fill ... refused: <reason>`, with nothing else writing the pyramid.
- **Confirm** with `APS5_NO_FILL_CLEAR=1` or `APS5_NO_FILL_HLE=1`.

**(5) Virtual aliases.**
- No write line of any kind over the pyramid at the scene change, every object current, no address-based build mapping it, and still the ghost.
- Look for a second mapping of the same direct memory.

**(6) CMASK.**
- `draw target 0 0x2017870000+0x870000 1920x1080 mip 0: image <A> (version <v>), cmask 0x<c> fast clear 1, dcc 0x207fe09000, ...`
- with `cmask 0x<c> of target 0x2017870000+...: clear recorded on the GPU into image <A> (dcc 0x207fe09000, ...)`, which loses the clear;
- or `metadata pass eliminate over target 0x2017870000+... (cmask 0x<c> fast clear 1, dcc 0x207fe09000)`.
- No `fast clear 1` on the pyramid rules it out.

**(7) DCC address.**
- A `draw target` or `sample` line naming a DCC address other than the one in the `create image ... dcc` line of the object it uses (`storage lookup ...: cached image <X>`).
