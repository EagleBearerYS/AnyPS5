## Step 3: `feat(relinker): lower rsp only as far as a frameless red-zone leaf reaches below it` (1a262894a)

**Which leaves:** frameless ones (no `push rbp; mov rbp, rsp`).

**How far rsp is lowered.** Per leaf, `shift` is:

- the deepest negative RSP displacement in the body, rounded up to 16, and at most 0x80;
- the whole 0x80 if the leaf has `lea` of a negative RSP displacement, as the task asked.

**Index registers (beyond the task).** An RSP operand with an index register (`-0x10(%rsp,%rcx,8)`) also takes the whole 0x80.

- The reason is the one the task gives for `lea`: an index can reach deeper than the displacement.
- Before this step, the fixed 0x80 shift covered such operands anywhere in the red zone. A smaller shift would have broken that silently.
- To spot the index, `MemoryUse` now reads the SIB index together with REX.X / VEX.X.

**Where the shift is used:**

- The prologue stub lowers RSP with `lea -shift(%rsp), %rsp`.
- The exit stub raises it with `lea shift(%rsp), %rsp`, always in the 32-bit displacement form, as before.
- Every RSP operand moves by `shift`.
- Leaves with a frame pointer keep 0x80.

**Positive 8-bit displacements** now move in place when `disp + shift <= 0x7f`.

**Still refused:** EVEX operands, operands without a displacement, and positive 8-bit displacements that the shift takes past 0x7f.

**Structural tests** (`RedZoneFramerTests.cpp`):

- `framesLeafWithoutFramePointer` was updated. #5640's shape now lowers by 0x10 and moves -0x10 to 0x00, and the exit stub is checked too.
- `skipsLeavesWithOperandsThatCannotMove` now uses a +0x78 load, which overflows at shift 0x10, and a `(%rsp)` load. Its old +0x08 load now fits.
- New:
  - `shiftsByTheDeepestOperandBelowRsp` (-0x28 → shift 0x30)
  - `movesPositiveOperandsThatFitAfterTheShift` (+0x08 → 0x18)
  - `shiftsByTheWholeRedZoneForAddressesBelowRsp` (`lea -0x20` → 0x80; the control `lea +0x08` keeps shift 0x10)
  - `shiftsByTheWholeRedZoneForIndexedOperands`

## Step 4: `feat(relinker): frame red-zone leaves that allocate with sub after their pushes` (132597c6e)

**Prologue.** In a frameless leaf, directly after the push chain, the prologue site now also takes one of:

- `48 83 ec ib`
- `48 81 ec id`
- `48 8d 64 24 ib`
- `48 8d a4 24 id`

with a negative adjustment. The stub lowers RSP by `shift` after it.

**Exits.** An exit must have the matching `add` / `lea` of the same size directly before its pops. That instruction becomes part of the exit site, and the stub raises by `shift` before it.

- The free is found in a pre-pass, so the body loop doesn't refuse it as RSP arithmetic.

**Depth** is measured from the post-`sub` RSP, as the body's displacements are.

**Refusals:**

- A leaf with an allocation whose exit has no free before its pops is skipped with the new reason **"exit does not free the prologue's allocation"**.
- An `add` of another size before the pops, and any other RSP arithmetic in the body, still give "other use of rsp". The loop reaches them before the `ret`.
- Leaves with a frame pointer that allocate are unchanged (still "other use of rsp"). The debt entry says so.

**A leaf with no pushes** but an allocation and a prologue of at least five bytes (`sub $imm32`) is now accepted too. It is the same check with "no pushes" relaxed to "no pushes and no allocation".

**Structural tests:**

- **`framesLeavesThatAllocateBelowTheirPushes`, the `inflate_fast` shape:**
  - code: `push rbp; push r15; push r14; push r13; push r12; push rbx; sub $0x40,%rsp`, a spill `mov %rdi,-0x20(%rsp)`, locals at `0x0`, `0x8` and `0x38(%rsp)`, a `dec; jne` loop over them, and the exit `add $0x40,%rsp; pop rbx; pop r12; pop r13; pop r14; pop r15; pop rbp; ret`;
  - expected: framed with shift 0x20, the prologue site covering the pushes and the `sub`, the exit site covering the `add` through the `ret`, and all 8 RSP operands moved by 0x20.
- **`framesLeavesThatAllocateWithLeaOrALargeSub`:** `lea -0x40(%rsp),%rsp` / `lea 0x40(%rsp),%rsp`, and `sub $0x100` / `add $0x100` with a 32-bit displacement local.
- **`skipsAllocatingLeavesWithoutAMatchingFree`:**
  - no `add` → the new reason;
  - `add $0x30` against `sub $0x40` → "other use of rsp";
  - an extra `sub $8` in the body → "other use of rsp".

**A caveat for GTA V's `inflate_fast`.** The local at offset 0 in the test is encoded with an explicit 8-bit displacement (`0x0(%rsp)`, mod 1). A compiler emits `(%rsp)` with no displacement (mod 0), which stays refused as the task asks.

- If the real `inflate_fast` (eboot 0x28aa440) addresses a slot as `(%rsp)`, it is still skipped, with "rsp operand that cannot move with rsp".
- The same happens if any 8-bit displacement in it is above 0x5f, since its spill puts the shift at 0x20.
- I don't have the binary to check. tibzejoker's disassembly can answer it with a grep for `(%rsp)` without a displacement.
