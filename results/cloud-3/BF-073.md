## What changed

- **The reader.** `GuestMemorySnapshot` (Graphics/include/GuestBufferMemory.hpp) gains three fields with defaults, `reader` (a new `SnapshotReader`: Unknown, Draw or Dispatch), `queue` and `program`, so every existing `{address, bytes}` initialiser still compiles. The two capture sites fill them in:
  - **The draw** (Execution/src/Driver/Draw/Draw.cpp, the `snapshot` lambda at ~463):
    - kind `Draw`;
    - `submission.queue`;
    - the code address of the draw's last program, `programs.back().binary.codeAddress`, i.e. its last stage. A draw has several programs, and I carry one so the snapshot stays small.
    - Both calls of the lambda (~466 and ~548) run before `programs` is moved into the pipelined commit, so the address is valid.
  - **The dispatch** (Execution/src/Driver/Dispatch/Dispatch.cpp:406): kind `Dispatch`, `submission.queue`, and the dispatch's program address (`address`, from registers 0x20c/0x20d).
  - **Cost:** three stores per captured region.
- **The bytes.** A new `SnapshotMismatchDetail(snapshot, live, first)` (GuestBufferMemory.cpp) formats one string:
  - the snapshot's address and size;
  - the start offset, which is the first difference rounded down to 16;
  - the snapshot's and the live bytes from there, up to 0x20 bytes, as hex dwords (a tail shorter than 4 bytes as hex bytes);
  - the reader (`read by a draw on queue N, program 0x…`, `a dispatch on queue N`, or `an unknown reader`).
- **The new line.** `AddSnapshot`'s live-mismatch branch prints `[gpu]   <detail>` after the existing warning, for the first 8 mismatches of the run, under the same `reportMutex` and capped at 8 in total.
  - The existing warning line, its wording and its 10-second rate limit are untouched.
  - The new line has its own cap, so it also prints when the rate limit suppresses the warning. That's why it carries the snapshot address itself.
  - Nothing runs outside the mismatch branch, and there is no new switch.

## Test

**The case:** `snapshotMismatchDetailTests` in `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/tests/BdaResources.cpp`, called from `RunBdaResourceTests`. It runs in ctest `agc_driver_graphics` (`agc_driver_graphics_tests`, mock Vulkan).

1. **Setup:** a 256-byte buffer of words 0x1000+i. A 0x30-byte snapshot of it at +0x10 is captured, then the live word at +0x24 is changed to 0xdeadbeef.
2. **The helper, three ways:**
   - a draw on queue 2 with program 0x123400, first difference at +0x14: eight dwords from +0x10, snapshot `00001008 00001009 …` against live `00001008 deadbeef …`, and the reader text;
   - a dispatch on queue 5 without a program, over a 0x16-byte snapshot: the tail is printed as bytes (`00001008 09 10 | live 00001008 ef be`);
   - a snapshot without a reader: "read by an unknown reader".
3. **The real path:** a `GuestBufferMemory` with `AddWritable` over the buffer, then `AddSnapshot` of the stale snapshot, with stderr redirected to a file. The test checks that:
   - the build does not fail (a live-backed mismatch only warns);
   - the existing warning is printed with "differs first at +0x14";
   - the new `[gpu]   ` line follows it with exactly the expected words and reader.

   The test then echoes what was printed.
