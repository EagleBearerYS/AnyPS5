## Step 1 as written: the sleep at the checks does not go red

I added a 200 ms sleep right before the `Require` at `HostWriteHook.cpp:137` and `:183`, and before `HostWritePendingStorage.cpp:207`.

| binary | runs | result |
|---|---|---|
| main, hook | 10 | 10 passed |
| main, hook, `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE=1` | 10 | 10 passed |
| main, pending storage | 10 | 10 passed |
| main, pending storage, `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE=1` | 10 | 10 passed |

**What the sleep did:** nothing. By the time it runs, the idle store has nothing left to store.
- `StorageTexture::FlushPending(..., "sampled texture")` removes the image from the pending list (`Texture.cpp:4802`, `it = pending.textures.erase(it);`) and records its store.
- After that, `StorageTexture::AnyPending()` (`Texture.cpp:5371`) is false.
- So `idleResultsPending` (`QueueWorker.cpp:206`) is false. Even if the idle store ran, `VulkanDevice::StoreIdleResults` returns at its first line (`VulkanDevice.cpp:2905`).
- Nothing else submitted the recorded store during the 200 ms either.
- With `APS5_TRACE_FLUSH=1`, the sleep run shows only `[flush] image 0x…+0x20000 for sampled texture` lines, one per check. There is no `for idle` line.

## What else writes the range: the same idle store, one step earlier

The step-1 stop rule says to look for whatever else writes the range between the dispatch and the check. It is the idle store, but in an earlier window: between `AgcDriverWaitIdle` (the end of `Dispatch`) and `FlushPending`. In that window the image is still pending. If the test thread is held there for more than `IdleResultsQuietPeriod` (50 ms, `QueueWorker.cpp:20`), queue 0 stores the results to guest memory (`QueueWorker.cpp:225-239`). `FlushPending` then finds nothing to record, and the check at :137 sees non-zero bytes.

Scratch (not committed): a 200 ms sleep right before `FlushPending` at `HostWriteHook.cpp:136` and `:182`, and right after `Dispatch` in `HostWriteAfterResults` (before `HostWritePendingStorage.cpp:204`).

| binary | runs | result |
|---|---|---|
| main, hook | 10 | **0 passed, 10 failed**, all at :137: `host write waits: the image's store landed when it was made, so none was recorded over the range` |
| main, hook, `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE=1` | 10 | 10 passed |
| main, pending storage | 10 | **0 passed, 10 failed**, all at :204: `a store recorded before a 64 KiB host write: the image's results reached guest memory before the host write, so none was pending` |
| main, pending storage, `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE=1` | 10 | 10 passed |

- The failure message is CI's exact message.
- With `APS5_TRACE_FLUSH=1`, the failing run shows `[flush] image 0x7f9feb3d0000+0x20000 for idle 0x7f9feb3d0000+0x20000`. That is the idle store writing the range.
- The shutdown line matches the failing log's: `host imports at shutdown: 0 live (0.0 MiB); made 1 (0.1 MiB) … access 1/0/0`.
- **`HostWritePendingStorage.cpp`:** the :207 sleep does not turn it red, for the same reason as :137. The pre-flush sleep turns it red 10 of 10, at :204 rather than :207, because :204 checks first. All four of its cases need the results to stay pending after `AgcDriverWaitIdle`: :204 runs in every case, and "a 64/4 KiB host write over pending results" depends on it.

## Summary for the Bug Finder

**The cause is queue 0's idle store, but in a different window than step 1 assumed.**
- The window is between `Dispatch`'s `AgcDriverWaitIdle` and the test's `FlushPending`, not after the flush.
- `FlushPending` takes the image off the pending list. After it, the idle store has nothing to store, so the 200 ms sleep at :137 and :183 (and :207) stays green, 40 of 40 runs.
- The same sleep moved before the flush is red 10 of 10 on main, in both tests, with CI's exact :137 message and an `APS5_TRACE_FLUSH` line `for idle` over the range. It is green 10 of 10 with `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE=1`.

**The fix:** db56b94722 on `fix/agc-host-write-tests-idle-store` sets `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE=1` in both tests' `main()`.
- `HostWritePendingStorage.cpp` is included: with the pre-flush sleep it goes red at :204, because all its cases need the results to stay pending after `AgcDriverWaitIdle`.
- With the fix, all four sleep variants pass 20 of 20, and `until-fail:50` passes.
- Without a sleep, main passed 200 of 200 under `guest_` `-j4` load here, so the race needs a stall of 50 ms or more, like the one CI's loaded runner hit.
- The checks are clean, and 14 of 14 `^agc_driver_(host_|idle_store|store_idle)` tests pass.

**Possibly the same race elsewhere (unchanged):** `agc_driver_storage_file_write` (`tests/execution/StorageFileWrite.cpp:127-128`) calls `AgcDriverWaitIdle` and then `Require(StorageTexture::AnyPending(), …)`, without `APS5_NO_IDLE_STORE`, so a 50 ms stall there would fail the same way. The idle-store tests themselves (`IdleStoreOverUnmap`, `IdleStoreYields`, `QueueZeroIdleWait`) need the idle store on.
