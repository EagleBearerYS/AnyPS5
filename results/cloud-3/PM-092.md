### What
Implements 7 libSceNet functions that threw, and leaves 4 more of the same batch throwing because their answer would be a guess:
- sceNetSocketInternal, sceNetGetsockoptEx and sceNetSetsockoptEx forward to sceNetSocket, sceNetGetsockopt and sceNetSetsockopt. Their restored argument lists match the public ones; how the console tells them apart is recorded as debt.

- sceNetInetNtopWithScopeId and sceNetInetPtonWithScopeId convert IPv6 text with a numeric zone (`fe80::1%2`, RFC 4007 section 11) through sceNetInetNtop and sceNetInetPton, as FreeBSD's getnameinfo with NI_NUMERICSCOPE prints it and getaddrinfo parses it. Interface-name zones and AF_INET throw.
- sceNetConfigGetDefaultRoute reports the IPv4 gateway of the host interface sceNetCtlGetInfo describes as DEFAULT_ROUTE, through a libSceNetCtl helper next to the DNS one. Without a gateway, or for a null pointer, it throws.

Still throwing: sceNetConfigGetDefaultRoute6 (NetCtl has no IPv6 model), sceNetConfigGetIfFlags and sceNetGetIfnameNumList (the console's interface numbering is not known), and sceNetInitParam (NetInitializeParameter's layout is not known).
