# BF-293: ceiling switch C2 (a dispatch's complete stage outside the GPU mutex): not built (cloud-3)

## Verdict

I did not build or push `diag/village-ceiling-c2-on-41ef8ff856`.

The switch cannot be made crash-safe in the cheap form the task asks for: one small mutex per container family, taken only by the complete stage.
- **The reason:** stage B and a hit's `Revalidate` are not only container work. They record Vulkan commands into the device's one open command batch, and they mutate per-image containers that only the GPU mutex guards. They do both while holding the texture-cache mutexes.
- **What each way out costs:**
  - Lifting the GPU mutex as the task describes leaves unlocked recorder and container mutation (a crash) or an AB/BA deadlock against draws.
  - Re-taking the GPU mutex at those points puts almost all of stage B back under it, so there would be nothing to measure.

A binary that can crash or deadlock in tonight's Village boot would cost the boot, so I stopped here. The details are below, with what a working C2 needs (BF-291's scope) and an upper bound from the PM's own numbers.

Base: fork `diag/village-preresolve-on-fjseries-50c9bee85c` = **41ef8ff856**. Paths are under `core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/`.

## Why the complete stage needs the GPU mutex

Where the hold is today: `Driver::dispatch` takes the GPU mutex (`Execution/src/Driver/Dispatch/Dispatch.cpp:509` pipelined, `:603` inline) and calls `VulkanDevice::dispatch`. Under that mutex `VulkanDevice::dispatch` runs (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp`):
- the reap (`:5543-5548`);
- stage B `ShaderResources::Complete()` (`:5583-5617`), or the hit's `Revalidate`, `MovedDispatchBuffers` and `PrepareDrawBindings` (`:5618-5687`);
- a locked full build (`:5688-5697`);
- the recording (`recordCompletedDispatch`, `:5737`).

**1. Stage B records into the device's single open batch.**
- **There is one recorder for every queue,** `activeRecorder` (`Graphics/src/Recorder.cpp:47`, `Recorder::Active()` `:1290`).
- **Its contract** (`Graphics/include/Recorder.hpp:37`): "Every call happens under GuestMemory::GpuMutex".
- **`Recorder::ensureOpen`** (`Recorder.cpp:1578`) creates the batch and pops the spare list. Its only guard is `AssertGpuLockHeld`, which aborts only under `APS5_ASSERT_GPU_LOCK` and otherwise proceeds (`Execution/src/GuestMemory.cpp:2746-2751`).
- **Stage B reaches it from:**
  - **sampled lookups:** the sampled-texture snapshot constructor (`Graphics/src/Texture.cpp:440-542`, through `cachedTexture`'s miss path `Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp:995-1031`, which holds `Textures().mutex` from `:935`), and `refreshForView` → `StorageTexture::Refresh` (`ShaderResources.cpp:945`, `:868`);
  - **storage lookups:** `StorageTexture::Create` → `upload` and `Refresh`, inside `cachedStorageTexture` under `StorageTextures().mutex` (`ShaderResources.cpp:1238-1280`, `Texture.cpp:2676`, `:2143`);
  - **`GuestBufferMemory::UploadFinish`**, which opens the batch (`Graphics/src/GuestBufferMemory.cpp:5596`) and records `recordGpuCopies` (`:5737-5843`) and `ResidentBuffer::FlushChanged` (`Graphics/src/ResidentMirror.cpp:52-61`);
  - **`Keep`, `KeepBytes`, `NotePendingRead(s)`, `NotePendingWrite(s)` and `OnComplete`** throughout (for example `ShaderResources.cpp:1267`, `:1285-1289`; `GuestBufferMemory.cpp:456`, `:5791`, `:5807`, `:5818`, `:5823`).
- **The CPU-path upload goes further:** it submits a `CommandBatch` on the device's command pool and queue (`Texture.cpp:2926`, `:2965` → `Graphics/src/Resources.cpp:348-412`). Vulkan requires external synchronization on those objects.

**2. Stage B and `Revalidate` mutate per-image containers that only the GPU mutex guards.**
- **Images are shared:** the storage cache hands every lookup the same `shared_ptr` (`ShaderResources.cpp:1254`), and cached `ShaderResources` objects keep them.
- **The header contract:** `Graphics/include/Texture.hpp:367-370`, "Under GuestMemory::GpuMutex only, as Refresh is; never from a build's stage A".
- **Examples:**
  - `StorageTexture::StorageView` inserts into the image's `uintViews` map (`Texture.cpp:2058-2062`); the descriptor writes call it, and `AtomicView`/`Atomic64View` likewise (`ShaderResources.cpp:2527`, `:2572`).
  - `upload` resizes and fills `original`, `layerOriginal` and `savedTicket` (`Texture.cpp:2688-2713`).
  - `refresh` writes `layerGeneration`, `layerPending` and `layerBehind` (`Texture.cpp:2160-2458`).
- **Also GPU-mutex-only:**
  - the image-mirror contents (`GuestBufferMemory.cpp:2414-2416`), refreshed through `syncRange` → `Recorder::Sync` (`:2810-2823`);
  - the copied-writer lists that `CompletePendingCpuWrites` walks (`Execution/src/VulkanDevice.cpp:522-524`, `Graphics/src/Draw.cpp:1127-1131`);
  - the `TextureDetiler` descriptor rings (`Graphics/src/TextureDetilerDescriptors.cpp:10-64`).

**3. Both queues can hold the same cached object.** `ResourceCache::Find` returns the same `shared_ptr` to every caller with the key, and `PrepareDispatch` already holds it without the mutex (`VulkanDevice.cpp:4965-4975`). Its `Revalidate` writes these object containers (`ShaderResources.cpp:3141-3155`, `:3234`, `:3352`, `:3575-3667`, `:4166-4173`):
- `validatedTextures` and `storageUntracked`;
- `comparedTableSpans`, `textures[i]` and `descriptorImages`;
- the write-index subscription fields.

**Which containers are already safe:** the caches the task names are mostly self-locked already.
- the resource cache (`Graphics/include/ShaderResources.hpp:808`);
- `Textures()` (`ShaderResources.cpp:133`) and `StorageTextures()` (`:1084`);
- the import set (`GuestBufferMemory.cpp:254`) and the pending-results registry (`Texture.cpp:1570`);
- the write-watch tracker (`GuestMemory.cpp:1159`);
- `DescriptorCache` (`ShaderResources.hpp:143`) and `BufferPool` (`BufferPool.hpp:157`).

The recorder, the per-image state, the mirror contents and the cached objects' proof state are the ones with no lock of their own.

## Why per-family mutexes don't fix it

**Option 1: a recorder mutex taken only by the complete stage.** This doesn't exclude the GPU-mutex holders that record at the same time: draws, the other queue's recording, labels, flips. Two threads would then push into the same batch, keep lists and command buffer.

**Option 2: the complete stage re-takes the GPU mutex (or a recorder lock everyone takes) wherever it records.** It records inside `Textures().mutex` and `StorageTextures().mutex`, so that thread's order is cache mutex → GPU mutex. Every draw takes the GPU mutex first and then the same cache mutexes in its own lookups (`cachedTexture` under the draw's hold), so the draw's order is GPU mutex → cache mutex. The two orders deadlock (AB/BA).

**Option 3: a recorder lock made innermost.** That doesn't hold either, because the recorder calls outward while it would hold it:
- `Recorder::finish` runs the batch's completion actions, which store GPU results to guest memory (`Recorder.cpp:4214-4241`), and releases its kept objects (`:4247`);
- `Sync` waits and finishes;
- `StorageTexture::FlushPending` takes the GPU mutex inside the storage registry mutex (`Texture.cpp:4289`, reached from `ShaderResources.cpp:1259`).

**Option 4: take the GPU mutex at stage B's entry points, before the cache mutex.** That is `cachedTexture`, `cachedStorageTexture`, `fastTexture`, `UploadFinish`, the descriptor-write block, `captureValidation` and `Revalidate`'s recording steps. It is deadlock-free and crash-safe. But by the PM's own profile, B images (24 s) plus B upload (44 s) are 68 of the 72 s of `resources: complete (wall)`, and those would be back under the mutex. Two queues would still take turns, so it would not measure the ceiling.

## What a working C2 needs (BF-291's scope)

1. **Deferred recording.** Stage B records its uploads, copies, `Keep`s and pending notes into a per-build list, which `recordCompletedDispatch` replays into the open batch under the GPU mutex. The alternative is a recorder lock taken by every recorder entry point, with completions and syncs moved out from under it.
2. **A per-image mutex** for `StorageTexture` and `Texture` refresh, upload, copy refresh and lazy view creation. Order: registry mutex (`StorageTextures()`/`Textures()`) → image mutex → recorder stage. Nothing takes a registry mutex or the GPU mutex while holding an image lock.
3. **A per-object mutex** around a cached `ShaderResources`' `Revalidate` and `RefreshData`.
4. **Locks for** the mirror contents, the copied-writer lists, the detiler rings and the device command pool/queue (`CommandBatch`).
5. **One global order:** GPU mutex → cache/registry mutexes → image mutex → per-build recording stage, with the replay under the GPU mutex. The flush hook's unlocked wait (`Recorder::syncThroughUnlocked`, `Recorder.cpp:3970-4045`, today only at depth 1) then becomes reachable from stage B, because stage B no longer nests in the dispatch's hold.

## Upper bound from the PM's numbers (analytic, not measured)

- **The numbers:** queues 0x0 and 0x20 each wait 2.1–2.3 s per 10 s for the GPU mutex, mostly behind each other's dispatches.
- **The bound:** if those waits disappeared entirely, each queue would finish the same work in about 7.7–7.9 s instead of 10. That is at most about 1.27–1.30× throughput on those queues.
- **For the menu:** if they are the whole critical path, about 0.7 → 0.9 fps.
- **It is optimistic:**
  - the 32 s of "hook waits" are GPU waits that would remain, only unlocked;
  - two concurrent complete stages compete for the same cores and memory bandwidth;
  - the graphics queue's own draws still need the mutex.
