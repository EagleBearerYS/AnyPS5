**H1, the drawn-path sync hazard. Found with sync validation, confirmed by reading.**
- In the drawn path, `PresentationPass::RecordStage` renders into the stage with a render pass whose final layout is SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL. `PresentationPass::target`'s dependency to VK_SUBPASS_EXTERNAL has a destination of TRANSFER only for TRANSFER_SRC; for every other layout it is BOTTOM_OF_PIPE with no access.
- So nothing orders the end-of-pass layout transition (COLOR_ATTACHMENT_OPTIMAL → SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL) or the colour writes before EASU or RCAS sample the stage.
- The `RecordStageWritten(SHADER_READ_ONLY → SHADER_READ_ONLY, COLOR_ATTACHMENT_OUTPUT → FRAGMENT_SHADER)` barrier that follows cannot fix it. A barrier can't chain onto a transition whose second scope was BOTTOM_OF_PIPE.
- What validation reports: `SYNC-HAZARD-READ-AFTER-WRITE` (MessageID 0xe4d96472), "vkCmdDraw(): Hazard READ_AFTER_WRITE for VkImageView … imageLayout: VK_IMAGE_LAYOUT_SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL … prior_usage: SYNC_IMAGE_LAYOUT_TRANSITION … command: vkCmdEndRenderPass".
  - It fires once for each of the 10 drawn-path presents that `agc_driver_presentation_fsr` records (`fsrPresent`, which mirrors `present()`'s drawn branch).
  - Both EASU draws and RCAS draws report it.
- Effect: lavapipe executes in order, so nothing shows here. On a GPU that decompresses or resolves colour metadata in that transition, EASU or RCAS can read a stale or partly transitioned stage. That would be intermittent corruption on the path converted resident display buffers take.
- Fix **5798fc3d8** `fix(agc): order the FSR stage's render pass before EASU and RCAS sample it`:
  - Adds `PresentationPass::ExitDependency(finalLayout)`. It gives SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL a fragment-shader read as its second scope, and keeps PRESENT_SRC_KHR (BOTTOM_OF_PIPE, no access) and TRANSFER_SRC_OPTIMAL (TRANSFER, TRANSFER_READ) as they were. Main's render passes are therefore unchanged.
  - `PresentationPass::target` and `PresentationUpscaler::target` both use it, so the copy of that mapping in the upscaler is gone.
  - Removes the ineffective barrier from `present()`'s drawn branch and from the test's `fsrPresent`.
  - Test, in the same commit: `exitDependencyTests` in `agc_driver_presentation_fsr` checks all three mappings.
- Verified:
  - `agc_driver_presentation_fsr` under sync validation: 10 hazards before the fix, **0 after**.
  - Mutation check: putting the old mapping back fails the new test with "a presentation render pass ending in SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL does not order its writes and final layout transition before EASU and RCAS sample the image".

**M1, USAGE disclosures.** USAGE calls `2.0` "weakest" but never says it isn't off, and it doesn't mention the flat-area shift.
- I measured the shift instead of quoting it. I evaluated ffx_fsr1.h's RCAS arithmetic for a flat area in single precision, including `APrxMedRcpF1`'s bit trick. For 0 < v < 1, every channel's lobe is -0.25, clamped to -0.1875. The output is v·x·rcp~(x) with x = 1 − 0.75·2^(−sharpness), so the factor depends only on the sharpness, never on v:

  | sharpness | factor | a flat 0.5 becomes |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0.0 | 0.996847 (−0.32%, the worst case over 0–2) | 0.49842 |
  | 0.2 (default) | 0.998335 (−0.17%) | 0.49917 |
  | 1.0 | 0.999445 | — |
  | 2.0 | 0.999327 (−0.07%) | 0.49966 |

- This matches the test's printed measurements exactly ("a flat 0.5 region stays uniform at 0.49966 and 0.49917"). The task's "up to ~0.3%" is the 0.0 case; at the default it is 0.17%.
- In 8-bit SDR the shift is at most one level (255 → 254.20 at 0.0). In 10-bit HDR10 it is at most three codes (1023 → 1019.8).
- Fix **a33e22b89** (docs only) appends to USAGE.md:198: "RCAS always sharpens: `2.0` is its weakest setting, not off. Its approximate reciprocal also darkens flat areas by a fixed fraction of their value: 0.17% at the default `0.2` (a flat 0.5 becomes 0.4992), 0.32% at `0.0` and 0.07% at `2.0`. That is at most one 8-bit level for SDR, and at most three 10-bit code values for HDR10."

### Area by area: what I checked, how, what I found

**Present paths: drawn, direct, shared, clear-only.** I read `present()` (VulkanDevice.cpp:3131-3330) and the upscaler.
- **clear-only** skips FSR: `upscaled = upscaler && !clearOnly` (:3159). Clear-only keeps both of today's swapchain barriers, since `!drawn && !upscaled` (:3209, :3309).
- **shared and direct:**
  - The stage goes UNDEFINED → TRANSFER_DST in `RecordStageTransferDestination`, with TOP_OF_PIPE and no access; its old contents are discarded and fully overwritten.
  - The source is blitted whole (`RecordStretch`, or `RecordStretchFrom` for direct).
  - `RecordStageWritten` (TRANSFER/TRANSFER_WRITE → FRAGMENT/SHADER_READ, TRANSFER_DST → SHADER_READ_ONLY) runs before EASU or RCAS read it.
- **drawn:** the stage is written by a render pass. That ordering was wrong: H1.
- **EASU → RCAS:** ordered by the EASU render pass's exit dependency (FRAGMENT_SHADER/SHADER_READ).
- **Swapchain image:** written by RCAS with initialLayout UNDEFINED and final PRESENT_SRC. The acquire is fence-waited on the host, and the submit has no wait semaphore. Same model as today.
- **Letterbox:** RCAS writes opaque black outside the rect, matching today's letterbox blit (0 = PQ black).
- **Per-slot images and descriptor sets** are only touched for the cursor slot, which `present()` requires to be retired. Release happens in `RetireSlot`, after the slot's fence.
- **Validation:** on the shared path under sync validation, no messages beyond main's.

**HDR10/PQ and SDR.**
- SDR is always B8G8R8A8_UNORM with SRGB_NONLINEAR (:2722 and `DynamicRangeSurfaceFormat`). The stage uses the swapchain format, so FSR sees sRGB-encoded values, not linear ones.
- HDR10 is A2B10G10R10 or A2R10G10B10 with ST2084, so FSR sees PQ codes. RCAS output is clamped to [0, 1].
- **Format or colour-space change at run time.** `SelectDynamicRange` → `WaitIdle` → `useSurfaceFormat` → `recreateSwapchain`. On the next present, `Stage()` and `ensureSurface` recreate each slot's stage and `upscaled` in the new format as that slot is used. `target()` keys pipelines by format; the old entries stay, at most 2 formats × 2 filters.
- The test's HDR10 case (A2B10G10R10, PQ) passes: a flat region stays within one code, and the letterbox is opaque PQ black. `video_out_hdr_present` under Xvfb runs the SDR path, since Xvfb offers no HDR10.

**Size changes.**
- `StageExtent` and `Upscales` are recomputed every present from the buffer size and `state->extent`, and `RecordPresent` requires the stage to match (:390).
- Swapchain recreation, a resize, and a buffer that re-registers at another size (VideoOut's 64→32 case) each recreate the cursor slot's surfaces in `ensureSurface`, never while that slot is in flight.
- **Minimize:** extent 0 → `Presentable()` false, so `present()` isn't called.
- **Larger, then smaller:** EASU stops and the stage shrinks to the fitted size. The stale `upscaled` surface is L1.
- **The EASU decision is never wrong in direction.** `ComputeContainSize` uses one uniform scale with rounding, so a scale ≥ 1 never rounds either axis below the buffer. EASU never gets an output smaller than its input on an axis.
- **Leaks:** transient views and framebuffers are destroyed at retire, and everything else in `release()` at teardown, which runs after `vkDeviceWaitIdle`. None found.

### Addendum: what the FSR path holds and waits on, compared with today

**Locks.** `present()` runs under `GuestMemory::GpuMutex()`, inside `Driver::Present`'s second locked block (Presentation.cpp:106-131), as today. FSR takes no new lock. It lengthens that hold with:
- **Every present:** 1–2 extra render passes recorded, 1–3 `vkCreateImageView` + `vkCreateFramebuffer` (today: 0 on shared and direct, 1 on drawn), and 1–2 `vkUpdateDescriptorSets`.
- **On the first present per (format, filter):** `vkCreateRenderPass` + `vkCreateGraphicsPipelines` under `context.LockPipelineCacheForUse()` (PresentationUpscaler.cpp:320). The title's pipeline compiles use that same lock.
- **When the size or format changes:** `vkCreateImage`, `vkAllocateMemory` and `vkBindImageMemory` for 1–2 surfaces per slot, and frees of the old ones.
- Release of the transient objects happens in `RetireSlot`, under `presentMutex`. On the game path that is `RetirePresents` at Presentation.cpp:101, which runs before the GPU mutex is taken.

**Waits.** None new.
- The fence waits are today's: `RetirePresents(inFlight or 0)` before acquire, outside the mutex, plus the unchanged fallbacks inside `present()`. Those fallbacks fire when the cursor slot or a shared slot is still in flight; FSR doesn't change `shared` or `PresentWaitsForSlots`.
- Stage-image reuse is per slot and covered by the slot fence `present()` already requires, so it adds no wait.
- `vkDeviceWaitIdle` happens only on a dynamic-range switch or a swapchain recreation, as today.

**GPU.** The presentation submission carries more work: the stage write at stage size, EASU at fitted size when upscaling, and RCAS at window size. It shares the VkQueue with the title's batches, so the next frame's batches execute after it, and the slot fence signals later.

**Can it block the queue worker's draws?** From the code, there are two channels.

1. **The GPU mutex.**
   - `commitDraw` takes it (Draw.cpp:615).
   - `DrawPipeline::Queue0().Enqueue` blocks while the pipeline is full (DrawPipeline.cpp:68-80), and that wait is what `Driver.Draw.draw_enqueued` measures (Draw.cpp:590).
   - `Queue0().Drain(...)` runs before `deviceUse.lock()`, so its wait lands in `Driver.Packet`'s `device_lock` mark (PacketExecution.cpp:192-197).

   **The PM's own trace bounds this channel:** `Driver.Present` grew only 6.6 → 9.1 ms per frame, and `present()`'s hold of the mutex is part of that. So the presenter holding the mutex longer explains at most 2.5 ms of the +76 ms in `draw_enqueued`.
2. **GPU queue order.** The presentation submission shares the VkQueue with the title's batches. Any recorder sync that waits on a batch fence while the committer holds the mutex (`vkWaitForFences`, Recorder.cpp:3798) also waits for work queued ahead of that batch.
