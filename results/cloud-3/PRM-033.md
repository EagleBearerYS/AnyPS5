**Natural steps: not quite, two points.**
1. **4ca3656ab is a fix-up.** It rewrites one sentence of the USAGE "Upscaling" paragraph that a3918b8d8 added two commits earlier, in the same PR. By the user's "no fix-up or typo commits" rule, it belongs in the commit that adds the paragraph.
