## The switch (`Graphics/src/ShaderResources.cpp`)

`APS5_DIAG_SKIP_IMAGE_PROOF` (presence) is read once (`SkipImageProof()`). It has a row in `docs/dev/ENVIRONMENT.md` marked diagnostic only.

**In `fastRevalidate`.** After the template's `NoRecord` check, P1 keeps only the write-watch collect over each texture's and each storage image's guest bytes, and fails `collect` when a collect fails. It skips:
- the depth-surface hold, `Released`, `FlushDepthPlanes`;
- all three key paths;
- the source/storage `Cached` (evicted) checks;
- the pending-image registry scan and its T1 path;
- `UnchangedSinceAll`, `StorageImagesCached`, `NoteProved` and the record updates.

**In `fullWalk`.** P1 returns right after setting `lastFullWalk`. It does no texture, storage-image or image-table lookup or compare. It also skips `captureValidation`: with no lookups the log is empty, and the call would wipe the records the build left, which the collect needs.

**Unchanged in both arms:**
- the pre-proof `CompletePendingCpuWrites` and flushes;
- the imported-buffer part (pending scan and flush over `directRegions` and written ranges, the import serial loop behind `HostImportsUnchanged`);
- the staging copies;
- #8047's audit (`TemplateAuditDue`), which still forces a full walk about 40 times per 10 s.

**The collect timer** (profile-only, both arms):
- `TimedCollectWrites` wraps the per-image `CollectWrites` calls of the fast proof.
- The `[rescache] revalidate (10 s)` line now reads `… N calls X ms (collect Y ms): fast …`.
- The timer costs a branch when profiling is off.

## The harness mode

**The harness waits for the GPU.** `Submit` calls `AgcDriverWaitIdle_nid_postfix()` after every frame (`VillageDrawCost.cpp:345-350`). It has no `VILLAGE_GPU_SYNC`, and needs none.

**The default `table` mode exercises no templates on main.** A probe (60 frames, then 2 frames with `APS5_TRACE_BDA=1`) showed why:
- Every draw is an address-based build: `[draws] … per kind: recipe hit 0 (0) template hit 0 (0) build 0 (0) BDA 252 (9343)`.
- lookup is 3.2 ms per 10 s and resources 1,854.6 ms, with no `Revalidate` calls.
- The cause, from the trace: `[bda] LoadAddressU32 at pc 0x00000038: scalar address access, offset dynamic (a register offset is not planned by the SRT walker)`. The pixel program's `s_load_dwordx8 s[16:23], s[2:3], s36` takes its offset from the object V#'s `s_buffer_load`. The SRT walker doesn't plan that load as an image table. Main doesn't cache address-based builds (#6636 is what would reuse them), so every draw rebuilds.

**An image-table mode didn't take.** I tried a `lane` mode, routing the same index through `v_mov_b32 v12, s32+slot; v_readfirstlane_b32 s36, v12` (the shape `ImageTableCacheBounds` uses with `v0`).
- The walker followed it back to the buffer load and still took the address-based path (`LoadAddressU32 at pc 0x00000040`, same reason).
- Main's image-table tests take their index from a compute thread ID, which a pixel program here can't use and still render the texels `Check` expects.
- The mode is reverted and not in the branch.

**So both arms run `VILLAGE_INDEX=constant`, unchanged from the harness.** Templates are reused there:
- The 4 pixel programs sample 1–4 T#s that they read from the 2,000-entry table at constant offsets.
- After warm-up every draw is a template hit, e.g. 53,735 of 53,735 per 10 s.
- `Revalidate` runs ~55,000 times per 10 s, all fast apart from ~40 audit full walks.
- The other parameters are the harness defaults: `VILLAGE_DRAWS=850`, `VILLAGE_TABLE=2000`, `VILLAGE_FULL_PERCENT=10`, `VILLAGE_SETS=8`, `VILLAGE_PROGRAM_RUN=8`, 1920×1080.
- The image proof here is per texture (1–4 per draw). There is no image table, so this is not Village's 2,000-entry image-table proof.

### The share P1 saves

**(P0 − P1) / P0 of lookup = (13.83 − 12.81) / 13.83 = 7.4%.**
- The runs overlap (P0 13.43–14.41, P1 12.28–13.84), so part of that is noise.
- What the switch itself removes is the `Revalidate` drop, consistent in every run: 99.45 → 72.90 ms per 10 s, or 0.49 µs per draw. That is **~3.5% of lookup**.
- `Revalidate` is only ~13% of lookup here, 99 of 748 ms per 10 s. The other ~87% is outside the proof: the content key, the cache find, the buffer-alias and moved-buffer checks.

**Collect's share in P1:**
- 2.8% of P1's lookup (20.55 of 731.1 ms per 10 s);
- 28% of P1's remaining `Revalidate` (20.55 of 72.9 ms).
- The rest of P1's `Revalidate` (~52 ms per 10 s, ~0.9 µs per call) is the pre-proof flushes, the buffer part, the staging check and the bookkeeping.

**The kill line (P1 saves < 50% of lookup) is met in this harness, by a wide margin.** But this harness's template proof (~1.8 µs per call, 1–4 textures, no image table) is three orders of magnitude below the ~1,150 µs per call lane P's memo gives for Village's menu. Village's lookup is mostly `Revalidate`, so this measurement doesn't decide lane P for Village.
