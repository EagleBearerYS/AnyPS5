## One change from the task's suggestion: the trigger is 1.5× the bound, not 2×

- The task suggested `shrink_to_fit()` when the capacity is over twice the bound, and a test bound of twice the bound. On libstdc++ that cannot work: the vector doubles, so any build of 1,025–2,048 records ends at exactly **2,048 = 2 × bound**.
- With the 2× rule, the shrink never runs for those builds. That range includes the 1,500-texture test and the reviewer's own 2,000-texture example. Measured with that rule: "the thread's log capacity is 2048 records after the build". The test's 2× check therefore passed even with no shrink at all, so there could be no red.
- `> bound` would be wrong the other way. MSVC grows by half (…, 711, 1,066), so a full ordinary log already has capacity 1,066 there and would be shrunk and regrown after ordinary builds.
- So the trigger is `capacity > bound + bound/2` (1,536):
  - a log within the bound never reaches it: at most 1,024 under libstdc++ and libc++, 1,066 under MSVC;
  - every build past the bound reaches it: 2,048 under libstdc++, at least 1,599 under MSVC.
- The test checks `capacity <= bound` instead of `<= 2 × bound`. After a shrink the capacity is 0, because `captureValidation` has already cleared the log. It is at most 1,024 when an interrupted build left records.

## Why trimming after the build is safe

These are all the log's consumers at bde2a8f0af:
- **`captureValidation`** (reverse search, then `clear()`) runs inside the guard. In a build it is called from `noteReusable`, inside `buildComplete`'s `BuildLookups`. In a full walk it runs at the end of `fullWalk`, inside that lambda's guard. So the log is already empty when the outermost guard ends on these paths.
- **`fullWalk`** clears the log before its own guard.
- **`resolveImageTables`** reads only `log.back()`, right after its own `cachedTexture` lookup added it.
- **`logLookup`** keeps at most 1,024 records outside a build.

Nothing reads the log after a build ends. The trim only matters when a build or full walk ends without `captureValidation`: a throw, or a full walk that returns false early. It keeps what `logLookup` would have kept.
