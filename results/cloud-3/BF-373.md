### How `Poll` handles the closed descriptor, read at a26b7827e1

1. `GuestSockets::Poll` calls `IsPipe` once per entry, in its first scan (`Udp.cpp:2683`).
2. With the fix, a false result goes to `DescriptorIsOpen_nid_no_patch` (`Udp.cpp:2686`).
3. On POSIX that is `::fcntl(descriptor, F_GETFD) >= 0` (`File/src/Stdio.cpp:774-776`).
4. After the host `close`, that check is false, so the entry gets `revents = GuestPollInvalid` and is counted ready (`:2687-2689`).
5. `poll` returns 1 with `Invalid` set. `PollsAPipeClosedDuringTheWait` (`tests/GuestPoll.cpp:369`) accepts exactly that.
6. If the close lands after the scan, the descriptor is already in the host `::poll` set and the wait times out with 0, which the test also accepts.

### The kevent siblings: the close race doesn't reach them, so I left them unchanged

I checked the `IsPipe` → second `fcntl` → `ioctl(FIONREAD)` paths in `PipeReadState` (`Udp.cpp:1260`) and `PipeWriteState` (`:1280` Linux, `:1290` macOS, `:1319` other).

**Your reading of the failure mode is right.** If the descriptor closes after `IsPipe` returns true:
- the second `fcntl` gives -1, and `-1 & O_ACCMODE` is 3, which matches neither `O_WRONLY` nor `O_RDONLY`;
- `::poll` on a closed descriptor doesn't fail, it reports `POLLNVAL`;
- then `ioctl(FIONREAD)` fails with `EBADF` and throws `kevent: querying pipe descriptor … failed`.

**But a close while the guest waits in `kevent` can't land there:**
- **Every POSIX caller of these paths runs under the queue mutex.** The callers are `GuestSockets::Ready` (`Udp.cpp:1196-1205`) through:
  - the level-triggered `pollFunc` in `EqueueDescriptorEvent` (`Equeue/Equeue.cpp:~933`);
  - `PollEdge` (`Equeue/src/Kqueue.cpp:208`, installed as `pollFunc` at `:246`).

  Both run only inside `KernelEqueuePrivate::PollEvents` (`Equeue.cpp:150-161`), and every call site holds `m_mutex` (locks at `:392`, `:410`, `:424`, `:435`, `:450`; `WaitForDescriptors` at `:255` takes the held lock).
- **The guest close takes the knotes out under that mutex before the host close.** `close_nid_postfix` (`File/src/Stdio.cpp:920-940`) does:
  1. `EqueueDescriptorClosed(d)` at `:929`;
  2. that calls `KqueueDescriptorClosed` (`Kqueue.cpp:662-682`), which calls `RemoveDescriptorEvents` on every kqueue under `m_mutex` (`Equeue.cpp:630`);
  3. only then `::close(d)` at `:938`.

  So a scan already in progress finishes first, and no later scan sees the descriptor.
- **The two other callers of `Ready` are Windows-only.** `EdgeWatch.cpp:100` sits in the `_WIN32` branch (`:86-119`), and `DescriptorControl.cpp:231` (`Readable`) is in `_WIN32` code.
- **A probe confirmed it** (scratch, not committed; diff in `/home/user/bf373-logs/scratch-kevent-probe.diff`).
  - Setup: 200 attempts per run. A thread waits 20 ms in `kevent` on a pipe registered for `EVFILT_READ`, and the main thread closes the read end 1-3 ms in.
  - On main: `kevent` returned 0 in 600 of 600 attempts.
  - With a 2 ms sleep added between `IsPipe` and the second `fcntl`/`ioctl` in `PipeReadState`: still 600 of 600 returned 0, with no throw.
- **One path not covered:** a registration racing a close on another thread. `kevent(EV_ADD)` calls `PipeReadState`/`PipeWriteState` at registration (`Kqueue.cpp:343`, `Equeue.cpp:891`) without that serialization. No test covers it, and it's a different race (registering a descriptor while another thread closes it), so I left it. With this fix, its first window (inside `IsPipe`) now returns false instead of throwing.

### Test decision

- **No new test.** A deterministic test would need the close to land between `fstat` and `fcntl`, which needs a hook inside `IsPipe`. That would be a test-only hook in production code, which the task rules out.
- **The existing `PollsAPipeClosedDuringTheWait` is the regression test,** and the scratch shows it covers this path: red 10 of 10 on main, green 10 of 10 with the fix.

## Summary for the Bug Finder

- **The race is real, and the fix is one line.** A 2 ms sleep between `IsPipe`'s `fstat` and `fcntl` throws `poll: querying pipe descriptor … failed` in 10 of 10 runs on main.
- **The fix:** `IsPipe` returns false when `fcntl(F_GETFL)` fails with `EBADF`. `Poll` then reports the closed descriptor as `GuestPollInvalid` through `DescriptorIsOpen`, which `PollsAPipeClosedDuringTheWait` accepts. Other errors still throw. Branch `fix/libkernel-poll-closed-pipe` at 10dd157871. With the fix, the same scratch passes 10 of 10.
- **Without the sleep it never showed here:** 2000 of 2000 on main, idle and beside `ctest -j4 -R '^guest_'`. After the fix: 2000 of 2000.
- **The kevent siblings aren't reached by a close during a wait.** Close removes the knotes under the queue mutex before the host `close`, and the readiness scan runs under that mutex. The probe returned 0 in 600 of 600 attempts even with a sleep in the window, so I left them unchanged.
- **CI mirror:** 4548 passed, 141 skipped, 25 failed. The failures are 24 that fail identically at main in this container (errno 47, missing CA file, `guest_apr_read_priorities`), plus one load timeout of `agc_driver_image_table` that passes alone. Registration and conventions pass, and `check_skips` flags only the 4 IPv6 skips.
