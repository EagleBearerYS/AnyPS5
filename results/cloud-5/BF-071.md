- the tracker's `blocks`, `cpuBlocks` and `hintBlocks` move from main's three flat Windows `std::vector`s (`assign(size / 64 KiB + 1, 0)` each) to the shared `Leaf` layout, with Windows-only hint leaves (`hintOf`/`hint`);

- **What it adds:**
  - `TouchedTable.hpp`;
  - `PageSpan::pages` as a `TouchedTable<std::uint8_t>`, reserved on Windows and on the heap on Linux;
  - the leaked `Pages()` singleton;
  - `TableReservations()`, which holds only the page-state tables, as in 8a14ccb2c;
  - the `agc_touched_table` test;
  - `CheckTableCommitCharge`, run after the stamp checks so that their leaves don't pre-exist.
