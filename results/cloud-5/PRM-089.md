## 2. What was cut, and what stays

**The case now:**
1. `CloseWindow(true)`, then `NoteLookups(DrawCacheBypass::Window, false)`: one low window noted on the driver's bypass, as `StageHitsKeepTheCacheLookedUp` already does.
2. A scene of **128** new draws, drawn for frames 1 and 2.
3. `CloseWindow(false)` after frame 2. The window then holds the scene's 128 first and 128 second sightings, plus noted non-useful lookups.
4. Frames 3 and 4 drawn; **the assertion is unchanged:** `10 * hits >= 9 * SceneDraws` in frame 4.

**Why 128:**
- With the warm-up, the 128 second sightings (`drawKeySeen`) are useful. That is more than the 103 of 5% of 2048, so the run of low windows ends and frames 3 and 4 hit.
- Without the warm-up, that window is the second low one in a row, so the 2048-draw bypass starts and frames 3 and 4 are bypassed.
- **Kept:** the scene's shape (new keys repeated each frame, enough of them that without the warm-up the window bypasses), the two-low-windows rule (the scene's window is the second), 4 frames, the `_off` arm (`APS5_DRAW_CACHE_BYPASS_PERCENT=0`, same registration) and the assertion.
- **Changed text:** only the assertion's and the success line's wording, which now name `SceneDraws` and the noted low window.

**Draws per arm: 4000 → 512.**
