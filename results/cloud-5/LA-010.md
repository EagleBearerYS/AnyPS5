## 2. The compute cases, his heap margin, and his `full_heap` tests

### His heap at cae12b7d5

`BindlessHeap::imageMargin()` is `max(1, min(1024, cap/4))` (1,024 at 4,096 slots, 275 at a cap of 1,100, 257 at 1,030, 1 at 2).
- After every slot `TextureSlot` takes, `noteTakenLocked` sweeps once the kind has fewer unused slots than the margin, and if it still has fewer, sets `NearlyFull`. Then new draws build per-draw stages and draw lookups skip GPU-mode variants.
- The present-time `SweepIfDue` clears it once every kind has twice the margin unused (cae12b7d5).
- `TextureSlot` itself still hands out slots until a kind is really full. His `Require(slot.has_value(), "the global image heap is full")` in `ShaderResources.cpp` stays for draws already queued with GPU-mode stages, which "use the margin".
