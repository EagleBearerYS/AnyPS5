## 2. Rule 5 (a6b6274a4d): the gap exists on main + #9533

**The gap.** With main's model a dispatch clear is pending between `NoteHtileDepthClear` and `ArmHtileDepthClear`.
- `NoteDepthMetadataFill`'s HTILE branch checked only `clearGeneration != 0`, so a whole-HTILE fill noted in between took the clear as a fill.
- Arm then kept the fill's pattern.

**The fix.** The fill now refuses first, with `native multisample depth metadata fill over a pending HTILE clear dispatch is not implemented`. Pending means `clearPending`, or a dispatch clear's generation with no fill pattern not yet consumed.

**The test.** `ClearFault::FillDuringClear` in `--clear-refusals` notes a fill between the clear's Note and its Arm.

**Debt.** The native depth entry names the refusal.

**Red** (a6b6274a4d with its DepthSurface.cpp hunk reverted, test kept): `agc_driver_native_depth_clear_refusals` → `AGC graphics: multisample target refusal did not match metadata fill over a pending HTILE clear dispatch: ` (accepted, nothing refused). Green on a6b6274a4d.
