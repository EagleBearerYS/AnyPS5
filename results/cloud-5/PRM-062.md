## What changed in each commit, besides cherry-picking

**In every commit:**
- The `(void)` declaration in `include/Api.hpp` of each export the commit implements becomes that export's real signature.
- The commit's stubs are dropped from `Export.cpp`.
- **The test** loses its `extern "C"` block, including `malloc_nid_postfix`/`free_nid_postfix`, which libc's `Api.hpp` already declares. It now passes byte-buffer contexts through #10488's `Context()` helper.
- **The debt file** keeps both sides.
- **Totals:** commit 1 declares 3 exports, commit 2 declares 9, commit 3 declares 6, commit 4 declares 3, commit 5 declares 1 and commit 6 declares none. That makes all 22 exports.

**Commit 1 (4fc1daf164):**
- **`struct LibreSslEvpCipher`** (88 bytes) moves from our `include/Evp.hpp` to `include/Api.hpp`, before `LibreSslEvpCipherCtx`, with a forward declaration of the context ahead of it.
- **One change to #10488's struct, beyond the declaration move:**
  - `LibreSslEvpCipherCtx::cipher` becomes `const LibreSslEvpCipher*`; #10488 has `const void*`.
  - This is the type our removed copy of the struct had, and our code needs it (`ctx->cipher->cleanup`, `->control`, `->contextSize`). The layout and all static_asserts are unchanged.
  - The alternative was a cast at every `ctx->cipher` use in our sources, which would be a larger change.
- **`include/Evp.hpp`** loses both struct copies and its `VabiMacros.hpp` include. It now includes `Api.hpp` and keeps the static_asserts.
- **`src/EvpCipherCtx.cpp`:**
  - its static_asserts move to `Evp.hpp`, as in our original commit;
  - its local `calloc_nid_postfix`/`free_nid_postfix` declarations are dropped (#10488 already includes libc's `Api.hpp`).
- **The test:** `static_cast<unsigned char*>(EVP_CIPHER_CTX_new_nid_postfix())` becomes `reinterpret_cast`, as #10488 did in its own test.

**Commit 2 (84f5d1334f):**
- **`Evp.hpp`** gains the commit's internal `Cipher*` constants and nothing else.
- **`src/EvpCipherCtx.cpp`** drops its local `calloc`/`malloc`/`free` declarations.
- **The test:**
  - **a new helper** beside `Context()`, `static const LibreSslEvpCipher* EvpCipher(const void* cipher)` (a `static_cast`). It wraps the cipher argument of every `EVP_*Init_ex` call: the test's own `Cipher` structs and its `const void*` descriptors.
  - **the test's own aliases** `Update` and `Final` take `LibreSslEvpCipherCtx*` instead of `void*`;
  - **calls through them**, and through the locals `encryptFinal`/`decryptFinal`, pass `Context(context)`.

**Commit 3 (88f90dde01):**
- **`include/Aes.hpp` is gone.** Its `MaxRounds`, `AesKey` and `AES_*` are already in main's `Api.hpp`.
- **`src/Aes.cpp` is unchanged from 6b1e364fc2.** The commit's hunk there only switched to `Aes.hpp` and removed the struct main had already removed, so it is reverted. Its include is dropped from `src/EvpAes.cpp`.
- **CMake** is the union: #10488's list, with `Error.cpp`, plus our `src/EvpAes.cpp`.
- **The test:**
  - the `Descriptor` alias returns `const LibreSslEvpCipher*`;
  - `static_cast<const Cipher*>(…)` of a returned cipher becomes `reinterpret_cast`;
  - the two checks `Require(cipher == test.descriptor())` compare `EvpCipher(cipher)`, with the same pointer equality and the same line.

**Commit 4 (6c6f37f1c6):**
- **`src/EvpAes.cpp`:** its local `malloc_nid_postfix`/`free_nid_postfix` block is replaced by libc's `Api.hpp`. The `HostEntropy.hpp` include it adds is kept.
- **`Evp.hpp`:** gains `CipherModeGcm` but not the `extern "C"` `EVP_CIPHER_CTX_ctrl` declaration, which `Api.hpp` now has.

**Commits 5 and 6 (c251455d11, d8ee395e44):** only the declaration (commit 5) and the test's `Context()`/`EvpCipher()` wrapping.

**Product code:** compared with the old head 8f269fb332, `src/EvpCipherCtx.cpp`, `src/EvpAes.cpp` and `src/Aes.cpp` differ only in includes and the removed declarations. Every check and message in the test is unchanged.

Commit 4's `InitForm` passes a `const void*`, so I rebuilt 2 and 3 with the `const void*` helper (`git commit-tree`, with the same messages and authors) and re-ran their checks on those exact commits.
