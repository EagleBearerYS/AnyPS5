## 3. Rule 5: a fill over a pending HTILE dispatch clear (5e8a1cdb83)

**The gap.** With #7371, a dispatch clear is pending from Note (packet decode) until Arm (recording).
- A whole-HTILE `NoteDepthMetadataFill` landing in between found `clearGeneration == 0` and took the clear as a fill: pattern, note, generation.
- Arm then kept the fill's pattern, so the dispatch clear was proven as a fill.

**The fix.** The HTILE branch of `NoteDepthMetadataFill` now refuses first with `native multisample depth metadata fill over a pending HTILE clear dispatch is not implemented`. Pending means `clearPending`, or a dispatch clear's generation with no fill pattern not yet consumed by a sample.

**The test.** A new `ClearFault::FillDuringClear` in `--clear-refusals` notes a fill between the clear's Note and its Arm.
- Red with 5e8a1cdb83's driver hunk reverted: `multisample target refusal did not match metadata fill over a pending HTILE clear dispatch: ` (accepted).
- Green on the head.

The native depth debt entry names the new refusal.
