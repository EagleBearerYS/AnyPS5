- **Read this first: BF-068 measured against BF-063's head.**
  - The task describes the tables as at 32ac24cb3 (flat `std::vector`s, `assign(n, 0)`). BF-063's 3a361ed79 had already changed them on this branch to `TouchedTable<std::uint32_t>`: reserved, with a 64 KiB table page committed on its first write.
  - So BF-068 replaces BF-063's layout for these three tables, not the flat one.
  - **What each layout holds:**
    - BF-063 commits 64 KiB per table per touched GiB (64 KiB covers 16,384 blocks).
    - The Linux leaves hold 512 KiB (`blocks` + `cpuBlocks` together) per touched 4 GiB, plus a 256 KiB hint leaf if hinted.
  - **Dense stamping, both tables used:** the two are equal, 128–192 KiB per GiB.
  - **Only `blocks` stamped:** leaves cost 2×. `MarkWritten` and fresh collects stamp only `blocks`.
  - **Scattered stamps** (one per 4 GiB): leaves cost up to **8× more**. In this test's Windows pattern (16 regions about 62 GiB apart, `MarkWritten` only), leaves hold 8 MiB where BF-063 commits about 1 MiB.
  - **Both are far below main's 187.5 MiB,** which is committed and dirty at start.
  - What BF-068 adds is **one shared layout without the `#ifdef` split, and no reserved address space** (BF-063 reserves 3 × 62.5 MiB).
  - **It is a separate commit,** so it can be dropped without touching BF-063's.
- **Readers and writers** (all under the tracker mutex, like Linux's leaves, so no atomic pointer is needed):
  - `stampOf` serves `newestStamp`, `unchanged`'s single-block path, `StoredOver`, `WrittenSince`, `ChangedBlocks` and `UnchangedSinceCollected`.
  - `unchanged` scans a block range for `UnchangedSince` and `UnchangedSinceAll`.
  - `cpuStampOf` serves `StoredOver`, `UnchangedSinceCollected` and `ChangedBlocks`.
  - `stamp` serves the collects (`stampWrittenRun`), `MarkWritten`, `storeOwn` and `watchPrivateMapping`.
  - Hints (Windows only): `startHints`, `walkHints`, `hinted`, `newestHint`.

  - **The `#ifdef` split is gone** from `stampOf`, `unchanged`, `cpuStampOf` and `stamp`.

  - **Removed:** the tracker's three `TouchedTable`s, and their entries in `TableReservations()`.

## 1. Readers and writers

All of these are `WriteTracker` members in `Execution/src/GuestMemory.cpp`. Every caller holds the tracker mutex (`lockTracker`); I checked each public entry point.

| member | role | callers |
|---|---|---|
| `stampOf(block)` | read `blocks` | `newestStamp` (`NewestStamp`, `UnchangedCommittedSince`), `unchanged`'s single block, `StoredOver`, `WrittenSince`, `ChangedBlocks`, `UnchangedSinceCollected` |
| `unchanged(first, last, since)` | scan `blocks` | `UnchangedSince`, `UnchangedSinceAll` |
| `cpuStampOf(block)` | read `cpuBlocks` | `StoredOver`, `UnchangedSinceCollected`, `ChangedBlocks` |
| `stamp(block, generation, kind)` | write `blocks` and/or `cpuBlocks` | `stampWrittenRun` (collects), `MarkWritten`, `storeOwn`, `watchPrivateMapping` (Windows) |
| `startHints`, `walkHints` (Windows) | write hints | `UntrackedHint` paths |
| `hinted`, `newestHint` (Windows) | read and scan hints | `UntrackedHint` |

- **No bulk clear:** stamps only grow.
- **No snapshot across blocks:** `ChangedBlocks` reads block by block.

## 2. The change

- **Shared on both platforms:**
  - `LeafBlocks = 2^16`;
  - `struct Leaf { blocks, cpuBlocks }`;
  - `std::vector<std::unique_ptr<Leaf>> leaves`.
- **One code path on both platforms** for `stampOf`, `cpuStampOf`, `unchanged` and `stamp`:
  - a missing leaf reads as zero;
  - the first `stamp` allocates the leaf;
  - `unchanged` skips missing leaves.
- **Windows:**
  - `initialize` sizes `leaves` and `hintLeaves` to the arena;
  - hints use `hintOf`/`hint` over `std::unique_ptr<std::array<std::uint32_t, 2^16>>` leaves.
- **Linux:** `leaves.resize(LeafCount)` as before. `blockOf`, `covers` and `newestStamp`'s clip keep their platform split, because the addressing differs: Windows is relative to the arena, Linux is absolute.
- **`StampTableBytes()`** sums `sizeof(Leaf)` per allocated leaf, plus each hint leaf on Windows.
- **Comments:** the two comments on the Windows-only `blocks`/`cpuBlocks` members went with those members, since a moved comment would be a new one. The shared `Leaf` never had comments.

## 4. Tests

`agc_write_tracking` (no GPU):

**`CheckStampLeaves`**
- **Windows:** 16 regions of 2 blocks at `base + k × size/16`.
- **Linux:** 16 `AllocateWatched(2 × 64 KiB, crossLeaf)` blocks, each straddling a 4 GiB boundary.
- `MarkWritten` over each region.
- **Requires:**
  - the tables grew;
  - by at most 2 × 16 leaves of 768 KiB (a leaf of each of the three tables);
  - on Windows, at most a quarter of the flat size (3 × (size / 64 KiB + 1) × 4 bytes).
- **Linux:** "stamping 16 scattered regions grew the stamp tables by 8704 KiB to 8704 KiB".

**`CheckStampEquivalence`**
- **Areas:**
  - Linux: two blocks around a 4 GiB leaf boundary, checked to end there, plus a 4-block area;
  - Windows: two blocks around the first free arena leaf boundary (`base + k × 4 GiB`), the arena's last block, and a 4-block area.
- **400 steps,** each:
  1. `MarkWritten` a random range;
  2. update a flat `std::map` from block to stamp;
  3. read a random range against a random earlier generation;
  4. require `WrittenSince` to equal "any flat stamp newer", and `NewestStamp` to equal the flat maximum (when it is not 0).
- **Linux:** "400 stamps and reads over 2 areas, around a leaf boundary, match flat stamps".
