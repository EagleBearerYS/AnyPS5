## Gate: dropped, not narrowed

`if (index == 0u && target.htileDepthOnly) {` became `if (index == 0u) {` in `prepareDepthSampling` (`DepthSurface.cpp:186`).

**Why dropping it is safe:** the remaining checks already narrow what reaches the alias gate to the layouts the proof covers.
- **Admission (L167):** an HTILE that is not depth-only is refused at L167 unless it is pipe-aligned and without VRS. So dropping the condition admits exactly "depth-only, or depth+stencil pipe-aligned without VRS". The narrowed form would repeat L167.
- **Depth proof (L217, `htileProvesClear`):** it decodes both layouts. In the depth+stencil layout, every word masked with `0xfffff00f` (Z range and ZMask) must be `0` for clear 0.0 or `0xfffc0000` for 1.0. These are the words of Mesa's `radv_get_htile_fast_clear_value`, and ZMask 15 is rejected. Every depth sample then reads `DB_DEPTH_CLEAR`, whatever the plane or the colour target's host-only samples hold.
- **The alias record (L453, `sameDepthAlias`):** layout-agnostic. It compares the active owner, content generation, newest content and value.
- **Stencil:** still proven only by HTILE.
  - At import (L218) and at a completed clear (L494), every word must hold SMem 0.
  - With the stencil in HTILE, `stencilPlane` is false, so any colour target over the stencil plane (index 2) still refuses with `stencilAliasRefusal` (L200).
- **Not changed, after checking every other use of `htileDepthOnly`/`htileStencil` on the path:**
  - L159, L164-165: the stencil and plane admission;
  - L444, L2645: the separate-plane fill handling, which needs a depth-only HTILE and is untouched;
  - L493: the completion proof, already layout-aware;
  - L1637: the target identity;
  - L1867-1887, L2678-2690: the clear and fill aspects.
- **Also accepted (same proof): `htileDepthOnly=0, htileStencil=0`.** That is D32 without stencil, with the HTILE in the depth+stencil layout. The fixture can build it, so it is tested.

## Commit message (a2911ffb72)

```
fix(agc): accept a colour alias over a native multisample depth plane whose HTILE carries stencil

Dave the Diver (PPSA19115) stops at the first sample of its two-sample
D32S8 target on the #9717 + #9742 test branch: an R32F colour target of
the same sample count, newest a clear to zero, covers the depth plane,
and the target's HTILE carries its stencil (not depth-only, DB_DEPTH_CLEAR
0.0). #9742 accepts such an alias only with a depth-only HTILE, so
prepareDepthSampling refuses with the depth alias refusal.

The proof does not depend on the HTILE layout. prepareDepthSampling
already admits an HTILE that is not depth-only only when it is
pipe-aligned and without VRS, and htileProvesClear decodes both layouts:
in the depth and stencil layout every word, masked to its Z range and
ZMask (0xfffff00f), must equal 0 for a clear of 0.0 or 0xfffc0000 for
1.0, the words Mesa's radv_get_htile_fast_clear_value writes. Every
depth sample then reads DB_DEPTH_CLEAR, and none of the plane's bytes or
the colour target's host-only samples. The depth-only condition is
dropped from the alias gate, and the alias is recorded and checked again
at each sample as with a depth-only HTILE. A D32 target whose HTILE is in
the depth and stencil layout without stencil passes the same proof and
is accepted too.

The stencil is still proven only by HTILE: every word must hold SMem 0
at import and in a completed HTILE clear, and any colour target over the
stencil plane of a target whose stencil is in HTILE still refuses with
the stencil plane refusal. Unchanged: a colour target over the HTILE,
one of another sample count, two or more over the depth plane, an alias
over tiles the HTILE does not prove, and a draw into the alias after
eligibility is recorded.

agc_driver_native_depth_aliased_depth runs every alias case with the
stencil in HTILE (words 0xf0 and 0 for 0.0, 0xfffc00f0 for 1.0) and with
a D32 target in the depth and stencil layout, and adds a word with SMem 1
and a colour target over the stencil plane, which refuse.
```
