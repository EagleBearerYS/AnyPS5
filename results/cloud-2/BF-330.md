### Commit 1 (item 1)

**Its test, `recipe-snapshot`:**
- The draw is asked for a recipe while a snapshot (a 16-byte copy) of a word inside the space is in its snapshot list.
- The word is then changed in memory.
- If a recipe was made, it is replayed with `DrawFromRecipe`, which must not record.

- Then no recipe may exist.
