## Step 4: `PointerRecordPairs` against the old two walks

Read from `git diff e9d4626904 92cede35a0 -- core/shader`.

**The old code** walked each word on its own (`PointerRecordReads(…, pointerKeyed = true, …)`):
- Phis expanded.
- U32 immediates, lane masks (`LaneMaskOperand`, which also covers AND/OR/NOT/Phi of lane masks) and the lane-mask side of an `AND` skipped.
- Everything else had to be a record read at one offset, in one column (`SameRecordColumn` against the previous read).
- The caller then required `highReads.back()` at `lowReads.back()`'s offset + 4, in the same column.

**The new code** walks (low, high) pairs:
- A free low word (`PointerFreeWord`: a U32 immediate or `LaneMaskOperand`) drops its pair.
- A low Phi pairs its arms with the high Phi's arm from the same predecessor (`PhiIncoming`) when both Phis are in one block. Otherwise each low arm meets the whole high word.
- A record low word goes into `lowReads`. Every high word it meets must then be a Phi (expanded), the lane-mask side of an `AND`, free, or a record read chained into `highReads`.
- Both lists must be non-empty. The caller's offset + 4 and same-column check is unchanged.

**Is anything now accepted with a record low word whose high word isn't the same column at offset + 4, an immediate or a lane mask? None found.**
- Every high word that pairs with a record low word is checked, through the same rules as before: Phi, `AND` with a lane mask, free, or a chained record read.
- The final offset + 4 and `SameRecordColumn` check covers the whole `highReads` chain.
- Unpaired Phis, a high Phi in another block, and a back-edge high value all fall back to checking every arm against the record low word. That is conservative.
- One newly accepted edge is outside this category: a low word that is a *U32 immediate*, not just a lane mask, now also frees its edge's high word. So (0, second-buffer read) is skipped too. That is by design ("an immediate or a lane mask"), and the edge stays under the runtime pointer-value check, which faults outside the snapshot. The new test checks that fault only for the lane-mask edge.

**Planned before and refused now: one shape found.**
- **The shape:** the record's two words never arrive on the same edge:
  - edge A carries a record low word with a free high word;
  - edge B carries a free low word with the record high word.
- **IR**, in one block with predecessors A and B:
  - `low = Phi(A: ReadConstBuffer(record, o), B: lane mask (e.g. exec_lo) or immediate)`
  - `high = Phi(A: Immediate (e.g. 0x1234), B: ReadConstBuffer(record, o+4))`
  - `GetAddressResource(low, high)` feeds `GetImageResource`.
- **e9d4626904:** the low walk gives `{record@o}` and the separate high walk gives `{record@o+4}`. Offset + 4 and the same column hold, so it **planned**, with the runtime pointer check behind it.
- **92cede35a0:** edge A records the low read, but its high word is free, so no high read is recorded. Edge B is dropped because its low word is free. `highReads` stays empty, so `PointerRecordPairs` returns false and the plan is **refused** with "GetImageResource dword 0 is not a valid runtime value".
- **Failing case as text**, not added to the code: take `PointerHighSecondLaneCode`.
  1. Make the default (record) path set the high word to the constant: `s_mov_b32 s45, 0x1234` instead of the record read at o+4. The low word stays `record@o`.
  2. In the `s19 == 3` arm, keep `s_mov_b32 s44, exec_lo`, but load s45 from the record's own o+4 dword instead of the second buffer.
  3. Expect: planned on e9d4626904, refused on 92cede35a0 with the line above.
- **How much it matters:**
  - No edge in this shape carries a whole record pointer. The old plan joined halves from different edges and relied on the runtime check, so the refusal is arguably sound.
  - The cost of the refusal is the same stop as Rift Apart's.
  - If the old plan should be kept, the narrowest change is to fall back to the old independent high walk when `lowReads` is non-empty and `highReads` ends empty.
  - I made neither change.
- **Malformed Phis:** a paired high Phi with no incoming for one of the low Phi's predecessors (`PhiIncoming` returns null) is now refused where the old code accepted. That cannot happen in valid SSA, where both Phis of a block share its predecessors.
