## Short answer
- **The branch:** `test/e34-a2e-selected-reuse` = **c87a29d05d3087092ed23a4569f534859b7f27cd**, four commits on BF-006's `test/e34-a2e-selected` 320af7a8f.
- **The same vouching rule as PM-028.** A column is reused only when all of these hold:
  - no block was stamped since the pre-read stamp;
  - no recorded GPU work is over the runs;
  - deferred GPU results were flushed first;
  - the guest mapping generation is unchanged.
- **The same fallback:** otherwise the runs are read and compared, and a difference is walked afresh from the words just read.
- **No extra commit was needed.**
  - Three of the four commits ported with added and removed lines identical to their sources.
  - The first needed one 3-hunk conflict resolved and two references to main-only fields dropped.

- **Both of PM-028's red/greens reproduce here with PM-028's exact messages:** the flush fix, and walking from the compared words.
- `^agc` at the head: 2 of 546 fail. Both are the e34 tree's own sample experiments, as in BF-006; with their three `APS5_RE8_STRICT_*` switches set, both pass.
- **What differs from PM-028 on main:**
  - The memo key has no folded addend or carry, and the keep rule has no `Carried` state. The e34 tree plans no folded columns.
  - The e34 tree plans no pointer-indirect columns.
  - On this stacked tree, PM-027's selected records return before the memo, so they are never kept or compared.
  - The write tracker the vouching relies on is the same code as on PM-028's base, apart from a libc++ portability cast.

## What makes the port's behaviour differ from PM-028 on main
1. **No folded columns** (55eefe9c5 is not on this tree). The memo key has no folded addend or carry, and the keep rule has no `Carried` state. For every column this tree plans, both are what main's would be (zeros, and no Carried record).
2. **No pointer-indirect columns** (c794273ab is not on this tree). Main's indirect branch in `Column()` and the memo's exclusion of indirect columns have nothing to act on.
3. **Stacked on PM-027 (BF-006)**, which PM-028's base does not have:
   - A selected column (one record at a CPU-evaluated offset) returns from `Column()` before the memo. It is never kept, compared or vouched for, and reads its one record every capture, as BF-006 does.
   - Whole tables, such as per-lane or readfirstlane keys, are memoized exactly as PM-028 does. The reuse test uses such a key (`TableWholeCode`), and it passes here.
   - The `[image-table]` profile line carries both sets of counters.
4. **The vouching itself is unchanged:** same write tracker code, same stamp, refusal and flush rules.
