## Why it is not red: the HTILE store-back on bind

- `DepthSurface.cpp:1901`: `const auto htileCleared = RoutesToNativeDepthOwner(target) ? VkImageAspectFlags{0} : storeBackClearedHtile(context, target);`.
- `storeBackClearedHtile` (DepthSurface.cpp:1653-1697), when the HTILE range changed since the surface last read it:
  1. reads the words from guest memory (`GuestMemory::Read`, after the flush hook syncs pending GPU writes);
  2. computes `cleared &= HtileFillClears(word, htileStencil)` over every word (line 1686);
  3. expands them, and returns the cleared aspects.
- So any write that leaves uniform clear words in a single-sample surface's HTILE delivers the clear, noted or not. The queued fill writes those words, so the bind finds them.
- The note (`NoteDepthMetadataFill` → `NoteHtileClear`) is only a second route to the same result. No single-sample test can show it missing.
- Native multisample owners skip that store-back (`RoutesToNativeDepthOwner`) and rely only on the note. Hence JJenkx's refusal (`DepthSurface.cpp:423` at 514643ab16) and BF-142's finding that such a target must live on the driver's device.

## Colour sibling (step 3)

No existing test observes a colour fast clear through `NoteColorMetadataFill` after a DMA_DATA fill. `AlphaToCoverage.cpp:760/807` and `SampleMultisampleTarget.cpp:473/485/610/665/705/707` call `NoteColorMetadataFill` themselves, on their own Vulkan devices. Following the brief, I didn't build one.

**What it would take:** a hybrid like `DepthFastClearSubmit`:
- a multisampled colour target owned by a test `VulkanDevice`, as `SampleMultisampleTarget.cpp` builds;
- the driver's device created with a `DispatchFill`;
- a queued DMA_DATA fill over 64 KiB, with `APS5_PIPELINE_DMA` and pipelined draws, over its CMASK/FMASK or colour plane;
- then a sample.

`NoteColorMetadataFill` (`MultisampleTarget.cpp:1083-1100`) walks a global `targets()` list with no recorder check, so a missing note could be observable there. Whether a multisampled colour target, like the single-sample depth surface, also recovers the clear without the note would have to be measured first. It is a new test, roughly half a day.
