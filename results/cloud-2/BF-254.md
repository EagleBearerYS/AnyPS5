## What it does

- **`APS5_HEAP_WALK_MEMO_KEYS=<n>`:**
  - A positive decimal `n` replaces the 16,384-key cap in `HeapWalkMemo::slot()`.
  - If it is unset, empty, unparsable or 0, the cap stays 16,384.
  - The value is read once per process, and each memo copies it into a field when the memo is created.
- **With `APS5_PIPELINE_STATS` unset, the draw path gains only:**
  - `++inserts` after the emplace, and `++dropped` on the 4-way `pop_back` in `rememberWalk`;
  - `dropped += ways.size()` in the 2-key drain of `previous`;
  - one read of the memo's `stats` field at the top of the memo lookup, and one at the generation swap.

  The cap is now a field load instead of a constant; nothing else changes.
- **With `APS5_PIPELINE_STATS` set:**
  1. **Measuring:** at the top of the memo lookup, a thread whose own 10 s window has passed scans its own memo (`current` and `previous`) and builds its counts. No other thread touches the maps.
  2. **Publishing:** the thread stores the counts into a mutex-guarded registry keyed by the memo's address (one memo per thread through `HostThreadLocal`). Inserts and drops add up in the registry until the next print.
  3. **Printing:** when 10 s have passed since the last print, the publishing thread prints the line below and zeroes the stored inserts and drops.
  4. **Cleanup:** `~HeapWalkMemo` removes its registry entry, and the registry itself is never destroyed.
  5. **Swap drops:** at a generation swap, the entries of the `previous` being thrown away are added to `dropped`. This loop runs only when stats are on.
- **The line**, one per 10 s, on stderr like the other `[gpu-heap]` lines. The per-thread items are sorted by MiB, largest first, at most 16, then ` +K more`:

  ```
  [gpu-heap] walk memo (10 s): N threads; total X entries (Y keys), Z MiB, memo-only W entries / V MiB; inserts I, dropped D; per thread: <cur/prev, entries, MiB, memo-only MiB> ...
  ```

  `Y` is `current` keys plus `previous` keys. Each per-thread item is ` <cur/prev, entries, %.1f, %.1f>`.

## Byte accounting

- **"Vector capacity":** `capacity() × sizeof(T)`, and `(capacity + 7) / 8` for `std::vector<bool>`. This is the same rule `OwnedBytes` in `core/shader/recompiler/HeapTemplateBudget.hpp` uses.
- **Why not `OwnedBytes` itself:** the shader disk cache key guard (`ShaderCacheVersion.cmake`) stops the build when a driver source calls `ShaderRecompiler::OwnedBytes` or declares `using` for it. The name is not in `shaderCacheReviewedNames`, and adding it means editing `core/shader/recompiler/CMakeLists.txt`, which is hashed into the key. So HeapStages.cpp has a field-for-field copy of `OwnedBytes(const RecompileResult&)` and `OwnedBytes(const DescriptorBinding&)` (`resultBytes`, `bindingBytes`). Like the original, it does not charge the SPIR-V module.
- **Per memo:** `sizeof(HeapWalkMemo)`, plus the `seen` filter's capacity (65,536 × 8 B = 512 KiB per thread), plus both maps' bucket arrays (`bucket_count × sizeof(void*)`).
- **Per key:** an estimated map node (`sizeof(pair<const uint64_t, vector<HeapWalkEntry>>) + 2 × sizeof(void*)`), plus `ways.capacity() × sizeof(HeapWalkEntry)`. The ways vector's capacity covers `sizeof(HeapWalkEntry)` for every entry slot.
- **Per entry:** the capacities of `userData`, `ranges`, `values`, `accessible` and `userDataUse.reads`.
- **`result`:** `resultBytes(*entry.result)`, counted once per distinct pointer in the memo.
- **`capture`:** there is no existing accounting for `ResourceCapture`, so vector capacities are counted, once per distinct pointer:
  - `sizeof(ResourceCapture)`;
  - the capacities of `snapshot.buffers`, `images`, `samplers`, `flattenedSrt`, `userData`, `srtPoison`, `tables.tables` and `tables.ranges`;
  - the capacities of `readTrace.leaves`, `readTrace.otherReads` and `userDataUse.reads`;
  - for each table column, `size() × sizeof` of its shared `codes`, `words` and `readRanges` blocks (`SharedValues` exposes only the size).

  `plan` and `source` are not counted, because they are shared with the stage/source caches. A shared column block is charged once per capture that holds it, so two captures sharing one block count it twice.
- **`words`:** there is no existing accounting for `HeapStageWords`, so vector capacities are counted, once per distinct pointer: `sizeof(HeapStageWords)`, the capacities of `userData`, `flattenedSrt`, `vertexAttributes` and `bindings`, plus each binding vector's capacity.
- **Memo-only:** entries whose `result.use_count() == 1`. Each one is charged `sizeof(HeapWalkEntry)` + its own vectors + its result's bytes. Its capture and words are added only if their `use_count()` is also 1.
  - It is a lower bound: a result held only by two memo entries has `use_count() == 2` and is not counted.
  - A result being read on another thread at that moment can also briefly push the count to 2.
- **Not counted:** allocator overhead (malloc/NT heap headers and rounding), so the true heap use is higher than `Z`.
- **Scan cost under stats:** the 10 s scan visits every entry and fills an `unordered_set` of pointers, so it is O(entries) on the draw thread once per 10 s. At the full cap that is up to about 16,384 × 4 entries in `current` plus a `previous` generation.
