# PM-014: the overlay's numbers below 1 fps (cloud-2)

Result: below 1 fps the overlay now shows the last frame's rate, GPU time and driver time instead of 0. It is one commit, **5b49ab30b28d0ea66fea24924a69fab1e8dec8e4**, pushed as a normal fast-forward to `feat/videoout-performance-overlay` (226421e7e..5b49ab30b).
- The conventions check passes against upstream/main.
- The Linux CI mirror passes: 1176 tests, 1113 passed, 58 skipped and 5 failed. The 5 are the known no-IPv6 failures, the same counts as this branch's PM-003 baseline at 98b9b57ef.
- `feat/videoout-overlay-more-stats` is untouched. The notes for its rebase are at the end.

## The change

The base is the branch head 226421e7e ("docs: list the performance overlay with the diagnostics in USAGE"). The design is the one in results/cloud-2/PM-003.md.

- **Cause.** `OverlayStatistics` keeps the presents of the last second (`window`). Below 1 fps each new present pushes the previous one out, so `Fps()` and the per-frame GPU and driver counts had fewer than two samples and returned 0.
- **FPS.** When the last second holds fewer than two presents, FPS is now **1000 / the last frame time in ms**, from the frame ring that already feeds `FRAME`. A frame every 2 s reads 0.5.
  - With two or more presents in the last second it is the last-second average, as before.
  - The two modes meet at 1 fps. Frames 1.001 s apart read 0.999 (shown "1.0"), and frames exactly 1 s apart read 1.0 from the window.
  - A single present after a reset or toggle still reads 0.
- **GPU and DRIVER.** In the same case they are now the last frame's own growth of each total. `AddPresent` keeps the previous present's sample (`previous`, cleared by `Reset()`). `perFrameMs` uses `previous` to the latest sample over one frame, instead of returning 0.
  - A counter that went back still reads 0.
- **Format choice: two decimals below 1 fps.** The new `FormatOverlayRate` is used only for the FPS line:
  - **Two decimals for rates above 0 and below 0.995:** "0.50" at 2 s per frame, "0.08" at 12 s, "0.33" at 3 s.
  - The 0.995 threshold makes rates from 0.995 to 1 print "1.0" rather than "1.00", so the display is continuous across the mode switch.
  - Everything else goes through the existing `FormatOverlayValue`: one decimal below 100, whole numbers above, clamped to 0..9999, with NaN and negatives shown as "0.0".
  - A rate below 0.005 (frames longer than 200 s) shows "0.00". That is still honest, and `FRAME` then reads 9999.
  - I kept times at one decimal; only the rate changed.
- **Frame time and graph.** These were already right at very low rates and are unchanged. `FRAME` and `MIN/AVG/MAX` show the real times capped at 9999 by `FormatOverlayValue`. The graph scales against `max(slowest, 2 × average)` without a cap, so steady 12 s frames draw at half height (tested).
  - USAGE.md now states the cap: "anything above 9999 shows as 9999, so `FRAME` and `MIN/AVG/MAX` read 9999 for frames of 10 s or more".
- **Hidden overlay.** Nothing changes. Statistics are only fed by `NotePresent`, which `VulkanDevice::present` calls inside `if (overlay)` (VulkanDevice.cpp:3080-3083). The hidden path records no commands and does no extra work. `agc_performance_overlay` still reports "overlay hidden: presentation pass and blit output bit-identical before and after showing it".
- **USAGE.md**, in the same commit:
  - the FPS row describes the last-frame rate below 1 fps;
  - the GPU and DRIVER rows say "or of the last frame below 1 fps", and GPU also "trails … by a frame below 1 fps";
  - the closing paragraph gives the 9999 cap and FPS's two decimals below 1.

    const bool windowed = window.size() >= 2;
    if (!windowed && !previous) return 0;
    const auto first = windowed ? window.front().counters.*counter : (*previous).counters.*counter;

}
