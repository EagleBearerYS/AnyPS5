Title: `feat(libSceFont): render generated glyphs with sceFontGlyphRenderImage`. Head `feat/scefont-glyph-render-image-v2` (68820535b9); not opened.

```markdown
### What
Implements `sceFontGlyphRenderImage`, `sceFontGlyphRenderImageHorizontal` and `sceFontGlyphRenderImageVertical`. They draw a glyph from `sceFontGenerateCharGlyph` with the rasterizer and surface rules of `sceFontRenderCharGlyphImage`.

Two commits:
1. **`refactor(libSceFont): split RenderCodeWithStyle and ApplyStyleFrame out of the char render path`.** The second half of the char render path becomes `RenderCodeWithStyle`, which takes the style as an argument. The surface style-frame rule becomes `ApplyStyleFrame`, with `SurfaceStyleFrame` to find a surface's frame. The char calls pass the same style as before and behave as before; `guest_font` and `guest_system_font` pass unchanged at this commit.
2. **`feat(libSceFont): render generated glyphs with sceFontGlyphRenderImage`.** The three functions build on those helpers:
   - The glyph draws at the size it was generated with, as `sceFontGlyphRefersOutline` does.
   - The renderer argument stands in for the bound renderer and is checked as `sceFontBindRenderer` checks one.
   - The style frame argument is applied with `ApplyStyleFrame`, falling back to the surface's frame, and an invalid frame is refused as `sceFontRenderSurfaceSetStyleFrame` refuses it.
   - The Horizontal and Vertical forms place the image like `sceFontRenderCharGlyphImageHorizontal`/`Vertical`. The generic form adds the owner font's baseline or column offset, as `sceFontRenderCharGlyphImage` does.
   - Not confirmed on a console, and listed in the debt entry: the order of the error checks, and the argument frame winning over the surface's.

- `guest_system_font` renders a generated glyph with each form and compares pixels, metrics and output with the matching `sceFontRenderCharGlyphImage*` call.
- It also covers the glyph's own size, a frame argument, a surface frame, an unbound font, and null and invalid glyphs, surfaces, metrics, outputs, renderers and frames.
```
